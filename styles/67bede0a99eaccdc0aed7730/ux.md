# Overview

Yandex Market is a dense commerce app that connects discovery, search, product comparison, checkout, delivery, and account services in one persistent shopping shell.

# Navigation

- A five-item primary navigation keeps Home, Catalog, Cart, Orders, and Profile available.
- Search stays of discovery and listing screens.
- Product and checkout steps use focused pages and bottom sheets, then return to the shopping context.

# Core Flows

## Find and buy a product

1. Start from Home, Catalog, or search.
2. Narrow the result with suggestions, filters, and sorting.
3. Review the product, seller, delivery, and price options.
4. Add to cart and complete address, delivery, and payment steps.

## Manage shopping

1. Open Orders or Profile.
2. Review delivery status, rewards, payment products, and support shortcuts.
3. Return to recommendations without losing the main navigation.

# Interaction Patterns

- remains available actions preserve add-to-cart and checkout decisions on long pages.
