# Overview

gg uses small friendly transport and service illustrations to make otherwise utilitarian map actions immediately recognizable.

# Visual Style

Objects are simplified, rounded, lightly dimensional, and outlined with dark details. Color is restrained but varied by service, with vehicles, drivers, parcels, and tips treated as one coherent icon family.

# Composition

Place one isolated object inside a pale compact tile or circular field. Keep the silhouette legible at shortcut size and leave clear space for a short label beneath it.

# Color and Materials

Use soft blue, red, yellow, green, and neutral vehicle tones against white or pale gray. Keep the main UI action black rather than borrowing illustration colors.

# Variants and States

Use illustrations for service selection, delivery types, driver programs, tips, and lightweight education. Use maps, real vehicle facts, and plain controls for active-trip decisions.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the observed imagery language with unrelated stock art or arbitrary symbols.
- Do not change the documented crop, density, or relationship to nearby text.
