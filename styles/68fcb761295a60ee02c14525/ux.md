# Overview

Kupibilet is a flight-search and booking app that combines itinerary comparison, passenger forms, add-ons, payment, and order status.

# Navigation

Five persistent destinations cover Search, Map, Chat, Orders, and Profile. Search begins with route entry; results and booking continue through focused screens with back navigation.

# Core Flows

## Find a flight

1. Set origin, destination, dates, passengers, and cabin.
2. Review date prices and choose exact or flexible dates.
3. Compare ticket cards, then open itinerary details and baggage options.

## Book and pay

1. Enter contact and passenger document data.
2. Review fare extras and insurance choices.
3. Select a payment method and enter card details.
4. Return to the order screen for payment status and route details.

# Interaction Patterns

- Search and results keep price comparison close to dates and itinerary duration.
