# Overview

Pomosch supports transparent direct aid through beneficiary profiles, project funding, recurring payments, community activities, reports, and donor recognition.

# Navigation

A five-item primary navigation anchors Project, Payments, Help, Doing, and Awards. The Help feed adds category filters, map access, search, and a persistent project-support shortcut.

# Core Flows

## Find and provide help

1. Filter or search beneficiary profiles, open a person or project, review location, collection goal, progress, existing supporters, and reports, then make a transfer or recurring contribution.

## Verify and participate

1. Review project finances, reporting documents, people, partners, and FAQ; browse volunteer activities; inspect payment history and awards; manage the donor profile.
