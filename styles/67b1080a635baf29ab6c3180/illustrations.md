# Overview

memo uses a friendly 3D screen mascot and deliberately eclectic meme illustration to make language practice playful and memorable.

# Visual Style

The mascot is a compact retro screen with thin limbs, a black face, and simple white expressions. Learning media mixes rough cartoons, collage, expressive characters, and intentionally low-fi internet aesthetics.

# Composition

Center the mascot alone on a saturated field for brand states. In lessons, let one meme or character fill most of the vertical canvas and keep captions readable above or below the focal action.

# Color and Materials

Use acid green as the brand anchor with black, white, yellow, electric blue, red, and other saturated meme colors.

# Variants and States

Use the mascot for onboarding, progress, empty states, and encouragement. Use meme art only when it directly carries a phrase or lesson; controls must remain readable over any source style.

# Production Requirements

Preserve the documented placement, crop, negative space, and visual weight in production exports. Before final art exists, placeholders must preserve those same compositional properties.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the observed imagery language with unrelated stock art or arbitrary symbols.
- Do not change the documented crop, density, or relationship to nearby text.

# Known Gaps

Animation, export dimensions, safe-crop specifications, appearance variants, and unshown state treatments were not documented.
