# Overview

Litres is a book and audiobook service spanning personalized discovery, search, book detail, subscription, text reading, audio playback, collections, and personal library management.

# Navigation

Five fixed destinations cover Home, Search, Reader, My Books, and Profile. Home adds a content taxonomy for genres, recommendations, books, and audiobooks.

# Core Flows

## Discover and choose

1. Browse recommendations, promotions, genres, stories, quotes, or search.
2. Open a book or audiobook and review cover, rating, description, tags, versions, and subscription availability.
3. Buy, start a free fragment, save, or add to a collection.

## Read or listen

1. Open the reader or audio preview.
2. Adjust text, search, contents, playback speed, or sleep settings.
3. Continue from the persistent progress state.
4. Organize finished, downloaded, saved, and archived titles in My Books.
