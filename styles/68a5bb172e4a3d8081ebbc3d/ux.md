# Overview

Radio Arzamas is an editorial audio library organized around courses, podcasts, lecturers, saved material, downloads, and listening history. Content discovery is, while playback and subscription state remain available without leaving the current context.

# Navigation

- A five-item primary navigation leads to Home, Catalog, Saved, Downloads, and History.
- Profile opens from a control in the top-right corner.
- Catalog switches between Albums, Tracks, and Lecturers, with topic chips and sorting below.
- A persistent mini-player appears above the primary navigation during playback.

# Core Flows

## Discover and listen

1. Browse the editorial hero and scrolling content shelves.
2. Open a course or podcast to review its author, description, and episode list.
3. Start the full work or an individual episode.
4. Continue through the mini-player or open the full player for queue, timer, and playback settings.

## Search and filter

1. Open Catalog and enter a query.
2. Narrow results by content type, topic chips, lecturer, or ordering.
3. Open a result, save it, download it, or begin listening.

## Save and subscribe

1. Bookmark a course or episode from a shelf or detail page.
2. Manage saved and downloaded content from their dedicated tabs.
3. When premium material is opened, compare monthly and annual plans in the subscription sheet.
4. Complete or restore a subscription from Profile.

# Interaction Patterns

- Playback is continuous across screens through a persistent mini-player.
