# Overview

Perekrestok combines loyalty, in-store support, catalog browsing, grocery ordering, checkout, and order tracking in a familiar retail structure.

# Navigation

A five-item bottom bar anchors Home, Catalog, Cart, Promotions, and Profile. Home begins with loyalty barcode and service shortcuts before campaigns and product rails.

# Core Flows

## Browse and build cart

1. Enter from a category, promotion, recommendation, or search, open products as needed, then add and adjust quantities in the cart.

## Checkout and order

1. Review items, choose delivery and wishes, select payment in a bottom sheet, place the order, then follow its status in My Orders.

# Interaction Patterns

Green circular quantity controls support repeated basket edits, horizontally scrolling campaigns expose offers, and checkout choices use grouped rows and sheets rather than long free-form forms.
