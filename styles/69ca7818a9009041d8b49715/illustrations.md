# Overview

bushe uses a repeatable authored mascot and character language for brand moments, empty states, ratings, logout, and selected decorative home cards. It remains secondary to real food photography in commerce. New character art must be generated with the available image-generation model, visually approved before integration, and inserted as the approved raster asset. Do not reconstruct it with SwiftUI shapes, `Canvas`, programmatic vectors, SF Symbols, or emoji.

# Visual Style

Characters and small objects are warm, playful, and simplified, with rounded or slightly awkward proportions, sparse facial detail, and hand-made irregularity. The treatment is flat to lightly textured rather than glossy. Thin dark marks, loose contours, flowers, and simple food or human motifs create personality without dense scenery.

# Composition

Empty and modal states center one large mascot or a compact object cluster above short copy, leaving generous white space. Decorative home cards may place the character toward one edge while preserving a clean text zone. Rating sheets use smaller celebratory flowers or character accents around the decision. Keep complete silhouettes visible and subordinate art to transactional controls.

# Color and Materials

Warm orange is the primary character accent, supported by dusty pink, pale yellow, lavender, green, and occasional cool blue. Dark charcoal provides facial marks and structure. Surfaces feel matte and paper-like with restrained texture; avoid glossy gradients, metallic lighting, or photoreal rendering. The palette must sit comfortably against the white and charcoal roles in `ui.md`.

# Variants and States

Brand and home moments may use the most expressive character pose. Empty notifications use one centered figure with broad negative space. Logout and destructive sheets keep the art calm and smaller than the decision controls. Rating states add flowers or compact celebratory motifs. Product, catalog, basket, payment, and checkout screens remain photography- or UI-led.

# Avoid

- Do not replace real food photography with mascot art.
- Do not substitute the mascot with SF Symbols, emoji, SwiftUI shapes, or programmatic vectors.
- Do not use glossy 3D characters, photoreal people, or generic stock illustration.
- Do not place decorative art behind prices, totals, payment fields, or quantity controls.
- Do not crowd empty and modal states with multiple competing scenes.
- Do not crop away the main character silhouette or key gesture.
