# Overview

Trading 212 combines investing, cash pots, watchlists, market research, social ideas, and a payment card in one account. The experience moves from a summary dashboard into instrument-specific analysis and transactional order sheets.

# Navigation

- A six-item primary navigation covers Home, Portfolio, Search, Card, Social, and Menu.
- The Home header switches account context and exposes notifications.
- Instrument pages use close/back navigation and keep Buy and Sell available.

# Core Flows

## Open and fund an account

1. Register and provide personal, address, financial, and investing details.
2. Complete identity verification and accept required policies.
3. Add funds and follow the visible setup checklist.

## Research and buy an instrument

1. Search or open an item from a watchlist or market list.
2. Review chart, statistics, analysis, events, community posts, and company information.
3. Choose Buy, configure order type and value, then review the order.

## Manage a portfolio

1. Review account value, return, pots, and investment allocation.
2. Open a holding, pie, pending order, or alert.
3. Rebalance, deposit, sell, or adjust watchlist and alert settings.

# Interaction Patterns

The sampled flows use direct actions, explicit completion, and return to the current context. No additional repeated interaction behavior was documented.
