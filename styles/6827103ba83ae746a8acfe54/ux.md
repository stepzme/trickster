# Overview

Gold Apple combines editorial beauty discovery with search, catalog and brand browsing, product and variant evaluation, favorites, bag, checkout, delivery, and account tasks.

# Navigation

A light five-item bottom bar provides the main storefront destinations. Home, search, favorites, and bag remain visually quiet so campaigns and products lead.

# Core Flows

## Discover and evaluate

1. Enter through editorial campaigns, categories, brands, new arrivals, search, or recommendations, filter the catalog, then inspect imagery, description, reviews, price, and variants.

## Buy

1. Select shade or size, add to bag, review benefits and availability, choose delivery and payment, confirm the order, and follow its state.

# Interaction Patterns

Photography opens discovery, filters use compact controls, product detail reveals information through fine-rule sections, and one black action remains visually dominant through purchase.
