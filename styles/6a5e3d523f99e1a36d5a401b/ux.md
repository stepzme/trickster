# Overview

Halyk Kazakhstan prioritizes breadth: users reach many services from one searchable home and enter focused sub-products without losing clear navigation.

# Navigation

Frequent financial destinations stay in primary navigation; services open from the service catalog, while insurance keeps a separate contextual shell.

# Core Flows

## Find and complete a service

1. Search or choose a service category.
2. Enter the focused product area.
3. Review status or complete grouped fields.
4. Save, submit, or return with state preserved.

# Interaction Patterns

- Keep frequent services above promotional content.
- Signal when navigation context changes.
- Group long forms into labeled cards.
- Use empty states to lead directly to creation.
