# Overview

Tolan turns an AI companion into a persistent character living on a small planet. Conversation, daily wellbeing activities, journaling, customization, and progression all return to that shared world.

# Navigation

- The planet is the home surface, with camera, chat, and voice actions anchored at the bottom.
- A left drawer opens Journal, Intentions, Insights, Check-ins, Library, Family, Shop, character, planet, and settings.
- Contextual cards above the character launch affirmations, questions, sessions, and suggested conversations.

# Core Flows

## Meet the companion

1. Enter basic identity and preference details.
2. Let the app generate an alien companion, then choose its voice and name.
3. Review membership and arrive on the companion's planet.

## Talk and build a routine

1. Start text or voice conversation from the planet.
2. Use daily cards for intentions, affirmations, journal prompts, and wellbeing sessions.
3. Return over time to unlock insights and progress the relationship.

## Personalize the world

1. Open the companion or planet editor.
2. Buy or select clothes and decorations.
3. Preview changes on the live character and place them in the world.

# Interaction Patterns

- The animated planet remains visible behind lightweight cards and chat overlays.
- Daily activities are presented one at a time in a horizontal card carousel.
- Tokens, locked insights, and membership benefits make progression explicit.
- Privacy-sensitive journal and profile actions use clear confirmation and lock states.
