# Overview

Arbuz.kz is a grocery marketplace combining delivery context, product discovery, categories, favorites, cart, bonuses, and profile. Green actions and a five-item bottom bar keep a promotional home feed usable.

# Navigation

Home, Catalog, Cart, Favorites, and Profile remain in the bottom bar. Search stays high on commerce screens. Address and delivery time appear before products so availability and pricing have context.

# Core Flows

## Entry and home

1. Authenticate by phone or continue to browse.
2. Set address and delivery timing.
3. Browse campaigns, shortcuts, discounts, and horizontal product rails.

## Catalog and favorites

Catalog groups illustrated food and household tiles into clear sections. Product cards expose discount, name, weight, rewards price, regular price, favorite, and add control. Favorites preserves the same purchase context.

## Cart

Empty cart offers one recovery path plus recommendations. Filled cart shows address, delivery threshold, items, quantities, rewards benefit, recommendations, total, and a fixed pay action.

## Profile

Profile combines orders, reviews, cashback, bonuses, subscription, payments, delivery, policies, and support without mixing them into shopping navigation.

# Interaction Patterns

- Keep address and delivery time visible.
- Show rewards and cash prices together.
- Use green for selected navigation and purchase actions.
- Explain free-delivery and cashback thresholds in cart.
- Use mascots for recovery and education, not product choice.
