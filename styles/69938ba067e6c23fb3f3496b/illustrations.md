# Overview

Avito uses a recognizable authored illustration language to welcome, explain, fill empty states, and close purchase or account tasks. The art is clearly separate from listing photography and functional icons: it appears as a centered scene or compact decorative object cluster on white or softly tinted space. When new art is required, generate it with the available image-generation model, obtain visual approval before integration, and insert the approved image asset; do not approximate it with SwiftUI shapes, `Canvas`, programmatic vectors, SF Symbols, or emoji.

# Visual Style

Forms are rounded, simplified, and playful, with sparse internal detail and soft dimensional shading. People and objects use friendly proportions rather than realism. Recognizable motifs include bags, homes, devices, coins, seller tools, and category objects. The treatment sits between flat editorial art and soft 3D: silhouettes remain clean while subtle volume, highlight, and shadow make objects feel tangible.

# Composition

Empty, login, and success states center one clear subject or a small scene above concise copy and an action, leaving broad white negative space. Promotional cards may place a compact object cluster toward one edge while reserving an uncluttered area for text. The illustration is a meaningful focal mass in these states but never covers navigation, prices, form fields, or listing evidence. Crops keep the full readable silhouette of the main object or character.

# Color and Materials

Use Avito cyan, commerce purple, green, pink-red, and warm yellow in controlled combinations against white or a pale tinted field. One accent should dominate each scene while the remaining colors provide small supporting notes. Surfaces are smooth and lightly shaded, with soft shadows and occasional glossy highlights; avoid gritty texture, hard black outlines, or photoreal materials.

# Variants and States

Login and profile guidance use a welcoming person or object-led scene. Empty states use a single readable subject with ample surrounding space. Order or task success uses a brighter celebratory cluster and may emphasize green while retaining the multicolor palette. Promotional guidance can be more compact and edge-aligned. Across variants, preserve rounded forms, soft depth, sparse detail, and a clear separation from real marketplace photography.

# Avoid

- Do not imitate listing photography or use generic stock imagery.
- Do not substitute the authored scene with SF Symbols, emoji, SwiftUI shapes, or programmatic vectors.
- Do not place art behind prices, totals, filters, or editable controls.
- Do not crowd the scene with many tiny props or multiple competing focal points.
- Do not use harsh outlines, gritty texture, photoreal people, or unrelated corporate-gradient characters.
- Do not crop away the main silhouette or remove the negative space that separates art from copy.
