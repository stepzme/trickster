# Overview

Pillowtalk turns journaling into a daily voice-first ritual, then organizes transcripts, typed entries, moods, patterns, and AI-guided reflection.

# Navigation

A four-item bottom bar anchors Today, Explore, Entries, and Patterns. A floating white add button starts a new entry from multiple sections.

# Core Flows

## Capture and reflect

1. Start from Today, choose Type, Yap, or Transcribe, record or write, review the transcript, attach mood or people, and open the analysis or deeper prompts.

## Review and patterns

1. Browse entries by date, open or edit an entry, review progress and recurring feelings or people, then unlock and share deeper patterns.

# Interaction Patterns

Voice entry is foregrounded in a large atmospheric panel, dates use a compact weekly rail, the plus action persists, and deeper AI interpretation stays downstream of the captured journal entry.
