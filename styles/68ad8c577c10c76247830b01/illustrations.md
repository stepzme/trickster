# Overview

Sportmaster uses a lively service-illustration family beside its product photography.

# Visual Style

Rounded, toy-like 3D icons use saturated blue, violet, coral, and orange with soft volume. Promotional scenes may add a friendly mascot, coins, sports props, or devices.

# Composition

Place one compact object cluster inside a rounded square tile. Larger promos combine a short headline with a centered object or mascot group on a solid blue field.

# Color and Materials

Blue is the anchor. Add one or two bright supporting hues per tile and keep the background pale or strongly branded.

# Variants and States

Use the language for services, activity tools, loyalty, media, and playful promotions. Keep product, category, and sizing imagery photographic.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the observed imagery language with unrelated stock art or arbitrary symbols.
- Do not change the documented crop, density, or relationship to nearby text.
