# Overview

ATTO joins city services, transport cards, trip planning, and fare payment. The signed-out hub advertises breadth; the signed-in transport workspace prioritizes balance and the next payment action.

# Navigation

The service hub opens from a hamburger and support header. Transport uses Main, Trip history, Transport, and Menu. Routes and metro become focused map workspaces with back, mode, and zoom controls.

# Core Flows

## Setup and access

1. Onboarding explains contactless transport, tickets, and stored services, then offers language and skip. Registration, sign-in, recovery, and card setup remain distinct.

## Transport and routes

1. Open the transport card and confirm balance.
2. Choose route, stop, metro, or transaction history.
3. Search, plan, pin, or switch map modes while the map remains visible.

## Payment

1. QR, NFC, top-up, fare plans, FacePay, and PalmPay are separate actions. Mode sheets identify bus or metro before a QR payment, and failure states keep a clear recovery path.

## Account and support

1. Menu groups cards, security, language, appearance, devices, tariff plans, questions, support, and transport feedback.

# Interaction Patterns

- Show balance before every payment action.
- Keep payment methods visually distinct.
- Preserve map context while opening search or route choices.
- Transport-mode decisions open as focused choices and return to search.
