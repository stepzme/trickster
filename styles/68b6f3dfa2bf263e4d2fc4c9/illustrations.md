# Overview

Idoo uses fashion-sketch illustration and cloud-like organic frames to make city discovery feel expressive and personal.

# Visual Style

Figures are drawn with thin black outlines, sparse flat fills, and exaggerated fashion details. Pale gradient backgrounds and cut-paper white shapes keep the compositions airy.

# Composition

- Center one cropped figure or accessory against generous negative space.
- Let illustration edges extend beyond the viewport.
- Use irregular white cloud silhouettes behind editorial content and photography.

# Color

Keep line work black and surfaces white. Add one or two high-energy accents such as coral red, cobalt blue, or warm peach over soft lavender or sand gradients.

# Usage

Use original illustrations for onboarding, permissions, and route-discovery transitions. Use photography for real places; do not replace factual place imagery with drawings.
