# Overview

Arbuz.kz uses smiling grocery characters, expressive shopping bags, and bright 3D food objects to explain promotions, rewards, subscription, favorites, and empty states.

# Visual Style

Render rounded friendly characters with tiny faces, simple limbs, and soft studio shading. Supporting food and grocery objects may be glossy 3D or carefully isolated photographic cutouts.

# Composition

Use one central character in empty states with generous white space. Promotions may build playful product scenes around a clear headline, while loyalty banners keep one object anchored to the edge.

# Color

Anchor the language in fresh green and mint, then add yellow, orange, aqua, and pink. Preserve natural food colors and keep faces dark and minimal.

# Usage

Use characters for empty cart/favorites, cashback, subscription, and campaign education. Use literal product photography for shopping decisions and simple line icons for navigation.
