# Overview

GO Club uses bold graphic objects and compact line-art scenes as essential visual counterweights to metrics, plans, onboarding, weather, and milestones.

# Visual Style

Objects are simplified, softly dimensional, and saturated. Supporting scenes use loose black line work with orange fills and intentionally imperfect contours. The tone is energetic and playful rather than polished corporate 3D or stock photography.

# Composition

Use one object or one compact scene. On large cards, art commonly occupies 25–45% of the surface and sits opposite or below a short headline. Preserve generous negative space around both copy and art. Cropping may be assertive at a card edge but must keep the motif recognizable.

# Color and Materials

Anchor art in cobalt, white, lemon, mint, and orange. Black outlines supply contrast. Dimensional objects may use soft blue highlights, but avoid realistic materials, detailed textures, and neutral gray rendering.

# Variants and States

Plans and weather use contextual scenes. Onboarding and permission states use one optimistic object or character. Progress and success use simpler celebratory objects. Empty states remain sparse and should not become decorative collages.

# Production Requirements

Prepare transparent-background assets for object compositions and card-matched backgrounds for full scenes. Keep safe crop regions for compact iPhones. Provide light-on-blue and dark-on-yellow contrast variants when needed. A temporary placeholder must occupy the same region and approximate the same color mass as final art.

# Avoid

Avoid stock photography, generic SF Symbols enlarged as artwork, glossy corporate 3D, dense multi-object scenes, tiny corner illustrations, and removing art while preserving only text.

# Known Gaps

Animation, dark appearance variants, and high-density promotional compositions were not observed.
