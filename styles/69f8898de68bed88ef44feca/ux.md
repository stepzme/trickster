# Overview

Poizon blends a social media feed, short-form product content, marketplace search, authenticity information, product comparison, and checkout.

# Navigation

A four-item primary navigation anchors Dewu, Shopping, Discover, and Me. Home-level tabs switch social topics, while Shopping adds search, category, sorting, and filter controls.

# Core Flows

## Discover and shop

1. Browse the social feed or Shopping, search by text or photo, filter results, open a product, inspect media, authenticity, sales, reviews, sizing, and related products.

## Buy and manage

1. Choose the product or variant, review price and service assurances, buy, add delivery details, then manage orders, favorites, wallet, subscriptions, and seller tools from profile.

# Interaction Patterns

The sampled flows use direct actions, explicit completion, and return to the current context. No additional repeated interaction behavior was documented.
