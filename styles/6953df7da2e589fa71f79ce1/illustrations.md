# Overview

Drinkit uses softly rendered 3D baristas, expressive heads, miniature seasonal worlds, and whimsical product companions to turn order status and campaigns into warm character moments.

# Visual Style

Use smooth toy-like 3D forms, rounded faces, simple features, soft studio lighting, gentle shadows, and slightly surreal miniature props. Characters wear recognizable café uniforms and remain friendly rather than cartoonishly exaggerated.

# Composition

Center one character or miniature scene in a rounded card with generous pale space. Keep status text directly below or beside the figure and reserve detailed environments for campaign cards.

# Color and Materials

Anchor characters in cobalt, periwinkle, cream, and soft gray; vary seasonal scenes with warm orange, winter blue, cocoa brown, or mint while retaining the cobalt brand cue.

# Variants and States

Use illustration for order acceptance, barista identity, tips, predictions, gifts, onboarding, and seasonal stories. New scenes should retain the same toy-like material and soft lighting so they remain harmonious with `ui.md`.

# Production Requirements

Use only the formats and crops supported by the implemented placement. Where the documented imagery is compositionally important, temporary placeholders must preserve its placement, crop, scale, negative space, and approximate visual weight until final art exists. No unobserved light/dark variants or export ratios are prescribed.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the documented image language with unrelated stock art, arbitrary symbols, or a denser composition.
