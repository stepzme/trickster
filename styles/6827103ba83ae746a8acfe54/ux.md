# Overview

Gold Apple supports discovery, catalog search, product evaluation, saving, cart management, checkout, order status, account tasks, and gift-card creation. Preserve the behavioral pattern of moving from broad discovery into progressively more specific product and fulfillment decisions; do not copy retail destinations that the adapted product does not need.

# Navigation

Primary destinations remain directly switchable during browsing. Search opens catalog, brand, new-item, editorial, and promotional paths. Product details are pushed from results or recommendations and retain a return path. Cart and checkout form a task sequence; checkout steps return to the active order rather than creating new primary destinations. Account tasks lead to orders, saved items, addresses, notifications, gift cards, and documents.

# Core Flows

## Discover from the home feed

1. Open the home destination.
2. Choose a campaign, shortcut, product group, or recommended item.
3. Continue into the selected collection or product.
4. Return to the previous browsing context without losing the current destination.

## Search and refine the catalog

1. Open search.
2. Enter a query or choose a catalog, brand, new-item, or promotional path.
3. Apply filters or sorting when the result set needs refinement.
4. Open a result and review its details.
5. Return to the result set with the query and refinement state retained.

## Evaluate and add a product

1. Open a product from a listing or recommendation.
2. Review media, price, offer terms, reviews, description, and delivery availability.
3. Choose a required variant when the product has one.
4. Add the product to the cart or save it for later.
5. Receive immediate confirmation that the chosen action succeeded.

## Complete an order

1. Open the cart and review items, quantities, discounts, and total.
2. Start checkout.
3. Choose delivery or pickup and provide the required address details.
4. Select or confirm the recipient and payment method.
5. Apply a promo code when available and review the updated total.
6. Submit the order.
7. Receive confirmation with the order identifier, fulfillment details, and a path to order status.

## Create a gift card

1. Open gift cards from search or account tasks.
2. Choose the recipient and design.
3. Select or enter the value.
4. Add an optional greeting and delivery time.
5. Choose payment and confirm creation.

# Interaction Patterns

- Opening a product, editorial item, or account task preserves a clear return path.
- Search keeps query and refinement state when the user inspects a result and returns.
- Required choices block progression and identify the missing decision near its control.
- Adding, saving, applying a promo code, and changing quantity provide immediate confirmation and update dependent totals.
- Checkout exposes delivery, recipient, payment, discount, and total before submission.
- Cancel and close actions do not silently discard an in-progress checkout or gift-card task.
- Order confirmation provides a persistent route to status and fulfillment information.
