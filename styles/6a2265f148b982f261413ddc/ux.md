# Overview

Mycar.kz combines car discovery, financing, selling, vehicle records, service booking, insurance, government services, assistance, notifications, and profile tools.

# Navigation

Four primary destinations connect Home, Services, Notifications, and Profile. Home starts with sell, buy, and owned-car entry points, then service shortcuts, campaigns, and a personalized vehicle results.

# Core Flows

## Find and finance a car

1. Enter Buy or browse personalized listings.
2. Compare image-led cards, price, location, and seller context.
3. Review the gallery, specifications, financing estimate, and contact options.
4. Calculate a monthly payment or message or call the seller.

## Sell a car

1. Start Sell and provide vehicle identity.
2. Complete the progressive specification questionnaire and add photos.
3. Review the listing and submit it to drafts or publication.
4. Manage the result from My listings.

# Interaction Patterns

- Long seller forms expose one grouped decision at a time with visible step progress.
