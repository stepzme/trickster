# Overview

FocusPomo uses expressive tomato and citrus characters plus trophy motifs to turn completed sessions into a warm collectible garden.

# Visual Style

Use flat rounded fruit shapes, tiny faces and limbs, imperfect hand-drawn details, warm paper texture, and simple celebratory icons.

# Composition

Gather many small characters along the lower edge or arrange them in a tidy progress grid; reserve one large trophy or fruit for milestone cards.

# Color and Materials

Use tomato orange, lemon yellow, leaf green, cream, peach, and dark taupe with soft pastel chart colors.

# Variants and States

Use illustration for timer atmosphere, completed sessions, goals, statistics, onboarding, and empty states. Extend the same fruit world for new visuals so they remain harmonious with `ui.md`.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the documented image language with unrelated stock art, arbitrary symbols, or a denser composition.
