# Overview

CDEK combines parcel creation, pickup-point discovery, order tracking, payment, support, business services, and shopping.

# Navigation

Home exposes primary services and recent orders; profile holds identity, payments, notifications, support, and settings.

# Core Flows

## Send a package

1. Set origin, destination, and pickup points.
2. Enter package count, dimensions, packing, and value.
3. Compare delivery terms and rates.
4. Add sender and recipient.
5. Review total, create the order, pay, and follow drop-off instructions.

## Track and manage

1. Search by tracking number, inspect status and order detail, open pickup instructions, repeat, rename, print, contact support, or review payment.

# Interaction Patterns

- Recalculate total after every route or package change.
- Show package scale visually.
- Keep shipment and shopping orders distinct.
- Explain required pickup documents.
- Confirm payment before tracking begins.
