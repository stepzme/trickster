# Overview

Headspace uses expressive flat illustration as the primary emotional layer across discovery and playback.

# Visual Style

Use simple rounded characters, hand-drawn organic shapes, subtle texture, and warm editorial wit rather than realism.

# Composition

Build one clear idea per card with large color fields; for playback, expand the artwork into broad horizontal bands around the central control.

# Color

Favor saturated yellow, orange, purple, pink, and deep blue with a limited palette per scene.

# Usage

Use illustrations for practice categories, sleep content, guidance, and playback mood. New art should follow this flat friendly language and remain harmonious with `ui.md`.
