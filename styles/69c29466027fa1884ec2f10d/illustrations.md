# Illustration Language

Use bold flat vector scenes with simplified human forms, cropped body parts, and symbolic relationship objects. Shapes are chunky and graphic, with minimal facial detail and playful impossible color.

# Palette and Form

- Build artwork from saturated purple, hot pink, orange, red, electric blue, lime, and small pale accents.
- Use thick simple silhouettes, rounded joints, and clean edge-to-edge color fields.
- Favor one memorable metaphor per tile: crossed hands, masks, drinks, rings, flags, or conversation objects.

# Composition

- Card-pack thumbnails use centered symbols inside rounded squares.
- Onboarding artwork may float one larger object over the dark canvas.
- Match celebrations can combine clipped profile photos with pink banners and small confetti shapes.

# Do and Don't

- Keep illustrations direct, diverse, and conversation-oriented.
- Reuse the same flat proportions and saturated palette across every pack.
- Do not introduce detailed realism, thin corporate line art, or glossy 3D rendering.
