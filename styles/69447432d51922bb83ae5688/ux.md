# Overview

Burger King combines food ordering, delivery, loyalty crowns, coupons, challenges, and support in one branded shell.

# Navigation

My addresses, Menu, Crowns, Coupons, and More are persistent. Product, cart, checkout, and tracking use focused navigation.

# Core Flows

## Order

1. Select address and menu category.
2. Choose a product or configure a combo.
3. Review basket, suggestions, delivery threshold, and total.
4. Select payment and confirm address.
5. Track acceptance, preparation, courier, and delivery.

## Loyalty

1. Crowns shows balance, earning history, challenges, campaigns, personalized offers, and clear spending rules.

# Interaction Patterns

- Keep delivery fee thresholds visible.
- Preserve quantity and combo configuration.
- Confirm address immediately before payment.
- Show crowns earned after order completion.
- Separate coupons from crown balance.
