# Overview

Vivid is a consumer finance hub combining account pockets, debit cards, timeline analytics, payments, cashback rewards, investing, and support.

# Navigation

- Five primary destinations cover Pockets, Timeline, Payments, Rewards, and Invest.
- Pockets is the account home, with balances, product tiles, items, and creation actions.
- Payments and Invest use local categories and search while preserving the bottom product map.

# Core Flows

## Open and organize an account

1. Choose personal or business and register by phone or email.
2. Complete identity and security steps.
3. Create named pockets and connect a card or savings product.

## Move and review money

1. Open Payments to request, add, transfer, or send money.
2. Choose a recipient, pocket, and amount.
3. Review the result in Timeline with filters and analytics.

## Earn and invest

1. Choose cashback categories or open a reward offer.
2. Open Invest and browse personalized, stock, or crypto collections.
3. Review an asset, enter an order, and track it in the relevant pocket.

# Interaction Patterns

- Product creation is progressive and always returns to the pocket overview.
