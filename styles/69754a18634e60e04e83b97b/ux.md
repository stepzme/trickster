# Overview

Chizhik combines grocery discovery, store context, scanner, delivery ordering, promotions, and profile utilities.

# Navigation

Home, Catalog, Scanner, and Profile remain persistent. Product, basket, checkout, store, and order detail use focused navigation.

# Core Flows

## Browse and order

1. Confirm store or delivery address.
2. Search, scan, browse category, or open a promotion.
3. Review product detail and availability.
4. Add quantities to basket.
5. Enter address details, comments, delivery slot, and payment.
6. Track or review the order.

## Store and promotions

1. Store cards expose route, catalog, reviews, vacancies, and delivery terms. Stories and promotion cards remain separate from product search.

# Interaction Patterns

- Tie stock to the selected store.
- Keep minimum order and basket total visible.
- Require age confirmation where necessary.
- Preserve product favorites.
- Confirm cart clearing and cancellation.

# System Access Timing

No system-access request timing or denial recovery was documented in the reviewed source.
