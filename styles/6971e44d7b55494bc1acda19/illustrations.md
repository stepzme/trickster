# Overview

Airba fresh uses a recurring avocado mascot and related grocery/reward objects for onboarding, empty, success, bonus, QR, and assistance states. This is a supporting state language, not a substitute for product photography. New authored assets must be created with the available image-generation model, visually approved before integration, and inserted as approved image assets; an illustration-required state is incomplete until the asset is visible in the interface.

# Visual Style

The mascot is a friendly rounded avocado character with a green body, brown pit, large expressive eyes, tiny limbs, and simple cheerful poses. Forms are soft and volumetric with clean edges, gentle shading, and a polished cartoon finish. Supporting reward, grocery, or service objects use similarly rounded proportions and bright color, with more dimensionality than flat navigation icons but less realism than product photography.

# Composition

Center one mascot pose in generous white or very pale green space, usually as the primary visual mass of a sparse state. Keep the full silhouette, face, hands, feet, and relevant prop visible; do not crop through the pit or gesture. Place short text below or beside the character with clear separation. Promotional and bonus surfaces may combine the mascot with one oversized reward object or compact group of grocery props, but the illustration should remain bounded and leave a protected copy/action zone.

# Color and Materials

Anchor the character in fresh greens and a consistent brown pit, then use yellow, orange, blue, or pink for props and reward emphasis. Shading is soft and restrained, with smooth matte-to-lightly-glossy surfaces and no harsh black outline. Backgrounds remain white, pale green, or one clean promotional field tied to the green and blue roles in `ui.md`.

# Variants and States

Onboarding and promotional variants may use a more energetic pose and several reward or grocery props. Empty and help states reduce to one centered character with a simple expression and ample whitespace. Success variants use an affirmative gesture or celebratory prop while preserving the same body, eyes, pit, and rendering. Bonus or QR education may place the mascot inside a broader blue/green branded panel. Product browsing, detail, cart rows, and checkout evidence remain photo- and data-led.

# Avoid

- Do not draw required mascot art with SwiftUI shapes, Canvas, programmatic vectors, SF Symbols, or emoji.
- Do not integrate generated artwork before visual review and explicit approval.
- Do not replace product packshots or category photography with mascot scenes.
- Do not switch to flat corporate vectors, sketchy line art, photorealistic food characters, or glossy 3D toy rendering.
- Do not change the avocado body, brown pit, facial construction, or rounded proportions between states.
- Do not crowd a sparse empty state with many props, background patterns, or decorative copy.
- Do not crop the face, pit, gesture, or state-defining prop.
