# Overview

bushe uses hand-drawn characters and editorial collage to make food ordering feel like a local culture magazine. Illustration appears in intent cards, loyalty, onboarding, table ordering, projects, and seasonal features.

# Visual Style

Draw loose, slightly awkward human and food characters with thin dark outlines, flat pastel fills, off-center facial features, and occasional scribbles or sparkles. Editorial features may combine photographs, oversized serif type, and simple geometric collage.

# Composition

Keep one character or object scene inside a wide banner or full-screen card. Leave generous pale space around figures. For editorial pages, allow type to overlap collage elements while keeping the reading column clear.

# Color and Materials

Use warm cream, peach, orange, dusty pink, lavender, pale yellow, and occasional cold blue. Dark charcoal outlines and text provide structure. Avoid glossy gradients and highly rendered 3D materials.

# Variants and States

Use characters for intent selection, loyalty progression, instructions, and playful status moments. Use photographic-editorial collage for stories, events, and seasonal collections. Do not replace product photography or dense checkout controls with illustration.

# Production Requirements

Use only the formats and crops supported by the implemented placement. Where the documented imagery is compositionally important, temporary placeholders must preserve its placement, crop, scale, negative space, and approximate visual weight until final art exists. No unobserved light/dark variants or export ratios are prescribed.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the documented image language with unrelated stock art, arbitrary symbols, or a denser composition.
