# Overview

Aviasales turns travel intent into flight, hotel, and experience bookings. Search remains structured, comparison exposes trade-offs, and post-purchase tools keep tickets, baggage, support, statistics, and documents accessible.

# Navigation

Flights, Hotels, Experiences, Favorites, and Profile persist across discovery. Search context remains visible during long results; purchase flows switch to focused back navigation.

# Core Flows

## Discover and search

1. Home mixes route search with hot tickets, seasonal selections, anywhere search, smart search, complex routes, editorial stories, and city guides.

## Compare and purchase flights

1. Choose route, dates, travelers, and optional accommodation.
2. Compare filters, sorting, transfer, baggage, and price-chart signals.
3. Review ticket details, fare, protection, seller offers, passenger data, payment, and confirmation.

## Hotels and experiences

1. Hotel search supports map, filters, details, media, services, reviews, rooms, and booking. Experiences move from city guide to excursion or venue details, maps, collections, and booking.

## Profile and post-purchase

1. Profile contains orders, issuing and issued tickets, calendar, baggage, flight statistics, maps, visited places, documents, payment, region, notification, and privacy settings.

# Interaction Patterns

- Keep route, dates, travelers, and total price visible.
- Make filters reversible and show what was removed.
- Separate flight fare from seller choice and add-ons.
- Preserve favorites across flights, hotels, and places.
- Keep support adjacent to active orders.
