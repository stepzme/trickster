# Overview

Badoo moves from profile setup to nearby discovery, encounter decisions, likes, matches, chat, and ongoing safety. The product keeps identity, intent, and consent visible throughout.

# Navigation

Nearby, Encounters, Likes, Chat, and Profile persist in the bottom bar. Discovery filters stay in the top bar; full-screen matches and account subflows use close or back.

# Core Flows

## Setup and discovery

Onboarding gathers identity, education, photos, interests, location, and dating intent one question at a time. Nearby supports direct connection; Encounters supports pass, like, boost, undo, and filters.

## Match and chat

1. Like or connect from discovery.
2. Enter a match state with a clear conversation action.
3. Chat with text, media, emoji, voice, read state, calls, privacy guidance, block, and report.

## Profile and trust

Profile editing covers photos, bio, work, education, intent, interests, activity, and linked accounts. Verification and profile completion remain visible.

## Premium and safety

Plans compare likes visibility, instant chats, boosts, crushes, and receipts. Safety stays separate with help, verification, privacy, and reporting.

# Interaction Patterns

- Ask one onboarding question per step.
- Make intent and verification visible.
- Preserve an immediate safety exit from chat and profiles.
- Keep premium benefits concrete and comparable.
- Use overlays for transient match or boost state without losing context.
