# Overview

Ivi is a browse-first streaming app. Large media artwork carries discovery while persistent bottom navigation keeps search, short-form video, saved content, and account access one tap away.

# Navigation

Five fixed destinations: Feed, Search, Mini-series, My, and Account. Detail pages keep a back action and preserve the bottom bar until playback becomes the primary task.

# Core Flows

## Onboarding and subscription

1. Introduce the service with dark, media-led screens.
2. Present subscription benefits as focused full-screen steps.
3. Offer one high-contrast free-trial action and a quieter existing-subscriber path.

## Discover and watch

1. Scroll a continuous editorial feed of featured titles.
2. Open a title for synopsis, metadata, trailer, and related content.
3. Start playback or save the title for later.

## Search

1. Enter search from the fixed tab bar.
2. Refine with catalog, series, and film chips or filters.
3. Browse poster results in a dense three-column grid.

# Interaction Patterns

- Artwork is the main tap target; metadata confirms genre, rating, and duration.
- Bookmark icons support quick saving without opening a title.
- Horizontal rails preview more content while the main feed scrolls vertically.
- Subscription CTAs stay full width and close to the safe area.
