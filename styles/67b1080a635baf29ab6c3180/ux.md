# Overview

memo teaches practical language through meme clips, captions, reactions, themed collections, exploration, and lightweight profile progress.

# Navigation

Three fixed destinations connect Home, Explore, and Profile. The content viewer switches between Clips and Memes while keeping reaction, captions, speed, and sharing in thumb reach.

# Core Flows

## Learn from a clip

1. Open the feed.
2. Watch or read the phrase in context.
3. Toggle captions, adjust speed, react, or share.
4. Continue to the next clip or open a themed collection.

## Explore a topic

1. Open Explore.
2. Choose a colorful collection by situation or vocabulary goal.
3. Move through the selected sequence.
4. Return to the feed or profile progress.

# Interaction Patterns

- Humor and character expression provide the teaching context.
