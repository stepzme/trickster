# Overview

Bolt Food supports restaurant and grocery discovery, item selection, checkout, and live delivery tracking in one direct commerce flow.

# Navigation

Home, Stores, Search, Orders, and Account form the persistent base. Restaurant, item, basket, checkout, and tracking use focused back navigation.

# Core Flows

## Discover and select

1. Search or browse categories, compare rating, fee, ETA, and offers, then open a restaurant or store. the category selector keep long menus navigable.

## Order

1. Add or customize items.
2. Review quantities in the basket.
3. Confirm address and courier instructions.
4. Select tip and payment.
5. Review the full fee breakdown and place the order.

## Track and complete

1. A timeline shows confirmation, preparation, courier assignment, pickup, and arrival. Chat and cancellation remain available when relevant; rating follows completion.

# Interaction Patterns

- Keep basket total persistent after the first item.
- Show old and current price together for offers.
- Confirm address before payment.
- Expose every fee before placing the order.
- Preserve the order timeline when details expand.
