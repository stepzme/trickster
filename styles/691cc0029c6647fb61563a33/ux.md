# Overview

Ucom is a dark telecom self-service app for checking balance and allowances, topping up, managing tariffs and packages, paying bills, configuring roaming, and contacting support.

# Navigation

- Four primary destinations cover Home, Payments, Tariffs/Services, and More.
- Home summarizes the current number, balance, data, minutes, SMS, and priority shortcuts.
- Detail pages use back navigation and keep activation or payment actions pinned .

# Core Flows

## Check and replenish an account

1. Sign in with a Ucom number and verification code.
2. Review balance and current allowance gauges on Home.
3. Open Top-up, choose a method, and confirm payment.

## Change a tariff or service

1. Open Tariffs/Services and switch between the two tabs.
2. Compare allowance, coverage, and price cards.
3. Open a plan, review conditions, then activate it.

## Pay and manage services

1. Open Payments and choose top-up, utilities, taxes, bank payments, or transfers.
2. Select or add a payment method.
3. Confirm the amount and revisit the result in usage or payment history.

# Interaction Patterns

The sampled flows use direct actions, explicit completion, and return to the current context. No additional repeated interaction behavior was documented.
