# Overview

krisha.kz uses friendly object illustrations to turn property intents and service shortcuts into immediately recognizable entry points.

# Visual Style

Objects are semi-realistic 3D or softly shaded cutouts: a yellow house with keys, an orange sofa, a construction crane, a calculator, a megaphone, and simple service symbols. Forms are clean, bright, and literal rather than character-driven.

# Composition

Place one or two oversized objects inside pale rounded tiles. Promotional banners pair a short message with a larger object cluster, leaving a clear zone for the action.

# Color

Anchor art in brand yellow and warm orange, supported by light blue, green, and neutral gray. Keep tile backgrounds white or very pale gray.

# Usage

Use illustration for category entry points, services, and compact promotional banners. Do not replace property photography or analytical content with illustration; new art should inherit the UI's light surfaces, rounded geometry, and yellow emphasis.
