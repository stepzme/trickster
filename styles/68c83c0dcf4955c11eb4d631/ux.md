# Overview

gg supports taxi and delivery discovery, route setup, service and fare choice, active-trip tracking, driver contact, rating, tips, and account or support tasks.

# Navigation

The map and current bottom sheet form the primary shell. Search starts the trip; service shortcuts and a menu expose delivery, driver, support, settings, and related destinations.

# Core Flows

## Book and complete a ride

1. Confirm pickup, search or select a destination, compare services and fares, request a car, follow arrival and trip progress, then rate and optionally tip.

## Use supporting services

1. Open a service shortcut or menu destination, provide the required route or request detail, review the result, and return to the map context.
