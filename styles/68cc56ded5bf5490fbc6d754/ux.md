# Overview

VK Calls is a calling utility for creating, scheduling, joining, sharing, and managing voice or video rooms, with call history, contacts, and settings.

# Navigation

- Four primary destinations cover Home, History, Contacts, and Settings.
- Home presents Create Call, Schedule, and Join as equal top actions, followed by scheduled or active calls.
- The active call becomes a room with a dedicated control primary navigation.

# Core Flows

## Start or join a call

1. Create a call, join by link, or open an active call.
2. Configure waiting room, anonymity, microphone, and participant access.
3. Share the link or enter the call room.

## Schedule a call

1. Choose Schedule and set date, time, and participants.
2. Review the scheduled item on Home.
3. Join when active or open its menu to share, connect another device, or go to chat.

## Call a contact

1. Grant contact access and search or browse the list.
2. Choose voice or video beside a contact.
3. Revisit the call in History or recordings.

# Interaction Patterns

- Empty states explain the next action without adding decorative complexity.
