# Overview

Klarna combines merchant discovery, checkout, payment scheduling, purchase tracking, wallet utilities, cashback, and saved products in one shopping-finance experience.

# Navigation

A four-item navigation anchors Home, Payments, Wallet, and More. Search stays across discovery; merchant checkout can open inside an embedded browser with a persistent Klarna payment bar.

# Core Flows

## Discover a store

1. Search or browse recommended stores and local offers.
2. Open a merchant in the embedded browser.
3. Save the store or product, inspect price insights, or continue shopping.

## Pay with Klarna

1. Add products through the merchant checkout.
2. Choose delivery and a Klarna payment option.
3. Complete identity and payment verification when required.
4. Return to Klarna for purchase tracking.

## Manage payments

1. Review total owed and scheduled payments.
2. Open a purchase or payment date.
3. Pay early, adjust eligible settings, or inspect delivery and refund status.

# Interaction Patterns

- Payment screens present the total before supporting details and confirmation.
- Primary navigation remains available without interrupting the current task.
- Klarna payment actions remain available inside merchant browsing context.
