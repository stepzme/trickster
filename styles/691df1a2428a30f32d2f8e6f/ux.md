# Overview

An editorial course platform that moves from discovery to structured learning without changing its visual grammar. Large imagery attracts attention; progress, lesson counts, duration, and access state remain close to the content they qualify.

# Navigation

A persistent four-item bottom bar separates Home, My Courses, Library, and Profile. Home is a curated discovery surface, Library is a filterable catalog, and course details drill into lessons and the player without introducing another primary navigation layer.

# Core Flows

## Account access

1. Open the dark branded entry screen.
2. Enter an email and request a code, or choose account creation.
3. Return to the same shell after access; disabled actions remain visibly subdued until input is valid.

## Discover and start a course

1. Browse large editorial course cards and compact thematic collections.
2. Open a course to see hero media, lecturer, lesson count, duration, and access state.
3. Start the course from the prominent light CTA.
4. Watch the lesson in an embedded player, then continue through lesson navigation and key moments.

## Browse the library

1. Search or scan horizontal category chips.
2. Adjust sorting and filters from compact controls above the grid.
3. Open a two-column course tile for details.

## Get paid access

1. Choose a paid course or subscription entry point.
2. Wait on a branded loading state while the payment page opens.
3. Complete payment in the external provider surface and return to the app.

# Interaction Patterns

- Horizontal carousels reveal adjacent cards and categories without hiding vertical page progress.
- Bookmark controls sit directly on course art and details.
- Course metadata uses small icons and short numeric summaries.
- Selected bottom-navigation items switch to high-contrast white while inactive items stay muted.
- Player controls, lesson navigation, reactions, and key moments remain attached to the learning context.
