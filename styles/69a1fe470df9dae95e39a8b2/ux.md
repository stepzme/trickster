# Overview

Zopa keeps account state and money movement explicit while introducing savings and assistant features as secondary layers.

# Navigation

Home and Payments anchor the product; the central assistant is visually distinct, with Apply and Help completing the footer.

# Core Flows

## Move money

1. Choose add, move, or send money.
2. Select or create the source, destination, or payee.
3. Enter an amount and review details.
4. Confirm and receive a clear progress or failure state.

# Interaction Patterns

- Keep balance visible before transfer.
- Validate payee details before continuation.
- Separate AI assistance from final payment approval.
- Offer a direct path back home after success.
