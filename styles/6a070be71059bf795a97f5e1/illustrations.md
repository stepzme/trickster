# Overview

Fresh Screen Gallery evidence shows a stable Gosuslugi authored visual system repeated across onboarding, home, assistant entry, document cards, service shortcuts, GovScan, and service lists. Use authored raster assets where this visual language is needed; do not approximate it with generic symbols.

Missing Gosuslugi-style illustration assets must be created with an image-generation model, explicitly visually approved by the user, and integrated as raster assets in the app bundle. SwiftUI shapes, SF Symbols, emoji, font glyphs, vector doodles, gradients, and other programmatic stand-ins are not acceptable substitutes.

# Visual Style

- Official, clean, compact, and high-contrast.
- Blue/red Gosuslugi logo treatment on splash and auth surfaces.
- Minimal onboarding gesture artwork: thin black hand linework plus soft blue motion/ribbon accent.
- Compact blue assistant character/icon with rounded form and civic-service tone.
- Colorful document cards with saturated red, blue, green, orange, and red panels plus faint oversized symbols.
- Small service pictograms in rounded squares, using fine colored linework rather than heavy cartoon scenes.

# Composition

Keep illustration scale modest. One symbolic object or pictogram should support a shortcut, document, assistant, or onboarding message without crowding text. Document card silhouettes stay low contrast behind readable white titles. Onboarding art can sit above the heading with large white space.

Do not place decorative art inside long administrative forms, legal requirement lists, payment details, or document values.

# Color and Materials

Follow `ui.md`: cobalt blue is the official action color; red/green/orange/cyan/violet identify documents or service categories only. Use clean fills, faint shadows, and restrained dimensionality. Avoid glossy 3D objects, playful mascots, or unrelated government-themed stock art.

# Variants and States

Use illustrations for:

- Splash/auth logo treatment.
- Onboarding/security prompts.
- Assistant entry points.
- Document identity cards.
- Home service shortcuts and story tiles.
- GovScan and service-list pictograms.

Keep forms, data review, notifications, warnings, payment, and document detail text information-led.

# Avoid

- Do not replace document cards with plain white rows.
- Do not use SF Symbols as document/service identities.
- Do not invent a new mascot or unrelated illustration family.
- Do not use decorative gradients as a substitute for authored assets.
- Do not let illustration obscure labels, dates, document numbers, warnings, or primary actions.
