# Overview

SmartMed brings clinic booking, online care, pharmacy shopping, health services, and a medical record into one account-centered app.

# Navigation

- Five primary destinations cover Home, Pharmacy, Appointments, Medical record, and Profile.
- Home combines search, shortcuts, service hubs, tests, packages, and health programs.
- Focused booking and purchase tasks use a linear drill-down with back navigation.

# Core Flows

## Book care

1. Open Appointments and choose clinic, online consultation, home visit, diagnostics, or tests.
2. Narrow by specialty, doctor, or clinic.
3. Select location and the available service context.

## Use a health service

1. Start from Home or a themed service hub.
2. Review a package, test, calendar, or activity tool.
3. Continue through its purchase or consultation action.

## Shop in the pharmacy

1. Browse catalog groups or search.
2. Open a product, favorite it, or add it to the cart.
3. Use the persistent cart summary to continue.

# Interaction Patterns

- carousels expose service families without leaving the current page.
- Teal consistently marks active navigation and primary actions.
- Lists use chevrons for drill-down; transactional actions stay explicit.
