# Overview

MTS Music is a, artwork-led listening service covering mixes, recommendations, releases, playlists, stations, podcasts, favorites, search, subscription, and playback.

# Navigation

Four primary destinations connect Listen, Favorites, Search, and Subscription. A persistent mini player sits above the primary navigation, while history and Profile remain in the top-right of Listen.

# Core Flows

## Discover and play music

1. Enter through a personalized mix, daily playlist, editorial collection, artist, or release.
2. Start playback from the collection or an individual track.
3. Continue in the full player with timeline, queue, repeat, shuffle, output, and sharing.

## Curate the library

1. Favorite tracks, artists, albums, and playlists from lists or playback.
2. Revisit them through Favorites or Search.
3. Adjust recommendations, network, equalizer, sleep timer, and storage in Profile.

# Interaction Patterns

- The mini player preserves listening context across navigation.
