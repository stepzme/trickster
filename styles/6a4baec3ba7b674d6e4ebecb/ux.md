# Overview

Globus combines loyalty and promotions with grocery discovery, catalog search, product detail, cart editing, checkout, delivery or pickup, and order tracking.

# Navigation

A five primary destinations anchors the main destinations. Home and Catalog keep selected store or fulfillment, search, and utility actions close to product discovery.

# Core Flows

## Discover and add products

1. Enter through search, catalog, category, promotion, or repeat purchase, inspect product details, then add or adjust quantity.

## Checkout and follow the order

1. Review cart availability and totals, choose fulfillment and slot, confirm address and payment, place the order, and track its state.

# Interaction Patterns

Promotions and products use browsing sections, quantity changes happen inline, catalog context persists near search, and checkout groups one fulfillment decision per section.
