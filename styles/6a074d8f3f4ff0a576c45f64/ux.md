# Overview

Pure centers anonymous dating on a playful discovery feed, mutual matches, time-sensitive interest signals, chat, and expressive profile customization.

# Navigation

A three-item bottom bar anchors discovery, chats, and profile. Discovery keeps one profile in focus while secondary premium and safety entry points sit around the edges.

# Core Flows

## Discover and match

1. Complete onboarding, enter the profile feed, review a single candidate, send interest or pass, see match feedback, then move into chat.

## Chat and profile

1. Review likes and active conversations, open chat, manage safety guidance, edit profile sections, choose a visual style, verify photos, and manage premium or privacy settings.

# Interaction Patterns

Gestural profile decisions stay visually central, temporary interest badges create urgency, the feed offers only a few strong actions, and torn-paper transitions preserve personality across discovery, chat, and profile.
