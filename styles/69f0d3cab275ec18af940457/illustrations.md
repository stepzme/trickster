# Overview

Mail.ru uses a sparse authored illustration system on splash, onboarding, and notification interstitials. Glossy white-blue mascot-like objects, a pale-blue cupcake, and black monoline hand-drawn accents create a friendly focal point without entering the operational inbox. This system is separate from attachment previews, photos, ads, campaign media, logos, service marks, functional icons, app icons, charts, and progress indicators.

# Visual Style

Create simplified glossy 3D mascot or object forms with rounded white and blue volumes, high-key surfaces, soft shadows, and gentle studio lighting. Pair them sparingly with pale-blue prop objects or loose black monoline hand annotations. Forms are clean and softly surreal rather than photorealistic; outlines belong only to the hand-drawn accent layer, not the volumetric mascot.

Every new illustration must be generated with an image-generation model in this language. It requires separate visual approval before interface integration and must then be integrated as the approved raster asset.

# Composition

Use one large centered or upper-screen object composition on a white or blue field, sometimes intentionally cropped at the top or side to feel close and dimensional. Preserve generous negative space for a short title, explanation, and blue action. The artwork can occupy a substantial part of the upper half, but it should not compete with registration fields, native permission alerts, or bottom actions.

# Color and Materials

Lead with white, Mail blue, pale blue, and soft gray. Use restrained glossy plastic-like material, smooth gradients, diffuse highlights, and soft grounding shadows. Black monoline accents may point, gesture, or frame a prop. Keep contrast high-key and clean; do not introduce unrelated saturated palettes or dark textured environments.

# Variants and States

- Splash or onboarding may show the largest mascot-like crop against a blue or white field.
- Notification education may pair the volumetric object or pale-blue cupcake with a black hand-drawn gesture and concise copy.
- Native permission presentation remains a system alert above the illustrated context rather than becoming an illustrated substitute.
- No stable illustrated empty, success, or error family was confirmed in the sampled evidence.

# Avoid

- Do not draw or approximate the artwork with SwiftUI shapes, Canvas, programmatic paths or gradients, SF Symbols, emoji, text glyphs, or assembled mail icons.
- Do not integrate an unapproved generation, placeholder, screenshot crop, or temporary vector; use the visually approved raster asset.
- Do not substitute attachment photos, advertising, campaign art, logos, service marks, app icons, charts, or progress bars.
- Do not use stock office illustrations, generic envelope cartoons, glossy emoji, dense 3D scenes, or realistic characters.
- Do not spread mascot art into message rows, reading content, compose fields, folders, or settings lists.
