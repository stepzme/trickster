# Overview

Teremok uses a stable authored hand-drawn character universe across onboarding, loyalty, coupons, promotions, achievements, order status, and help or identity moments. Pancake and food characters, badges, keys, and loose handwritten brand lettering form a recurring visual layer distinct from real menu photography. When a composition requires this layer, generate the artwork with the available image-generation model, obtain visual approval, and integrate the approved raster asset. Do not recreate it from SwiftUI shapes, SF Symbols, emoji, or programmatic drawings. The screen is not visually complete until the generated image is visible in the running app.

# Visual Style

Use warm hand-drawn cartoon art with thin slightly irregular black linework, simple dot eyes, expressive stick-like limbs, and flat or lightly textured color fills. Pancakes, blini, and other food forms become friendly characters carrying small props or taking part in compact everyday scenes. Supporting badges, keys, signs, and loose marker-like lettering share the same imperfect drawn quality. Keep the result charming and editorial rather than polished vector, 3D, or photoreal.

# Composition

Use one centered mascot, a compact two- or three-character scene, or a small badge-like vignette. Coupon and promotional tiles place the character beside short native text with clear negative space. Achievement art may use a circular emblem or centered badge. Wider banners can use a flat landscape-like scene, while modal and status art stays small and focused. Keep characters out of product photography and dense functional form fields. Native text, prices, and controls remain outside the generated image.

# Color and Materials

Anchor the palette in warm wheat, cream, burgundy red, yellow, peach, and black linework, with pale blue or muted secondary accents when the scene needs contrast. Backgrounds are flat white, cream, peach, or a restrained brand color. Use subtle paper or marker imperfection only if it preserves legibility at mobile scale. Avoid glossy gradients, plastic 3D material, neon colors, heavy shadows, photoreal texture, or unrelated rainbow palettes.

# Variants and States

- **Onboarding:** a larger welcoming character scene with generous space for native title and action.
- **Loyalty or coupon:** compact mascot or food vignette on peach, cream, yellow, or white cards beside concise value information.
- **Promotion:** a wider authored scene with multiple characters or props, still using the same line and palette.
- **Achievement:** circular badge, key, emblem, or single celebratory character with stronger yellow/wheat emphasis.
- **Order status or completion:** one small encouraging character or badge near the state summary, without interrupting operational details.
- **Help or identity:** restrained character or handwritten brand mark used as a supporting focal point.

Do not invent character variants for payment forms, ingredient selection, permissions, or errors unless a generated proposal is explicitly approved.

# Avoid

- SwiftUI shapes, Canvas drawings, programmatic vectors, SF Symbols, emoji, or stock mascots replacing generated artwork.
- Smooth corporate vector art, glossy 3D characters, anime rendering, photoreal food characters, or thick geometric outlines.
- Using character art instead of real food photography in menu/product cards.
- Handwritten text baked into functional prices, controls, legal copy, form labels, or navigation.
- Dense scenes that crowd native text, inconsistent character anatomy, or crops that remove the main gesture.
- Integrating the first generated result without visual review and explicit approval.
