# Overview

VK Dating is a photo-led dating product for onboarding, profile discovery, interest-based collections, likes, matches, chats, and personal profile management.

# Navigation

- Five bottom destinations cover Profiles, Collections, Likes, Chats, and Profile.
- Profiles presents one immersive card with filter access and explicit dislike, priority, and like actions.
- Collections groups candidates around interests, activity, online state, and campaigns.

# Core Flows

## Create a profile

1. Sign in with VK ID and add photos, identity, location, and intent.
2. Choose interests and complete profile prompts.
3. Preview the profile and enter discovery.

## Discover and like

1. Review the current profile's media, distance, activity, and bio.
2. Dislike, send a priority reaction, like, or open more details.
3. Continue until a mutual match opens messaging.

## Browse collections and chat

1. Open Collections and choose an interest or campaign.
2. Review the filtered profiles and react.
3. Open Chats to exchange messages with matches.

# Interaction Patterns

- Photography dominates profile cards while dark scrims protect text.
- Red, purple, and blue separate dislike, priority, and like.
- Collection cards use real photography and bright category labels.
- Profile completion and interest choice use explicit progress and multi-select chips.
