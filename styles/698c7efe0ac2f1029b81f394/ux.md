# Overview

bunq brings daily money, savings, cards, stocks, crypto, and lifestyle utilities into one modular banking shell.

# Navigation

Home, Cards, Savings, Stocks, and Crypto are persistent. Profile contains personal data, support, settings, accounting, eSIM, and benefits.

# Core Flows

## Fund and move money

1. Choose Pay, Request, or Add Money.
2. Select account and counterparty or funding rail.
3. Enter amount and optional schedule.
4. Review limits, fees, source, and destination.
5. Confirm with the secure code and show the result.

## Manage products

1. Accounts expose personalization, limits, and schedules. Cards expose number, CVC, daily limit, countries, statements, and protections. Savings shows expected and earned interest.

## Invest and administer

1. Stocks and crypto separate discovery, search, detail, and risk context. Profile groups support, accounting exports, identity, and account closure.

# Interaction Patterns

- Keep net wealth and account totals explicit.
- Show source account before amount.
- Separate investments from spendable money.
- Preserve scheduled-payment visibility.
