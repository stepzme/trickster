# Overview

Buy.am unifies restaurants, supermarkets, retail stores, pharmacy, services, and mall shopping with shared address, basket, checkout, and tracking behavior.

# Navigation

Home, Restaurants, Mall, Basket, and Profile remain stable. Search and filters narrow the current commerce context.

# Core Flows

## Discover and buy

1. Select a vertical, merchant, or product; compare rating, delivery time, fee, and price; then add to basket without losing the source merchant.

## Checkout and tracking

1. Review quantity and all fees.
2. Confirm delivery timing, address, and comment.
3. Apply gift card or promo.
4. Choose payment and confirm.
5. Follow the order timeline and open order detail.

# Interaction Patterns

- Keep address visible before discovery.
- Retain merchant context in basket.
- Show packaging and delivery separately.
- Support reorder from history.
- Confirm cancellation explicitly.
