# Role

Illustration makes crowns, challenges, campaigns, and rewards feel playful while food ordering remains product-led.

# Visual Language

- Flat editorial characters and chunky crown symbols.
- Warm red, orange, yellow, and brown with occasional mint.
- Heavy outlines, simple faces, oversized hands, and rounded props.
- Cream negative space and short bold headlines.

# Composition

Keep one compact character scene or crown symbol inside a rounded campaign card, with a protected headline and CTA zone.

# Usage

Use for loyalty, crown education, challenges, charity, campaigns, and reward states. Keep menu products, basket, payment, address, and order tracking photo- or data-led.

# Guardrails

- Do not replace food imagery with illustration.
- Do not place characters behind prices or totals.
- Preserve the warm brand palette from `ui.md`.
- For a new campaign, use one playful flat scene, chunky crown geometry, bold outline, and a simple warm color field.
