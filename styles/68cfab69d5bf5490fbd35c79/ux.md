# Overview

MyAmeria is a modular banking hub for cards and accounts, savings, loans, transfers, payments, applications, partner apps, exchange rates, QR payment, history, and profile documents.

# Navigation

Home, Services, a QR scanner, History, and Apps form the primary navigation. Home can be customized and combines product tabs, service shortcuts, partner apps, mobile transfers, and exchange rates.

# Core Flows

## Pay or transfer

1. Start from a favorite shortcut, Services, template, or QR scanner.
2. Choose transfer, payment, application, or scanned merchant.
3. Enter or verify destination, amount, and funding account.
4. Confirm and show success or a specific recoverable issue.

## Manage products and services

1. Switch among cards/accounts, MyPay, savings, and loans.
2. Open the product menu or browse Services by transfers, payments, and applications.
3. Complete the focused action or save it as a template.
4. Revisit activity through History.

# Interaction Patterns

- The customizable Home announces configuration without blocking current banking tasks.
