# Overview

My Rostelecom organizes household telecom around accounts, balance, autopay, bonuses, connected internet and TV, equipment, options, new-service connection, and settings.

# Navigation

Four primary destinations connect My Accounts, Bonuses, Connect, and Settings. The account dashboard leads with balance and payment actions before promotional stories and connected services.

# Core Flows

## Review and pay an account

1. Select the personal account.
2. Review balance, promised payment, autopay, and connected services.
3. Top up or configure a recurring payment.
4. Return to the dashboard with updated account status.

## Connect or change a service

1. Choose an address and browse available home phone, internet, mobile, TV, or package offers.
2. Compare tariff composition and monthly benefit.
3. Choose a prepared tariff or configure a package.
4. Confirm the request and follow its activation state.

# Interaction Patterns

The sampled flows use direct actions, explicit completion, and return to the current context. No additional repeated interaction behavior was documented.
