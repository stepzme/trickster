# Overview

Moby uses polished 3D brand imagery for the launch and selected promotional moments, pairing the M-shaped cyan-magenta object with space, glass, coins, and luminous trails.

# Visual Style

Use glossy translucent 3D forms, cool blue rim light, cyan-to-magenta cores, and realistic soft reflections. The mood is optimistic and technological rather than cartoonish.

# Composition

Center one branded object in generous space. Promotional illustrations may sit at the edge of a compact banner but must leave a clean text zone and preserve financial clarity.

# Color

Use deep navy for immersive scenes, electric blue and cyan for light, and magenta as a small brand highlight. Keep ordinary product UI on white and blue instead of extending the cosmic backdrop everywhere.

# Usage

Reserve illustration for launch, branded onboarding, product promotion, and meaningful empty or success states. Do not replace transactional icons, account data, or controls with decorative art.
