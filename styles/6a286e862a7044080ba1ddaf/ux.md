# Overview

Banco Plata joins credit, cash account, cards, payments, cashback, installments, savings, investment, referrals, and chat. The dashboard surfaces current obligations before acquisition.

# Navigation

Home, Pay, Invest, Invite, and Chat remain in a primary navigation. Product details use back navigation while retaining quick actions.

# Core Flows

## Home and products

1. Home shows cashback, installment opportunities, transaction summary, credit availability, due state, cash account, and new products in descending urgency.

## Pay and transfer

1. Choose deposit, transfer, favorite, or bill category.
2. Select CLABE, own accounts, card number, or Plata phone.
3. Enter details, review, confirm, and return to transactions.

## Card and account control

1. Card detail supports freeze, reissue, discard, PIN, online purchase, ATM, statements, and repayment. Account detail supports deposits, bill pay, salary transfer, and linked cards.

## Savings and acquisition

1. Deposits, virtual or physical cards, Plata+, and investment remain separate product lifecycles with explicit rates and conditions.

# Interaction Patterns

- Show due state before promotional content.
- Separate credit from own funds.
- Keep card security controls explicit.
- Confirm irreversible product closure.

# System Access Timing

No system-access timing or denial-recovery behavior was documented in the reviewed source.
