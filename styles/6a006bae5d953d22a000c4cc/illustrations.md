# Overview

ONAY uses a standalone polished 3D/vector hybrid illustration system in onboarding and password-recovery or security heroes. It explains mobility, linked cards, routes, QR payment, auto top-up, and protection through product-like objects. It is separate from shop photography, maps, payment and brand marks, functional yellow icons, charts, campaign banners, and decorative glows.

# Visual Style

Create rounded cards, phone mockups, route-grid tiles, QR panels, shield, lock, passcode, map-pin, and transit objects with minimal or no outline. Forms are defined by soft fills, translucent panels, smooth plastic or glass material, blur/glow, gentle shadows, and diffuse frontal/top light. The result sits between clean 3D rendering and simplified vector geometry rather than photorealism.

Every new illustration must be generated with an image-generation model in this language. It requires separate visual approval before interface integration and must then be integrated as the approved raster asset.

# Composition

Use one large hero object or tightly layered object group in the upper half or center, followed by concise copy and bottom-anchored controls. Allow foreground cards or panels to overlap and selectively crop background objects while keeping the main concept legible. Preserve generous white or lavender negative space and avoid placing critical art behind text, inputs, or the raised QR navigation.

# Color and Materials

Anchor the system with ONAY yellow, then use controlled lavender, cyan, pink, green, blue, white, and soft gray to distinguish object roles. Backgrounds often use a lavender-to-white glow; security may introduce stronger blue. Keep materials smooth, lightly translucent, and softly lit, with rounded edges and restrained drop shadows. Avoid coarse texture, hard black outlines, realistic metal, or dramatic environmental light.

# Variants and States

- Onboarding may feature a city/map pin, family card stack, favorite-route grid, transit ticket, purchase, auto top-up, or QR payment composition.
- Password recovery and security use shield, lock, passcode, phone, or biometric-related objects with a clearer protective focus.
- Layered cards demonstrate relationship or hierarchy; a hand or device appears only when it directly clarifies interaction.
- No stable authored error or success family was confirmed beyond operational confirmation UI.

# Avoid

- Do not draw or approximate the art with SwiftUI shapes, Canvas, programmatic paths or gradients, SF Symbols, emoji, text glyphs, or assembled transit icons.
- Do not integrate an unapproved generation, placeholder, screenshot crop, or temporary vector; use the visually approved raster asset.
- Do not substitute maps, shop product photos, payment marks, logos, campaign banners, charts, or functional yellow outline icons.
- Do not use stock transit cartoons, photorealistic vehicles, glossy emoji, dense isometric cities, or unrelated character mascots.
- Do not spread the illustration material into ordinary history rows, forms, route grids, or settings controls.
