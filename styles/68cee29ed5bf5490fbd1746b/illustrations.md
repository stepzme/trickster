# Overview

Detsky Mir uses a friendly blue bear mascot and toy-like family graphics to explain savings, loyalty, resale, gifts, and notification benefits without making commerce feel childish.

# Visual Style

Use rounded 2D or softly dimensional characters, oversized simple props, bright flat blue fields, coral and yellow accents, minimal outlines, and cheerful facial expressions. The bear remains the primary recurring character.

# Composition

Place one clear character or benefit object inside a wide rounded banner. Keep copy on a clean side of the frame and avoid mixing mascot scenes into dense product grids.

# Color and Materials

Anchor scenes in brand blue and pale sky blue, with controlled coral-red, yellow, purple, and white accents. Maintain enough contrast for black or white campaign copy.

# Variants and States

Use illustrations for onboarding, loyalty education, notification prompts, family benefits, resale, certificates, and empty states. For new topics, extend the same friendly mascot world so the result remains harmonious with `ui.md`.

# Production Requirements

Preserve the documented placement, crop, negative space, and visual weight in production exports. Before final art exists, placeholders must preserve those same compositional properties.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the observed imagery language with unrelated stock art or arbitrary symbols.
- Do not change the documented crop, density, or relationship to nearby text.
