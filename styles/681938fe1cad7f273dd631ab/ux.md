# Overview

Ozon is a broad marketplace for discovery, search, product comparison, favorites, cart, installment offers, checkout, pickup delivery, order tracking, and profile services.

# Navigation

Six primary destinations connect Home, discovery or catalog tools, finance, messages or notifications, Cart, and Profile. Search stays on Home, results, and detail.

# Core Flows

## Find and evaluate a product

1. Enter through campaign, category, recommendation, or search.
2. Refine suggestions and compare result cards.
3. Open product detail for media, price, sale, bonus, delivery, seller, and reviews.
4. Favorite, choose variant, or add to cart.

## Complete an order

1. Review selected items, quantity, sale deadline, seller bonuses, and total.
2. Confirm pickup or delivery, recipient, payment, promo code, and installment conditions.
3. Pay online and show a clear order confirmation.
4. Track preparation and delivery from Profile.
