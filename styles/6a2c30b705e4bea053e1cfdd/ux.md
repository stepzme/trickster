# Overview

MegaPay combines mobile balance, tariff and data management, payments, transfers, QR, services, eSIM, transaction history, and partner tools.

# Navigation

Five fixed destinations connect Payments, Mobile, central QR, Services, and History. Mobile starts with account balance and data; payment completion returns through one clear close action.

# Core Flows

## Manage connectivity

1. Review phone balance, tariff, renewal, and remaining data.
2. Open tariffs, services, family, or eSIM.
3. Change or add an option and confirm cost.
4. Review the result in balance or history.

## Make a payment

1. Choose a service, recipient, template, or QR route.
2. Enter amount and funding source.
3. Review fee and recipient.
4. Confirm, then repeat, save as template, or open the receipt.

# Interaction Patterns

- Dark navy anchors confirmation and navigation; green and violet gradients differentiate telecom and wallet modules.
- Large balance cards keep amounts prominent.
- Success uses a restrained mint glow and structured receipt.
- Colorful 3D service imagery stays inside promotional tiles.
