# Overview

setka uses glossy violet network illustrations during onboarding and identity moments. Human silhouettes, planets, and circular nodes communicate career connections against a pure black field.

# Visual Style

- Dark 3D silhouettes with purple and electric-violet rim lighting.
- Glossy circular nodes connected by fine luminous lines.
- Sparse scenes with deep black negative space.
- Occasional recognizable employer or platform marks used only as small connection labels.

# Composition

- Build around one central person node and a few smaller connected nodes.
- Keep the network asymmetric but visually balanced.
- Leave large black areas for short white copy and progress controls.
- Use shallow depth and glow instead of a detailed environment.

# Color and Materials

- Use black as the dominant field.
- Lead with violet and purple light.
- Keep white highlights sharp and limited.
- Reserve red, blue, or other colors for tiny contextual connection badges.

# Variants and States

- Use in onboarding, network discovery, and major empty identity states.
- Do not place glossy network art inside ordinary feed or chat cards.
- Keep the subject directly related to connections, careers, or communities.
- Create original characters and node arrangements rather than copying existing assets.

# Production Requirements

Preserve the documented placement, crop, negative space, and visual weight in production exports. Before final art exists, placeholders must preserve those same compositional properties.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the observed imagery language with unrelated stock art or arbitrary symbols.
- Do not change the documented crop, density, or relationship to nearby text.
