# Overview

A dense health-commerce catalog that combines shopping, educational content, product evidence, reviews, cart controls, and international checkout.

# Navigation

Five primary destinations separate Home, Catalog, Categories, Cart, and Account. Search remains available throughout catalog and product contexts. Product pages use secondary tabs for overview, details, reviews, and learning.

# Core Flows

## Discover a product

1. Browse recommendations, health concerns, trends, or categories.
2. Apply quick filters, availability, promotion, sorting, or detailed filters.
3. Open a product for claims, rating, price, variants, and related items.

## Evaluate a product

1. Switch among overview, details, reviews, and learning tabs.
2. Open long-form supplement information in a modal.
3. Review photos, questions, keywords, and rating evidence.

## Checkout

1. Adjust quantity, promo code, and saved-for-later state in Cart.
2. Continue to checkout and select delivery.
3. Enter required customs data.
4. Review order information and completion feedback.

# Interaction Patterns

- Filter chips scroll horizontally.
- Actions keep quantity and purchase controls reachable.
