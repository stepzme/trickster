# Overview

Pi uses richly colored editorial illustrations to make prompts and self-reflection topics feel warm, imaginative, and human.

# Visual Style

Scenes combine hand-painted texture, simplified people and objects, saturated botanical greens, coral, orange, yellow, teal, and deep shadow. The result feels like contemporary magazine illustration rather than software iconography.

# Composition

Illustrations fill rounded portrait or landscape cards, often leaving a darker or calmer zone for large white serif titles. A tiny flower emblem provides a lighter conversational motif.

# Color and Materials

Use warm saturated color against charcoal: forest and mint greens, burnt orange, coral, mustard, sky blue, and occasional electric yellow.

# Variants and States

Use the language for Discover prompts, reflective exercises, and editorial briefs. Keep the main chat and call surfaces mostly typographic so the artwork does not compete with conversation.

# Avoid

Do not introduce an unrelated illustration style or substitute decorative symbols for source-supported imagery.
