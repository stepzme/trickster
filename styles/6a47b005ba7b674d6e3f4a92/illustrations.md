# Overview

Dixy uses a cheerful orange cat mascot, gift props, food still lifes, and glossy promotional objects to make loyalty and delivery feel warm and energetic.

# Visual Style

Use softly rendered 3D characters and objects, rounded proportions, clean white backgrounds, bright orange and lime accents, and playful food-scale exaggeration. The cat stays expressive but never overly detailed.

# Composition

Place the mascot at the lower edge or side of a product module so it guides rather than blocks content. Build category art as compact object clusters with strong silhouettes and ample white space.

# Color and Materials

Anchor art in orange, cream, white, and lime, with small purple or yellow campaign accents. Keep character contrast high against both white and orange surfaces.

# Variants and States

Use this language for loyalty, delivery, promo codes, empty states, seasonal hubs, and campaign banners. New illustration topics should extend the same mascot-and-still-life world so they remain harmonious with `ui.md`.

# Production Requirements

Exact export formats, aspect ratios, animation behavior, and appearance variants were not documented. Where imagery is compositionally important, temporary placeholders must preserve its documented scale, placement, crop, and visual weight until final art exists.

# Avoid

Do not introduce an unrelated illustration style or substitute decorative symbols for source-supported imagery.
