# Planning

## Goal

Turn the approved product description into the smallest useful design MVP and an ordered Full Scope implementation sequence without changing the product.

## Owner and input

The Product Researcher verifies explicit Research approval and reads only the approved Research artifact, existing project constraints, and `trickster/templates/plan.md`.

## Output

Create `trickster/artifacts/<run-id>/plan.md` with:

- MVP screens and flows sufficient to judge the product's real visual direction in Simulator;
- representative content and states needed for design approval;
- ordered Full Scope blocks, each large enough to be meaningful and small enough to review independently;
- the capabilities exercised in each block;
- a simple completion check for every block.

The MVP is not a throwaway prototype. It is the beginning of the production app and must include real navigation and enough real interaction to judge the design. Do not include the whole app merely to avoid making a product choice.

## Approval

The master presents the plan and records explicit user approval. Designer may research references before this approval, but cannot implement the MVP until the approved plan exists.
