# Overview

Optima24 is a dark banking super app for cards, accounts, transfers, payments, deposits, loans, government services, travel, partner services, and merchant products.

# Navigation

Home, Operations, a raised QR scanner, Services, and Menu form the bottom dock. Home combines stories, the selected card, a service shortcut grid, campaigns, and product tiles.

# Core Flows

## Pay or transfer

1. Choose a card or account context.
2. Enter Transfers, Payments, QR, or a saved service.
3. Select destination and amount, then verify commission and source.
4. Confirm and show the resulting balance or receipt.

## Manage bank products

1. Open My Bank to review cards, accounts, loans, installments, and deposits.
2. Enter a product for balance, details, history, or settings.
3. Add or apply for a missing product.
4. Return to the product group with updated status.

# Interaction Patterns

- Near-black surfaces and red line icons frame dense service and product content.
- Gold marks the selected card and milestone campaign; orange anchors QR.
- Home is promotional and modular, while My Bank becomes calmer and list-based.
- Payment amount remains large and close to source, recipient, fee, and confirmation.
