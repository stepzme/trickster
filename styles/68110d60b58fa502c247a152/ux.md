# Overview

Vkusno I Tochka combines restaurant loyalty, promotions, menu browsing, mobile ordering, restaurant selection, QR redemption, and account support.

# Navigation

- Five primary destinations cover Home, Promotions, Menu, Map, and More.
- Home summarizes bonus balance, campaigns, referral, and mobile-order entry points.
- Menu switches pickup and delivery, location, categories, product items, and cart.

# Core Flows

## Use a promotion

1. Browse special or active promotions.
2. Open a card and confirm location, eligibility, and conditions.
3. Present the QR code in restaurant or continue to mobile order.

## Build a mobile order

1. Choose pickup or delivery and confirm the restaurant.
2. Browse menu categories and add products.
3. Review cart, customize fulfillment, apply benefits, and pay.

## Earn and spend bonuses

1. Open Home and review current bonus balance.
2. Scan the loyalty QR code when purchasing.
3. Choose an eligible reward or promotion to spend bonuses.

# Interaction Patterns

- Loyalty actions are grouped into earn and spend.
