# Overview

Home Credit Bank uses small dimensional objects to distinguish payment categories and lightweight financial utilities.

# Visual Style

Objects are soft 3D miniatures with rounded geometry, matte materials, and recognizable silhouettes such as a house, gavel, plane, game controller, graduation cap, heart, or globe.

# Composition

Place one object near the lower or central area of a compact category cell, leaving room for a short label. Keep object scale consistent across the grid.

# Color and Materials

Use varied but muted orange, blue, mint, violet, gray, and red accents on white or pale gray, while preserving red-magenta for transaction actions.

# Variants and States

Use illustrations for payment catalogs, category navigation, onboarding, and education. Keep balances, transfers, card detail, confirmation, and analytics data-led.

# Avoid

Do not introduce an unrelated illustration style or substitute decorative symbols for source-supported imagery.
