# Overview

Tinkoff Investments combines portfolio monitoring, security discovery, charts, order book, social Pulse, analytics, screening, and trading.

# Navigation

- Five bottom destinations cover Home, What to buy, Pulse, Chat, and More.
- Home combines portfolio, operations, funding, analytics, and positions.
- Security pages use tabs for Overview, Order book, Pulse, Indicators, and News.

# Core Flows

## Research a security

1. Search, use favorites, a screener, calendar, strategy, or market ranking.
2. Open a security and inspect price, chart, liquidity, indicators, order book, and discussion.
3. Add alerts or continue to Buy or Sell.

## Trade

1. Choose Buy or Sell from a security.
2. Select account, order type, lots, and price conditions.
3. Review commission and total, then confirm.

## Review the portfolio

1. Open Home or Analytics.
2. Switch portfolio, balance, and forecast views.
3. Inspect performance, income, funding, turnover, and open positions.

# Interaction Patterns

- Black surfaces and white type prioritize market data.
- Green and red encode movement; blue marks actions and selected tabs.
- Charts are full-width and time-range controls remain near the data.
- Regulatory and liquidity warnings appear before trading commitment.
