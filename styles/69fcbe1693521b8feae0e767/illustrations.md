# Overview

FocusPomo has a stable authored mascot system built around expressive tomato characters and warm reward objects. The characters turn elapsed sessions and progress into a visible collectible world across splash, timer, completion, statistics, and promotional contexts.

# Visual Style

Generate all new illustrations with an image-generation model. Use soft rounded cartoon tomatoes with tiny faces and limbs, small green leaves, simplified trophy or reward objects, gentle dimensional shading, and an approachable toy-like finish. Forms are clean and compact rather than intricately outlined; expressions are minimal but readable. Maintain consistent character anatomy, face placement, and leaf treatment.

Every generated image must be visually reviewed and explicitly approved before integration. Integrate only the approved output as a raster asset. Do not recreate or replace it with SwiftUI shapes, `Canvas`, programmatic vectors or paths, SF Symbols, emoji, icon-font glyphs, or other code-generated substitutes.

# Composition

Use one centered character for splash or a focused reward, a loose pile of small characters along the lower timer field, and tidy repeated marks or clusters for accumulated progress. A hero mascot or trophy may occupy roughly a quarter of the viewport or most of a promotional card, while timer numerals remain unobstructed. Keep enough cream negative space for time, metrics, and actions; preserve full faces and recognizable leaf silhouettes when cropping.

# Color and Materials

Use tomato orange-red, coral, leaf green, cream, warm white, muted taupe, and reward yellow, with restrained pastel chart colors when art meets analytics. Shading is soft and rounded, with low-contrast highlights and diffuse shadows. Avoid cold metallic materials, hard black outlines, photoreal fruit texture, or colors disconnected from `ui.md`.

# Variants and States

Splash and onboarding use a single welcoming mascot or device composition. Active timer screens use a calm pile or small group below the numerals. Completion and reward states may add a trophy, brighter yellow, or a more celebratory expression. Statistics reuse small tomatoes as count/progress marks; promotional states can enlarge the mascot while retaining the same construction. Product mockups remain separate from the authored mascot system.

# Avoid

- Do not use unrelated fruit, generic stock vectors, corporate characters, anime treatment, or photoreal tomatoes.
- Do not substitute the approved art with SwiftUI shapes, `Canvas`, programmatic paths, SF Symbols, emoji, or icon-font glyphs.
- Do not integrate model output before visual approval.
- Do not cover timer numerals, actions, chart labels, or form controls with characters.
- Do not crop faces or leaves, or make dense crowds illegible at phone size.
- Do not change anatomy, face placement, shading style, or palette between states.
