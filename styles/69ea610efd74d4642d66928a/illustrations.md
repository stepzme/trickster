# Overview

Pillowtalk uses a coherent authored illustration language for onboarding questions, processing, paywall or locked moments, confirmations, and pattern surfaces. These symbolic compositions are separate from lifestyle photography, blurred content cards, the three-lobed brand mark, functional icons, charts, and decorative background glow.

# Visual Style

Build flat vector-like images from soft biomorphic blobs, pill-shaped lobes, scalloped clouds, bulb-ended stems, simple face or eye masks, and balanced ornamental forms. Most shapes have no outline; identity comes from filled silhouettes, sparse cutouts, bold scale, and symmetrical or gently mirrored arrangement. Keep the material flat and graphic, with no conventional rendered lighting or texture.

Every new illustration must be generated with an image-generation model using this vocabulary. The generated image requires separate visual approval before interface integration and must then be integrated as the approved raster asset.

# Composition

Illustrations are large and central rather than icon-sized. On onboarding screens, place one symbolic composition between the headline and bottom controls with ample black negative space. On locked, success, or paywall screens, allow the art to become the primary visual mass. Pattern variants may form wide banners and intentionally crop at card or screen edges. Preserve a single clear focal point and keep essential facial or symbolic cutouts away from unsafe crops and overlay text.

# Color and Materials

Acid lime is the core color against near-black, with controlled violet, lavender, orange, peach, olive, white, and off-white variants. Use flat opaque fills and high contrast. Depth should come from overlap, scale, and cutout shapes, not realistic highlights, bevels, glass, fabric, grain, or 3D shading. When art sits on an off-white or saturated panel, invert or adjust the palette while retaining the same organic geometry.

# Variants and States

- Onboarding may use masks, symbolic faces or eyes, stems, speech-cloud forms, cake, stars, or identity-related symbols as one central response to the prompt.
- Loading and listening may use a compact three-lobed or related organic form with restrained glow or motion supplied by the interface.
- Locked, subscription, confirmation, and success moments may enlarge a seal, check, star, or celebratory organic composition.
- Pattern surfaces may use wide cropped blob banners or symmetrical ornaments behind a small amount of data or copy.
- No explicit illustrated error variant was observed; do not invent one from this source.

# Avoid

- Do not draw or approximate these images with SwiftUI shapes, Canvas, programmatic paths or gradients, SF Symbols, emoji, text glyphs, or assembled UI icons.
- Do not integrate an unapproved generation, temporary vector, screenshot crop, or placeholder; integrate the visually approved raster asset.
- Do not substitute lifestyle photography, blurred photo cards, the brand logo alone, charts, or monochrome functional icons for authored illustration.
- Do not use stock character packs, thin-line icon scenes, realistic 3D, glossy clay, painterly texture, or detailed environments.
- Do not reduce the art to a small badge when the reference gives it central or banner-scale visual weight.
