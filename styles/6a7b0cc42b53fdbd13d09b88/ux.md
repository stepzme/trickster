# Overview

Russian Post brings tracking, sending, pickup, office search, queue booking, help, jobs, bonuses, and profile settings into one utility-oriented app.

# Navigation

- Five primary destinations separate the main service areas.
- Home exposes tracking, office, sending, courier, and account shortcuts.
- Deep operational tasks use a linear stack with back navigation and a persistent confirmation action.

# Core Flows

## Send a parcel

1. Choose the sending service.
2. Enter sender, recipient, and address details.
3. Specify parcel size and compare available rates.
4. Add optional services, review the summary, and pay.

## Track and receive

1. Open My Mail or enter a tracking number from Home.
2. Review the current state and event history.
3. Choose pickup, QR presentation, or courier options when available.
4. Rate or archive the completed delivery.

## Find help or an office

1. Open an office list or map, or enter the help area.
2. Filter by location or service need.
3. Review hours, queue availability, FAQ, or contact options.

# Interaction Patterns

- Search, filter pills, and shortcuts reduce the distance to frequent tasks.
- Tracking and pickup preserve parcel identity while changing the available action.
- Promotional tiles stay secondary to operational information.
