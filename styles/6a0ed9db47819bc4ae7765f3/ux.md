# Overview

kolesa.kz is a vehicle marketplace for cars, motorcycles, parts, services, financing, vehicle history, seller contact, and listing management.

# Navigation

Five primary destinations cover Listings, Favorites, Post, Messages, and Profile. Home exposes vehicle categories before a live listing feed; filters and sorting remain in the listing context.

# Core Flows

## Find a vehicle

1. Choose a vehicle category from Home.
2. Browse results with media, price, financing, and key specs.
3. Filter, sort, or subscribe to search updates.

## Evaluate a listing

1. Open the listing and inspect the image gallery and specifications.
2. Review price context, vehicle history, seller description, and reviews.
3. Save, message, or call the seller from persistent actions.

## Post an ad

1. Start from the central Post action.
2. Choose listing type and supply vehicle details.
3. Add media and contact information.
4. Publish and manage the listing from Profile.

# Interaction Patterns

- Filters use staged screens rather than one overloaded sheet.
- Contact actions stay fixed near the safe area on long details.
