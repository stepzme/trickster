# Overview

Moonly combines a personalized lunar calendar with astrology, affirmations, tarot, runes, healing, and learning. The experience feels exploratory but keeps five stable destinations close at hand.

# Navigation

A dark translucent bottom bar links Calendar, Affirmations, Practice, Healing, and Learning. The avatar opens account tools; large editorial cards and compact shortcuts move into specific readings.

# Core Flows

## Personalize and enter the calendar

1. Introduce the lunar premise and collect birth details, place, interests, and intent.
2. Show progress throughout the short questionnaire.
3. Land on the current day with moon phase, personal guidance, and recommended practices.

## Explore a reading or practice

1. Choose a horoscope, tarot layout, rune, affirmation, or learning module.
2. Present the focused ritual with minimal competing navigation.
3. Reveal the interpretation progressively and offer the next related practice.

# Interaction Patterns

- Large visual cards act as destinations, with small category labels and strong editorial titles.
- Locked content remains visible with subdued copy and a clear upgrade cue.
- Segments switch tarot modes and layouts without leaving Practice.
- Personalized or time-sensitive content stays close to the moon strip at the top of Calendar.
