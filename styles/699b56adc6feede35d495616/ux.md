# Overview

MBANK combines accounts, cards, payments, transfers, financial analysis, rewards, partner services, tickets, travel, marketplace, lending, deposits, and junior banking.

# Navigation

Five fixed destinations connect Home, Payments, central QR, Services, and More. Home switches between marketplace and bank contexts while preserving identity and notifications.

# Core Flows

## Review and move money

1. Open balances and cards from Home or My Bank.
2. Inspect financial analysis or a product detail.
3. Choose a transfer or payment route.
4. Confirm recipient, amount, funding source, and result.

## Use rewards and services

1. Open bonuses, tasks, partner earning rules, or a service category.
2. Browse promotional stories, tickets, travel, insurance, government, or lifestyle services.
3. Enter the selected service while retaining MBANK navigation context.
4. Return to Home or payment history.

# Interaction Patterns

- Green anchors banking actions; yellow marks the central QR and brand hub.
- White rounded cards organize a dense modular dashboard.
- Multicolor service icons and campaign tiles differentiate product families.
- Financial lists keep amounts aligned and descriptive metadata compact.
