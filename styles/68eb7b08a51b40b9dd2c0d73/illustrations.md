# Overview

Kinopoisk uses polished 3D Yandex Plus objects only in account onboarding; ongoing media discovery is poster- and photography-led.

# Visual Style

Glossy beveled symbols, coins, and soft figurative objects rendered with high contrast on black. Surfaces combine metallic gold, white ceramic, and saturated gradient glass.

# Composition

Arrange objects around a central account avatar, leaving the lower center clear for the message and CTA. Use asymmetrical edge cropping to suggest motion beyond the frame.

# Color and Materials

Use the Yandex Plus spectrum from coral and magenta through violet and blue, balanced by gold, white, and deep black.

# Variants and States

Limit this style to Plus membership, account confirmation, and premium benefit moments. New illustrations must follow the dark canvas and branded gradient materials without entering ordinary film catalogs.

# Production Requirements

Preserve the documented placement, crop, negative space, and visual weight in production exports. Before final art exists, placeholders must preserve those same compositional properties.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the observed imagery language with unrelated stock art or arbitrary symbols.
- Do not change the documented crop, density, or relationship to nearby text.
