# Overview

Trainline organizes international and local rail booking around a persistent search entry, legible timetable comparison, and a ticket area that remains useful before and after purchase.

# Navigation

A three-item bottom bar anchors Search, My Tickets, and Account. Search opens a focused ticket form; purchase steps replace the bar with a single sticky continuation action.

# Core Flows

## Find and compare a train

1. Set origin, destination, date or return, passengers, and railcards.
2. Compare departures by time, duration, changes, operator, and price.
3. Open stops or fare detail before choosing an option.

## Configure and buy

1. Choose class and flexibility.
2. Add return, seating, extras, passenger data, and delivery method where offered.
3. Review insurance, discounts, fees, and total before payment.

## Use or manage a ticket

1. Open My Tickets for upcoming and example ticket states.
2. View ticket detail, receipt, or refund controls.
3. Manage payment methods, railcards, currency, privacy, and support from Account.

# Interaction Patterns

- Origin and destination use a focused sheet with suggestions and saved Home or Work stations.
- Date, time, class, seating, and fare conditions stay editable in dedicated sheets or steps.
- Result rows keep departure, arrival, duration, changes, operator, and price visible together.
- Bottom actions remain full width or right aligned and repeat the current total near commitment.
- Empty My Tickets teaches the ticket format and provides a direct Plan a trip action.
