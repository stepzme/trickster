# Overview

Tinkoff Investments uses sparse graphite-and-yellow 3D illustrations for empty states and product education.

# Visual Style

Simple platform-like objects use matte graphite, white edges, and bright yellow markers. Forms are geometric, chunky, and softly lit on black.

# Composition

Center a single object on a dark field with generous empty space. Keep explanatory text beneath the object.

# Color and Materials

Use graphite, white, and Tinkoff yellow. Small blue or green accents may clarify context but should remain secondary.

# Variants and States

Use for empty analytics, onboarding, qualification, and explanatory states. Keep quotes, charts, orders, and portfolio screens data-first.

# Production Requirements

Preserve the documented placement, crop, negative space, and visual weight in production exports. Before final art exists, placeholders must preserve those same compositional properties.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the observed imagery language with unrelated stock art or arbitrary symbols.
- Do not change the documented crop, density, or relationship to nearby text.

# Known Gaps

Animation, export dimensions, safe-crop specifications, appearance variants, and unshown state treatments were not documented.
