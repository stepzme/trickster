# Overview

Freedom uses polished 3D financial props and compact campaign scenes to soften banking offers and guided applications.

# Visual Style

Use clean toy-like renders with soft studio lighting, realistic materials, and restrained emerald, teal, silver, and warm accents.

# Composition

Place one clear object group inside a rounded banner or above a sheet, leaving a quiet field for short copy and actions.

# Color and Materials

Anchor imagery in emerald and teal; use white, metallic gray, and one warm highlight for contrast.

# Variants and States

Use illustrations for offers, benefits, onboarding, and explanatory steps. Extend this 3D language for new states so it remains harmonious with `ui.md`.

# Production Requirements

Use only the formats and crops supported by the implemented placement. Where the documented imagery is compositionally important, temporary placeholders must preserve its placement, crop, scale, negative space, and approximate visual weight until final art exists. No unobserved light/dark variants or export ratios are prescribed.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the documented image language with unrelated stock art, arbitrary symbols, or a denser composition.

# Known Gaps

- Export aspect ratios, safe crop measurements, animation, and unobserved state variants were not documented.
