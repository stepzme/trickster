# Overview

WB Chat is a restrained messenger for contacts, private conversations, groups, channels, folders, and privacy settings.

# Navigation

- A three-item bottom dock switches between Contacts, Chats, and Settings/Profile.
- Chats can be filtered by All, Chats, Groups, and Channels before opening a thread.
- Thread-level menus contain search, notifications, participant details, moderation, and destructive actions.

# Core Flows

## Start a private conversation

1. Search contacts or compose a new message.
2. Open a person, send text, files, media, or voice, and use reply, copy, pin, forward, reaction, or delete actions.
3. Search inside the conversation or change notification and block settings from the profile menu.

## Create a group or channel

1. Choose the creation action and select members.
2. Name the space, send content, and inspect its details.
3. Manage invite links, administrators, history, subscribers, blacklist, or deletion.

## Configure the account

1. Open Profile to set status, photo, username, and details.
2. Organize folders and notification or privacy settings.
3. Switch theme, change wallpaper, manage contacts, or sign out.

# Interaction Patterns

- Search and compose stay at the top; active conversation categories use a thin underline.
- Empty states pair a direct explanation with one wide dark action.
- Light and dark themes preserve the same monochrome structure; avatar color supplies most identity.
