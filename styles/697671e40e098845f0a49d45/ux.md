# Overview

t2 combines mobile-account management, tariff controls, security, data exchange, subscriptions, home services, finance, and partner offers.

# Navigation

- Five primary destinations cover Connectivity, MiXX, Home, Finance, and More.
- The main account screen combines number, balance, allowances, tariff, and service entry points.
- Focused tasks use drill-down screens with persistent context in contextual controls.

# Core Flows

## Manage mobile service

1. Open the account and review balance, data, minutes, SMS, and tariff.
2. Top up, configure autopayment, exchange or share data, or change services.
3. Confirm the selected operation in its focused flow.

## Use an ecosystem service

1. Open MiXX, Home, Finance, or More.
2. Select subscription, banking, insurance, shopping, entertainment, or home connectivity.
3. Review terms and continue through the contextual action.

## Protect and support the number

1. Open Safewall, voice assistant, eSIM, roaming, or number settings.
2. Review status and options.
3. Activate, configure, report, or disable through explicit actions.

# Interaction Patterns

- Dense modules use bold condensed headings and clear chevrons.
- Playful 3D objects explain services without replacing prices or terms.
