# Overview

Numo uses bold editorial graphics for learning collections and onboarding: saturated texture fields, oversized condensed lettering, arrows, simple symbols, cutout photography, and irregular collage shapes.

# Visual Style

Mix screen-print grain, soft airbrushed gradients, paper-like texture, hand-drawn arrows, and tightly cropped photos. The result should feel like energetic independent publishing, not polished corporate 3D.

# Composition

Use one oversized word or symbol as the cover anchor. Allow type to crop at the tile edge, and combine circles, wedges, or cutout portraits into assertive vertical story scenes.

# Color

Work on black with electric blue, hot pink, orange-red, yellow, cyan, and emerald. Each tile may choose one dominant saturated pair; keep white type highly legible.

# Usage

Use this language for Hack collections, story covers, onboarding motivation, streak celebration, and community campaigns. When illustration is needed elsewhere, inherit the black UI, condensed typography, and high-energy collage language.
