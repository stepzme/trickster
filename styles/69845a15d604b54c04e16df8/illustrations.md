# Overview

SmartMed uses a soft medical illustration language to make clinical tasks feel approachable.

# Visual Style

Glossy clay-like 3D objects combine with translucent aqua, lavender, and pale-blue materials. Friendly service characters and simplified medical objects use rounded silhouettes and gentle highlights.

# Composition

Keep one centered object or a small object cluster inside a pastel card. Leave generous negative space and avoid detailed backgrounds.

# Color and Materials

Anchor illustrations in teal and aqua, supported by lavender and icy blue. Small white highlights and restrained coral details may clarify medical meaning.

# Variants and States

Use illustrations for service hubs, health programs, empty guidance, and supportive prompts. Keep pharmacy product imagery photographic and never let decorative objects obscure prices or booking controls.

# Production Requirements

Use only the formats and crops supported by the implemented placement. Where the documented imagery is compositionally important, temporary placeholders must preserve its placement, crop, scale, negative space, and approximate visual weight until final art exists. No unobserved light/dark variants or export ratios are prescribed.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the documented image language with unrelated stock art, arbitrary symbols, or a denser composition.

# Known Gaps

- Export aspect ratios, safe crop measurements, animation, and unobserved state variants were not documented.
