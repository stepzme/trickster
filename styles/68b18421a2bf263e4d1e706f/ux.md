# Overview

Tinkoff Journal presents editorial feeds, topic catalogs, financial courses, interactive lessons, calculators, and account settings.

# Navigation

- The available catalog has no recorded flow sequences; this description is based on all 34 inspected image screens.
- A three-part primary navigation covers Textbook, Journal, and Calculators.
- Topic pages open article feeds; course items open lesson content and quizzes.

# Core Flows

## Read the journal

1. Choose Journal or a topic visible in the category catalog.
2. Scan image-led article cards and open an article.
3. Read or continue through related editorial content.

## Complete a course

1. Open Textbook and select a course.
2. Move through illustrated lessons and interactive questions.
3. Review the result and rate the lesson.

## Configure the app

1. Open the account or settings surface visible in the catalog.
2. Manage notifications, report a problem, view app information, or delete the account.
