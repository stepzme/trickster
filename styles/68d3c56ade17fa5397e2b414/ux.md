# Overview

Telcell Wallet combines balance, cards, payments, transport, transfers, rewards, QR, banking, and account utilities.

# Navigation

- Four primary destinations cover Home, BON, QR code, and Banking.
- Home combines balance, pay-later, promotional stories, services, and favorites.
- Profile and settings open from the account identity.

# Core Flows

## Pay or transfer

1. Open a Home service or the Services grid.
2. Choose payment, transfer, transport, ticket, power-bank, loan, or partner task.
3. Enter the required details and confirm through its focused flow.

## Use BON rewards

1. Open BON and review balance, market, products, invitations, and mining.
2. Select a reward and inspect progress or price.
3. Exchange BON when the product becomes available.

## Manage banking

1. Open Banking to review attached cards and accounts.
2. Attach a card, open an account, or create a digital Visa.
3. Return to the account utility or transaction context.

# Interaction Patterns

- Four persistent destinations keep finance domains shallow.
- Modals explain BON rules without leaving the marketplace.
