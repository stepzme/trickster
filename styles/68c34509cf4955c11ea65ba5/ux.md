# Overview

Find My supports consensual person sharing, device and item location, directions, sound, lost mode, notification, and removal.

# Navigation

People, Devices, Items, and Me remain in the bottom bar. The map persists while the active entity expands in a bottom sheet.

# Core Flows

## Share location

1. Open People and start sharing.
2. Select a contact and duration.
3. Confirm consent and send.
4. Review the person on the map and stop sharing when needed.

## Find or protect an item

1. Choose a device or item.
2. Review location and availability.
3. Play sound or open directions.
4. Enable notification or Mark as Lost.
5. Add a message and contact, then activate.

# Interaction Patterns

- Request location permission with clear purpose.
- Distinguish live, stale, and unavailable location.
- Keep sharing reversible.
- Confirm erase and removal.
- Preserve selected entity while moving the map.
