# Role

Illustration explains benefits, welcomes users, fills empty states, and closes purchase or selling tasks with warmth. It must not replace listing evidence.

# Visual Language

- Rounded flat forms with light dimensional shading.
- Avito cyan, purple, green, pink, and yellow used together on a white or pastel field.
- Simplified people, bags, homes, devices, coins, and seller tools.
- Friendly proportions and minimal facial detail.

# Composition

Center one main scene above concise copy, or place a compact object cluster at the right of a promo card. Keep silhouettes clear and backgrounds quiet.

# Usage

Use for order success, empty profile, selling benefits, addresses, and promotional guidance. Keep search results, listing photos, totals, and forms illustration-free.

# Guardrails

- Do not imitate real listing photography.
- Do not place art behind prices or form fields.
- Keep the multicolor palette controlled by one dominant accent per scene.
- If a new subject lacks a reference, use the same rounded forms, soft depth, sparse detail, and Avito accent set so it harmonizes with `ui.md`.
