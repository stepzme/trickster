# Overview

ChallengeUp uses flat editorial human scenes to give ready-made challenge templates a distinct emotional and activity identity. The artwork is scoped to template and category cards; active progress, settings, forms, and completion surfaces remain geometric and type-led.

# Visual Style

Figures are angular, simplified, and expressive, shown in clear activity poses with stylized skin and clothing colors. Shapes are flat, saturated, and high-contrast, with minimal internal line detail, no gradients, and no modeled volume. Simple activity objects support the gesture without building a detailed environment. Heavy poster typography and the solid card background complete the graphic language.

Create every required scene with an image-generation model. Generate it as an image asset, review the result, obtain explicit approval, and only then integrate it into the interface. Do not reconstruct the figures with SwiftUI shapes, SF Symbols, ad hoc vector paths, or assembled interface icons.

# Composition

Use one person or one compact activity scene per rectangular template card. Reserve a clear zone for the large title, usually toward the upper or leading portion, and place the figure so its main gesture remains immediately readable. Intentional edge cropping is allowed for limbs or secondary objects, but faces and key gestures remain visible.

The illustration occupies roughly half of the card and is contained by the solid color field. Keep controls, progress tokens, and status badges outside the generated artwork. Preserve consistent figure scale and crop across the template set.

# Color and Materials

Use one saturated flat card field such as mint, coral, mustard, purple, cyan, red, blue, or pink. Figure skin, clothing, and props use a small contrasting flat palette. Black or white provides title contrast. Surfaces remain matte and vector-like: no texture, gradient, gloss, soft shadow, or realistic lighting.

# Variants and States

Variants change the person, pose, activity, and single background color while keeping the same angular anatomy, simplified objects, flat palette, title-safe zone, and graphic proportions. The system is appropriate for ready-made challenge templates and category browsing.

Generate only the activity scenes required by the consuming product. Every generated result requires explicit visual approval before integration. Active progress, forms, settings, destructive actions, and completion metrics should remain geometric and illustration-free.

# Avoid

- Do not substitute photography, glossy 3D rendering, generic corporate vector people, emoji, or SF Symbols.
- Do not recreate the art with SwiftUI shapes, programmatic paths, or assembled UI glyphs.
- Do not add gradients, texture, modeled lighting, or soft shadows.
- Do not cover faces or key gestures with titles, progress tokens, or controls.
- Do not crowd a card with several figures or a detailed environment.
- Do not embed interface controls, counters, or state labels inside the generated image.
- Do not integrate generated artwork before explicit approval.
