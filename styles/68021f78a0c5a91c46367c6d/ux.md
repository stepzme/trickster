# Overview

T-Bank combines accounts, payments, transfers, shopping, travel, city services, support chat, and a financial-product storefront.

# Navigation

- Five bottom destinations cover Home, Payments, City, Chat, and Showcase.
- Home exposes account context, shortcuts, operations, cashback, and product entry points.
- Focused financial and lifestyle tasks use drill-down screens and bottom sheets.

# Core Flows

## Transfer or pay

1. Open Payments or a Home shortcut.
2. Choose recipient, service, QR, or linked payment route.
3. Enter details, review source and amount, then confirm.

## Use City and travel

1. Open City for fuel, shopping, travel, entertainment, prizes, and orders.
2. Select an offer or trip task.
3. Complete booking or purchase through its dedicated linear flow.

## Discover a product

1. Open Showcase and select banking, insurance, business, government, or subscription services.
2. Review terms in a promotional sheet.
3. Continue through the explicit yellow action.

# Interaction Patterns

- Yellow identifies commitment and brand identity; blue supports navigation and utilities.
- White modular cards keep a very broad product surface scannable.
- Promotional 3D objects introduce products without replacing terms.
- Chat remains a first-class destination for support and transactions.
