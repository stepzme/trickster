# Overview

Ozon Travel organizes flights, stays, trains, and tours around a shared search shell, then shifts to highly structured booking and order details.

# Navigation

A five-item bottom bar anchors Tickets, Stays, Orders, Support, and Profile. Large mode selectors at the top switch transport or accommodation products without changing the overall search rhythm.

# Core Flows

## Search and choose

1. Pick a travel mode, set route or destination, dates, and travelers, search, then compare results and details.

## Booking and order

1. Confirm traveler and payment data, complete the purchase, then access tickets, route facts, and support from the order screen.

# Interaction Patterns

Date and passenger choices use bottom sheets, mode switching is icon-led, search actions stay full width, and confirmed orders compress time, route, carriage, and ticket download into scannable cards.
