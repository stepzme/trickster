# Overview

Banco Plata uses a scoped but repeatable authored asset system for brand, cashback, and financial-product moments: glossy orange/white 3D objects, dimensional coins, soft gradient orbs, and rounded volumetric symbols. Operational balances, transfers, and statements remain illustration-free.

# Visual Style

Use an image-generation model to create original isolated 3D objects with rounded volume, polished plastic/glass/soft-metal materials, orange-white brand surfaces, and controlled blue/violet spectral reflections. Motifs may include a coin, card, vault, plus, sphere, or simple financial object. Generate the image, obtain visual approval, and integrate only the approved raster asset. Do not use SwiftUI `Shape`, `Canvas`, programmatic vectors/gradients, SF Symbols, emoji, or other programmatic substitutes.

# Composition

Use one dominant object or a tight two-object composition centered inside a spacious hero/card, usually occupying one third to one half of the module. Preserve a protected zone for product title, value, and CTA; never place the object behind live financial data. Small gradient orbs may act as compact status/product markers, but avoid scattering them decoratively.

# Color and Materials

Orange and white are primary, with restrained black, blue, violet, or iridescent highlights. Materials are premium polished plastic, glass, or soft metal with broad studio highlights and controlled reflections. Backgrounds may be mist gray, white, or a restrained dark studio field when the product hero requires it. Maintain strong edge separation and consistent light direction.

# Variants and States

Cashback uses coin-like or reward objects; product acquisition may use card/vault/plus forms; deposit or premium moments may use a larger sculptural object; compact indicators use soft gradient orbs. Authentication, live balances, transfers, payments, statements, and security remain free of decorative 3D art. Partner logos and store imagery stay separate content assets.

# Avoid

- Do not use SwiftUI shapes, `Canvas`, programmatic vectors or gradients, SF Symbols, emoji, or icon-font substitutes.
- Do not integrate generated art before visual approval; follow image-generation → approval → approved raster integration.
- Avoid flat corporate vectors, cartoon characters, stock icon packs, noisy scenes, excessive rainbow materials, and inconsistent studio lighting.
- Do not place objects behind balances, transaction amounts, or input controls.
- Do not confuse partner logos/photos with the authored asset system.
