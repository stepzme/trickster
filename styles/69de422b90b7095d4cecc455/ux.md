# Overview

Coinbase keeps portfolio review, market discovery, payments, and transaction history as distinct top-level tasks with review steps around irreversible trades.

# Navigation

Home, Trade, Pay, and Transactions remain fixed in the bottom bar. Search and menu provide cross-product access; asset and transaction detail drill into focused screens.

# Core Flows

## Buy, sell, or convert

1. Open Buy and sell from Home or an asset.
2. Choose buy, recurring buy, sell, or convert.
3. Select asset, amount, and funding or destination account.
4. Review price, fees, and resulting amount.
5. Confirm and show transaction status.

## Explore the market

1. Open Trade.
2. Switch among volume, gainers, and losers.
3. Scan asset value, sparkline, and percentage change.
4. Open an asset for detail and action.

# Interaction Patterns

- Require a review before irreversible financial actions.
- Keep fees, risk, and funding source visible.
- Pair color-coded performance with numbers and arrows.
- Keep asset symbols and currency units explicit.
- Preserve task state when dismissing a bottom sheet.
