# Overview

Lovi uses a minimal smiling orb mascot and small line drawings to make skincare assessment feel friendly and non-clinical.

# Visual Style

The mascot is a soft white circular face with tiny black eyes and a curved smile, often floating over lavender-pink haze. Supporting icons are thin, hand-drawn black outlines for facial zones and routine choices.

# Composition

Float the mascot at a screen edge, beside a prompt, or above the tab bar. Keep it small enough not to obscure results. Line icons sit centered inside white selection cards.

# Color

Use white, black, pale lavender, blush pink, and periwinkle glow. Green belongs to fit and safety results, not the mascot itself.

# Usage

Use the mascot for assistance, encouragement, onboarding, and empty guidance; use line art for profile choices. Keep product imagery and scan results photographic and factual.
