# Overview

Glovo uses colorful hand-drawn service and category illustrations to turn a broad delivery marketplace into a friendly visual menu.

# Visual Style

Icons use playful irregular outlines, simple flat color, and recognizable food, grocery, pharmacy, courier, and shopping objects. The family feels warm and human rather than glossy or photoreal.

# Composition

Center one compact object or scene inside a white or softly colored circle, with ample negative space and a short label below. Keep line weight and visual density consistent across categories.

# Color and Materials

Build from Glovo yellow and teal, then add controlled orange, coral, pink, blue, and green to distinguish services.

# Variants and States

Use illustrations for category entry, onboarding hints, empty states, and lightweight service explanation. Keep restaurant cards, dishes, cart, and checkout photography- or information-led.

# Production Requirements

Use only the formats and crops supported by the implemented placement. Where the documented imagery is compositionally important, temporary placeholders must preserve its placement, crop, scale, negative space, and approximate visual weight until final art exists. No unobserved light/dark variants or export ratios are prescribed.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the documented image language with unrelated stock art, arbitrary symbols, or a denser composition.
