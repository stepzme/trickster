# Overview

MTBank Moby organizes daily banking around a card-and-account dashboard, quick money actions, product management, applications, support chat, and secondary services.

# Navigation

four persistent destinations link Home, Products, Chat, and More. Home leads with identity, balance, quick actions, recent activity, cards, and deposits; Products groups account families and applications.

# Core Flows

## Set up and reach the dashboard

1. Introduce Moby, identify the customer, and complete verification.
2. Create a smart code for later access.
3. Land on Home with balances, products, recent activity, and missing-product prompts.

## Manage a product

1. Choose a card, account, deposit, or application from Home or Products.
2. Review balance, status, history, limits, and information.
3. Use the three dominant actions—top up, transfer, pay—or change a focused setting.

# Interaction Patterns

- Settings use plain rows, switches, segments, and immediate status feedback.
