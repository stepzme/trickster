# Overview

Fresh Screen Gallery evidence shows a stable authored illustration system across Alatau City Bank promotional heroes, product cards, deposit screens, sheets, and service rows. Use image-generation model output or authored raster assets for these visuals; do not substitute programmatic drawings.

# Visual Style

Create glossy, softly lit 3D finance objects with rounded geometry, studio shadows, and bright blue, yellow, green, cyan, or violet materials. Product illustrations may include metallic rate numerals, card renders, coins, percent signs, gift-like safes, and compact service objects. The style is polished and dimensional, not flat iconography.

# Composition

Place one primary object or object cluster inside a rounded product card, hero panel, bottom sheet row, or deposit banner. Keep the object readable at small iPhone sizes, with text usually on the left and the object on the right. Maintain consistent camera angle, soft shadow, and apparent object scale across repeated assets.

# Color and Materials

Anchor imagery in Alatau blue and bright yellow. Use green for deposit and positive-money objects, violet only for investment or adjacent product families, and metallic gold for large rate/value emphasis. Preserve light-mode and dark-mode contrast by exporting raster assets that still read on white, pale blue, and charcoal surfaces.

# Variants and States

Generate raster assets for campaign heroes, product discovery cards, deposit banners, service thumbnails, empty states, and confirmation/supportive moments only after explicit user visual approval. Integrate approved assets as image files in the app bundle and reference them through normal raster image views. Produce dark-surface variants where the same object appears in dark theme.

# Avoid

Do not replace this system with SwiftUI shapes, SF Symbols, emoji, line icons, screenshots, or CSS/SVG-like programmatic substitutes. Do not introduce unrelated illustration styles, hand-drawn characters, flat pictograms, stock photos, or generic banking clip art.
