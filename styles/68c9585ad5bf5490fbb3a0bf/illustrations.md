# Illustration Language

Use glossy 3D product metaphors rendered like premium miniatures: linked rings, stars, gifts, wallets, coin stacks, and small sculptural plants. Objects should feel tactile, optimistic, and financially symbolic.

# Palette and Materials

- Favor violet and lavender gradients with accents of gold, teal, coral, and soft green.
- Mix polished plastic, brushed metal, translucent glass, and soft fabric in one controlled material family.
- Light objects with a broad studio glow and gentle contact shadow.

# Composition

- Center one object or a compact cluster on a square gradient tile.
- Preserve generous clear space and keep silhouettes readable at card size.
- For hero art, place the object inside a simple glass dome or atmospheric gradient field.

# Do and Don't

- Keep depth, lighting, and material quality consistent across financial products.
- Use one obvious metaphor per asset.
- Do not mix flat corporate vectors, photoreal people, or crowded scenes into this illustration system.
