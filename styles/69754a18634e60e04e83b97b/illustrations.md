# Role

Illustration gives promotions, stories, and the Chizhik brand a recognizable voice while the catalog remains product-photo led.

# Visual Language

- Small red-black-white bird mascot with sharp simple silhouette.
- Bold flat yellow, hot pink, black, and white fields.
- Angular confetti, speech-like shapes, and oversized display lettering.
- Simple vector scenes with little shading.

# Composition

Keep the mascot or one graphic scene inside a large promotional card with a clear headline and CTA area. Use generous solid-color space.

# Usage

Use for promotions, stories, onboarding, loyalty, and branded empty states. Keep products, prices, basket, checkout, payment, and order status photo- or data-led.

# Guardrails

- Do not replace product cutouts with illustration.
- Do not place the mascot behind prices or quantities.
- Preserve yellow, black, red, and pink from `ui.md`.
- For a new branded moment, use one compact mascot pose or flat scene on a bold solid field with heavy black type.
