# Overview

Monese is a European everyday banking app spanning onboarding, account overview, cards, transfers, payments, card ordering, invitations, exploration, and settings.

# Navigation

Five fixed destinations connect Home, Card, Pay, Invite, and Explore. Account and support actions live in the top corners or side menu.

# Core Flows

## Review and use an account

1. Open Home to review balance and recent activity.
2. Switch to Card for details, lock, limits, and card actions.
3. Open Pay to choose a transfer or payment route.
4. Confirm recipient, amount, and result.

## Extend the account

1. Order a physical or virtual card.
2. Review plan, allowances, fees, and limits.
3. Invite a friend or explore partner benefits.
4. Manage notifications and profile through Menu.

# Interaction Patterns

- Bright blue anchors brand and active navigation.
- White surfaces and pale blue gradients keep banking light.
- Card imagery and large balances lead core decisions.
- Confirmation and support use simple one-column sheets.
