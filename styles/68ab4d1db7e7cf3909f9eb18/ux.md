# Overview

Ostrovok is a hotel and apartment booking service built around search, destination inspiration, map and list results, favorites, bookings, transfers, support, and account tools.

# Navigation

Search, Trips, Favorites, Support, and Menu form the primary navigation. Home leads with a persistent accommodation search and editorial destination items.

# Core Flows

## Find and book a stay

1. Enter destination, dates, and guests from Home.
2. Compare list or map results using price, rating, review count, distance, and filters.
3. Open a property, inspect rooms and conditions, and choose a rate.
4. Provide guest and payment details, then retain the booking in Trips.

## Manage a trip

1. Open hotel or transfer tabs in Trips.
2. Review dates, payment deadline, status, and property or ride details.
3. Pay, contact support, or change the reservation when allowed.
4. Keep completed and upcoming travel available from the same destination.

# Interaction Patterns

- Trips use a simple segment for stays and transfers.
- Empty states immediately pair friendly art with one useful search action.
