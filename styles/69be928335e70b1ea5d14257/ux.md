# Overview

A map-led mobility system where users name a destination, offer a price, compare driver responses, and manage safety throughout the ride.

# Navigation

The map is the persistent base. A side menu holds profile, history, settings, help, and driver mode. Ride planning and active rides appear as layered bottom sheets over the map.

# Core Flows

## Enter and request a ride

1. Continue by phone or passkey.
2. Confirm the verification code.
3. Set pickup and destination on the map.
4. Choose service, offer a price, and start driver search.

## Manage an active ride

1. Review driver, vehicle, arrival time, price, and route.
2. Contact the driver or share pickup notes.
3. Open Safety to share the ride, contact support, or use emergency actions.
4. Complete and rate the ride.

## Build a multi-stop trip

1. Add and reorder stops.
2. Review the offer and available drivers.
3. Cancel, edit, or proceed without leaving the map context.

# Interaction Patterns

- Large white bottom sheets float over a muted map.
- Acid-lime marks primary actions and safety emphasis.
- Offer pricing uses strong numerals with increment controls and a timer.
- Ride progress keeps driver and safety actions above route details.
- Promotional service tiles use compact 3D scenes and bright backgrounds.
