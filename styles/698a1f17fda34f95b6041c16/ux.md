# Overview

MES Diary brings schedule, grades, assignments, school services, attendance, meals, rankings, and account balances into one student dashboard.

# Navigation

Five fixed destinations cover Schedule, Grades, Tasks, School, and Accounts. A weekly date strip remains close to schedule context; profile and notifications sit in the top bar.

# Core Flows

## Follow the school day

1. Select a date in the weekly strip.
2. Review lessons, rooms, substitutions, breaks, homework, and events.
3. Open grade or assignment details when needed.
4. Move between schedule, assessments, and tasks without losing student context.

## Use school services

1. Open the School dashboard.
2. Review attendance, meals, portfolio, subjects, rankings, and news.
3. Favorite frequently used services.
4. Check invoices, balances, payment history, and autopayment from Accounts.

# Interaction Patterns

- White cards sit on a pale lavender page with purple or blue emphasis.
- Color-coded service icons aid scanning but do not replace labels.
- Schedule hierarchy separates lessons, breaks, homework, and extracurricular events.
- Errors and payment notices use clear inline banners close to the affected account.
