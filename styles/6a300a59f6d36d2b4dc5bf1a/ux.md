# Overview

Janymda is a telecom super-app that brings account, shopping, media, finance, travel, and support into one dense but scannable home surface.

# Navigation

A five-item bottom bar anchors Account, Shop, the central Services launcher, Simply, and Messages. The central launcher expands into a service catalog sheet; settings remain in the home header.

# Core Flows

## Onboarding

1. Choose language.
2. Review a short benefits carousel.
3. Enter the service hub with sign-in available but not blocking exploration.

## Find a service

1. Scan the compact service grid on Home.
2. Open the central launcher for the complete catalog.
3. Choose a category and continue into its focused flow.

## Choose a tariff

1. Compare plan families and benefits.
2. Open a tariff detail with price and included services.
3. Expand terms when needed, then connect with the sticky primary action.

# Interaction Patterns

- Home mixes service shortcuts, promotional banners, and media rails in one vertical feed.
- Favorite actions can be customized for repeat tasks.
- Catalog and support open as bottom sheets without losing home context.
- Long commercial terms remain collapsible while price and CTA stay easy to find.
