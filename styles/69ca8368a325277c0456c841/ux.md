# Overview

Shop combines merchant discovery, brand following, product browsing, saved collections, cart, checkout, delivery tracking, and account management in an image-led marketplace shell.

# Navigation

- Floating bottom controls provide back, home, search, bag, and overflow.
- Top chips expose profile, notifications, Following, Minis, and Saved.
- Merchant shops retain their identity while using the shared product and purchase model.

# Core Flows

## Discover and buy

1. Browse followed merchants, recent items, or search results.
2. Open a brand shop or product.
3. Select size, color, and quantity.
4. Add to cart or buy immediately.
5. Review shipping, payment, discounts, and total before paying.

## Save and follow

1. Heart a product or follow a merchant.
2. Organize saved products into a collection.
3. Return through Saved or Following and continue shopping.

## Track and manage

1. Open an active or delivered order from Home, orders, or Profile.
2. Review progress, details, product imagery, or map.
3. Revisit the merchant or buy the product again.

# Interaction Patterns

- Floating navigation preserves browsing context across merchant surfaces.
- Product hearts and following state synchronize discovery and saved areas.
- Option selection happens before the violet purchase action.
- The dark cart sheet creates a focused review step without leaving the product.
- Checkout removes merchant atmosphere and exposes transaction details plainly.
