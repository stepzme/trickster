# Overview

Open is an immersive breathwork, meditation, movement, and sound experience built around a daily practice, programs, discovery, community, studio tools, and profile progress.

# Navigation

A minimal bottom bar connects a global surface, Discover, practice or waveform, a location-like community destination, and Profile. Today is centered and combines the daily practice with Meditate, Breathe, Move, and Sound modes.

# Core Flows

## Begin the daily practice

1. Land on Today with the current date streak and one cinematic daily session.
2. Choose Meditate, Breathe, Move, or Sound, or continue the featured practice.
3. Review teacher, duration, and intent, then begin playback.
4. Complete the session and return to streak or life-score progress.

## Discover a program

1. Enter Discover and browse recent, library, timer, programs, For You, and Trending.
2. Filter by modality or search by need.
3. Open a session or multi-day program.
4. Save, begin, and continue from Home or Profile.

# Interaction Patterns

- Full-bleed cinematic photography and warm blur create emotional context on a black canvas.
- Fine white lines, widely spaced type, and small red details keep controls quiet.
- Content lists stay almost borderless; image crops and typography create grouping.
- Large outlined or white pill actions appear only at entry, paywall, or timer commitment.
