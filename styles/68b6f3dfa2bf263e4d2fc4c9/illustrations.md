# Overview

Idoo uses a stable authored illustration language for onboarding, selected permission or transition moments, empty friends/history-like states, and city identity. Cropped walking legs and fashion figures sit on warm peach-to-pink/lilac gradients, pairing expressive movement with oversized geometric display type. Real places remain photographic and maps remain factual.

# Visual Style

Figures are fashion-editorial rather than character-cartoon: elongated cropped legs or partial bodies, thin black line work, sparse flat fills, and a few bold clothing or footwear accents in coral red, black, cobalt, or warm neutrals. The background is a soft luminous gradient with little texture. Anatomy and garments may be stylised, but the pose clearly communicates walking or forward movement.

Generate required illustrations with the available image-generation model. Do not construct figures, legs, garments, gradient scenes, or transition artwork from SwiftUI shapes, emoji, or SF Symbols. Present generated results for explicit approval before integration.

# Composition

One cropped figure or pair of walking legs is the focal subject and may occupy roughly a third to half of the viewport. Crops are deliberately bold: feet, calves, garments, or accessories may extend beyond one or more screen edges, while the walking gesture remains legible. Large display text and a broad CTA occupy protected negative space rather than overlapping the most detailed part of the figure.

On empty or city states, the figure may become smaller but still remains more substantial than a decorative icon. Warm gradient fields can fill the entire viewport or a large bounded region. Organic white cut-paper or cloud-like transitions may connect illustration to the white product surface.

# Color and Materials

Backgrounds blend warm peach, dusty pink, pale sand, and soft lilac. Line work is near-black. Coral red links the art to the primary action in `ui.md`; cobalt, black, white, or muted clothing tones provide one or two additional accents. Keep fills mostly flat and edges crisp, with only gentle gradient light behind them. Avoid glossy 3D materials, painterly texture, or busy scenic backgrounds.

# Variants and States

Onboarding variants use the largest crop, strongest gradient, and most direct pairing with oversized display text. Permission or route-transition variants may simplify the pose and leave more space for concise product explanation. Empty friends or similar states use a centered or lower-cropped figure with more white/gradient negative space. City variants can change clothing accent or crop while preserving the walking-leg motif and warm gradient medium.

Guide cards, place detail, saved places, and route facts use real photography or maps instead of this illustration style.

# Avoid

- Do not replace generated authored imagery with SwiftUI shapes, emoji, SF Symbols, generic vector people, or stock lifestyle photography.
- Do not show full busy street scenes, detailed environments, crowds, or multiple competing figures.
- Do not use children's-book characters, glossy 3D avatars, anime styling, or realistic fashion photography.
- Do not crop so tightly that the walking pose or footwear/clothing accent becomes unreadable.
- Do not place body copy or CTA labels over the figure's focal details without protected negative space.
- Do not substitute illustration for factual place photography, guide imagery, or maps.
