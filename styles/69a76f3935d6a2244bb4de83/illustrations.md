# Overview

Illustration differentiates banking products and services on the dashboard. It supports discovery and promotion, not transaction confirmation or statements.

# Visual Style

- Glossy soft-3D objects with compact, slightly surreal compositions.
- Azure, navy, cyan, silver, and selective gold.
- Cards, coins, rings, safes, jars, branch pins, passports, and payment devices.
- Pale blue halo or transparent background with gentle studio shadow.

# Composition

Center one object cluster inside a white product tile, leaving title and conditions clear. Campaign banners may use photography but should not mix with the 3D object set.

# Color and Materials

Color and material rules are included in Visual Style; no additional palette relationship was documented.

# Variants and States

Use for new cards, deposits, savings jars, loans, appointments, branches, and service discovery. Keep balances, transfers, statements, identity, and security screens illustration-free.

# Avoid

- Do not place 3D objects behind financial values.
- Do not mix multiple lighting directions or rendering styles in one grid.
- Keep product art compact and subordinate to title and rate.
- If a new subject lacks a reference, match the same glossy material, cool palette, isolated object cluster, and soft studio shadow so it harmonizes with `ui.md`.
