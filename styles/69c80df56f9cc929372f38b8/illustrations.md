# Overview

Opal uses surreal luminous imagery to make focus states feel tangible: glowing glass orbs, misty gradients, laser-like perspective fields, dream silhouettes, rain, waves, and abstract light landscapes.

# Visual Style

Blend dark photographic or rendered environments with iridescent glass, neon rim light, soft bloom, and cinematic blur. Subjects should feel contemplative and slightly otherworldly rather than literal productivity icons.

# Composition

Place a single luminous subject or horizon inside a rounded card with protected space for title, schedule, and Add action. Hero objects may float centrally above progress.

# Color

Build from black and deep blue-green, then use mint, cyan, violet, amber, and coral as controlled light. Avoid broad flat fills; color should appear as atmosphere or emitted light.

# Usage

Use this language for focus presets, sleep modes, soundscapes, score loading, and meaningful completion states. Match the black glass UI and keep illustrations out of settings or permission copy unless they clarify the step.
