# Overview

Gosuslugi supports service discovery, applications, identity documents, payments, appointments, notifications, agency correspondence, QR utilities, and result tracking.

# Navigation

Five primary destinations anchor Home, Services, assistant, Payments, and Documents. Search and the assistant provide cross-cutting entry points when the user does not know the agency or service name.

# Core Flows

## Find and submit a service

1. Search or browse a category, review requirements and outcome, start the application, verify personal data, complete conditional steps, attach evidence, choose fulfillment, and submit.

## Manage documents and results

1. Open a document or notification, inspect validity or application status, share or present it when allowed, follow agency messages, and retrieve the final result.

# Interaction Patterns

Long tasks are divided into named steps, personal data is reviewed before submission, warnings sit beside the affected field, and each screen exposes one clear next action.
