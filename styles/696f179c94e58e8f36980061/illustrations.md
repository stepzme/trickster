# Overview

Lenta uses a friendly orange cat mascot and playful campaign compositions to humanize grocery delivery, cart, loyalty, and order states.

# Visual Style

The mascot is a soft 3D cartoon cat with rounded features, expressive poses, and small grocery or delivery props. Campaign art mixes the character with blue fields, snow, parcels, carts, and product cutouts.

# Composition

Center the mascot above short state copy or pair it with one prop. Banners keep the character on one side and preserve a clear copy area on the other.

# Color and Materials

Use warm orange and cream for the mascot, supported by Lenta blue, white, yellow, and restrained red promotion accents.

# Variants and States

Use the mascot for onboarding, empty cart, order confirmation, loyalty, and seasonal delivery campaigns. Keep ordinary product cards photography-led; new art must inherit the UI's blue actions and clean white canvas.

# Production Requirements

Use only the formats and crops supported by the implemented placement. Where the documented imagery is compositionally important, temporary placeholders must preserve its placement, crop, scale, negative space, and approximate visual weight until final art exists. No unobserved light/dark variants or export ratios are prescribed.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the documented image language with unrelated stock art, arbitrary symbols, or a denser composition.
