# Overview

Translate is a focused language utility for typed, spoken, camera, and face-to-face translation, plus favorites and recent results.

# Navigation

- Three primary destinations cover Translation, Conversation, and Favorites.
- Two language selectors stay pinned of translation and conversation surfaces.
- Translation results expose listen, expand, favorite, and copy actions in context.

# Core Flows

## Translate text or speech

1. Choose source and target languages.
2. Type text or use one of the microphone actions.
3. Read or listen to the result, then copy, expand, or favorite it.

## Hold a conversation

1. Open Conversation and confirm both languages.
2. Speak with automatic or side-specific microphone input.
3. Use face-to-face orientation when each person needs a dedicated side.

## Revisit a translation

1. Open Favorites.
2. Scan favorites and recents grouped by language pair.
3. Select an entry to reuse its text and audio actions.

# Interaction Patterns

- Teal marks translation output, speech actions, and active destinations.
- Language menus favor familiar checked lists over custom pickers.
