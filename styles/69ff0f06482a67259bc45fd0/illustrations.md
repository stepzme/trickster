# Overview

Ivi uses glossy 3D promotional objects only for subscription and brand moments. The catalog itself stays photography-led.

# Visual Style

Soft, toy-like 3D objects with inflated surfaces, rounded edges, and subtle material shine. Objects combine familiar devices or hands with playful branded details.

# Composition

Center one compact object cluster above the message. Leave dark negative space around it; do not mix illustration into poster grids or title details.

# Color

Use magenta and pink as the dominant material colors, supported by skin tones, white highlights, and a near-black background.

# Usage

Reserve this language for subscription offers, empty promotional states, or brand onboarding. New illustrations must inherit the dark UI, pink glow, and rounded 3D material treatment rather than introduce a separate visual style.
