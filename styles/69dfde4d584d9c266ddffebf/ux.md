# Overview

PayPal reduces everyday payment tasks to balance, recent activity, send/request, and wallet management with unusually sparse screens.

# Navigation

A three-item primary navigation anchors Home, Send/Request, and Wallet.

# Core Flows

## Send or request money

1. Open the central action, choose Send or Request, search a contact, enter the amount and note, review, then confirm.

## Wallet and activity

1. Review balance and recent transactions, open Wallet for cards and payment preferences, or inspect activity for completed transfers.

# Interaction Patterns

The sampled flows use direct actions, explicit completion, and return to the current context. No additional repeated interaction behavior was documented.

# System Access Timing

No system-access request timing or denial recovery was documented in the reviewed source.
