# Overview

ONAY brings transit cards, QR fare payment, top-up, transfers, trip history, routes, tickets, metro, and a small marketplace into one Kazakhstan mobility app.

# Navigation

Routes, Cards, a prominent central QR scanner, Shop, and Menu form the bottom bar. Cards opens by default with balance, city, trip count, and immediate top-up or transfer actions.

# Core Flows

## Pay and manage a transit card

1. Select a personal or linked card.
2. Review balance, recent trips, and city context.
3. Show QR, top up, transfer, inspect history, or add another card.
4. Receive immediate balance or payment feedback.

## Find a route

1. Choose city and transport type.
2. Search a route number or switch to directions.
3. Open a route, stop, or saved home and work destination.
4. Return to the persistent QR action when ready to travel.

# Interaction Patterns

- Yellow identifies cards, scanner, selections, and primary mobility actions.
- Large rounded cards and circular shortcuts sit on a cool almost-white canvas.
- Route grids prioritize number scanning over visual decoration.
- Shop opens as a distinct commerce surface but keeps ONAY identity and support close.
