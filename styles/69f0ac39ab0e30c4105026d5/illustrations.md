# Overview

Freedom uses a narrow authored 3D object system for onboarding, identity verification, biometric explanation, progress, and product completion. It is separate from card artwork, payment and merchant marks, service icons, campaign banners, finance metrics, charts, logos, and photography-like promotional media.

# Visual Style

Create polished soft 3D objects with rounded cubes, simplified locks, devices, cards, silhouettes, and circular check or progress forms. Build volume without outlines, using smooth plastic-, glass-, or light-metal-like surfaces, gentle gradients, diffuse studio light, mild soft shadows, and low contrast. Forms should feel clean and calm rather than toy-busy or photorealistic.

Every new illustration must be generated with an image-generation model from this language. It requires separate visual approval before interface integration and must then be integrated as the approved raster asset.

# Composition

Center one object or tightly related object group in generous white or very pale space. Keep the full silhouette visible, usually around the upper or middle third, with concise copy and action below. Rarely crop the object at an edge. Completion and biometric variants may be smaller, but they remain a clear focal mass rather than an icon inside a row.

# Color and Materials

Anchor the system in Freedom emerald, mint, white, and light gray with occasional deep green. Use soft material transitions, restrained glass or metal cues, diffuse highlights, and subtle grounding shadows. Avoid loud rainbow reflections, heavy black, hard specular light, grain, and realistic environmental texture.

# Variants and States

- Onboarding uses the largest green cube or ecosystem object as a centered hero.
- Verification may use a simplified person silhouette or circular progress/check object.
- Biometric explanation uses a soft 3D lock or related security object.
- Product completion may show a contained gray card render or check medallion above the result.
- No stable authored error family was observed.

# Avoid

- Do not draw or approximate the art with SwiftUI shapes, Canvas, programmatic paths or gradients, SF Symbols, emoji, text glyphs, or assembled service icons.
- Do not integrate an unapproved generation, placeholder, screenshot crop, or temporary vector; use the visually approved raster asset.
- Do not substitute card brands, merchant logos, campaign banners, finance charts, literal service icons, or stock photography.
- Do not use glossy 3D emoji, clay characters, dense scenes, realistic people, or dramatic cinematic lighting.
- Do not spread the 3D language across ordinary form controls and transaction rows.
