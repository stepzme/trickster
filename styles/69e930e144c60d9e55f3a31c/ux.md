# Overview

Simbank organizes card management, transfers, payments, savings, cashback, rewards, analytics, and support around a balance-led home and contextual product tabs.

# Navigation

- Five primary destinations cover Home, Payments, Savings, Cashback, and More.
- Home exposes top-up, transfer, QR payment, stories, onboarding tasks, and recent operations.
- Focused tasks use back navigation and keep their primary action available until completion.

# Core Flows

## Transfer or pay

1. Choose card, phone, contact, QR, or service payment.
2. Enter the amount and supporting details.
3. Review source and fee, then confirm.
4. Open details to share, split, repeat, tag, or request help.

## Save money

1. Open Savings and choose deposit or goal.
2. Configure amount, rules, and recurring contributions.
3. Review interest, progress, and withdrawal settings.

## Manage the card

1. Open Card from Home.
2. View CVV or details, manage subscriptions, limits, PIN, security, blocking, or statements.

# Interaction Patterns

- Stories and checklists introduce features without blocking transactions.
- Receipts expose follow-up actions after completion.
