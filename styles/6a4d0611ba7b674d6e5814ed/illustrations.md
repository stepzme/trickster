# Overview

Airba pay uses small glossy 3D finance and shopping objects to explain loans, verification, repayment, and partner purchasing without overwhelming the forms.

# Visual Style

Use softly lit 3D objects with simple silhouettes, pale blue circular backplates, and restrained cyan-to-violet gradients. Keep objects friendly rather than literal or photorealistic.

# Composition

Center one object in an open white area or place it at the edge of a compact information tile. Empty states may use a larger circular scene above one action.

# Color

Base illustrations on Airba blue, cyan, violet, and pale aqua. Warm orange or pink is reserved for warnings and shopping props.

# Usage

Use this language for verification education, loan-empty states, repayment guidance, and partner-product introductions. Use merchant photography or logos when the user is choosing a real partner.
