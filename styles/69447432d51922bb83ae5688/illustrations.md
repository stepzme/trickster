# Overview

Burger King uses an authored illustration layer for loyalty, crown education, seasonal promotions, profile identity, and reward messaging. It complements rather than replaces food photography: transactional menu, customization, address, payment, and order content remain photo- or data-led. New illustration assets must be produced with the available image-generation model, visually approved before integration, and inserted as approved image assets; an illustration-required screen is incomplete until the asset is visibly integrated.

# Visual Style

Use bold, flat editorial artwork with chunky simplified characters, crown or trophy motifs, rounded props, and playful exaggerated proportions. Forms are broad and graphic, with heavy dark outlines or strong edge separation, minimal interior detail, and little to no realistic texture. Faces and gestures are simple and expressive. Depth comes from overlapping flat shapes and occasional restrained shading, not photorealistic lighting or 3D rendering.

# Composition

Illustration normally occupies roughly one third to one half of a large rounded campaign card, or becomes a centered hero object in a loyalty/explanatory state. Keep one clear focal character, crown, trophy, or festive object rather than a dense scene. Protect a clean zone for a short heavy headline and CTA; never run essential copy across faces, hands, or the main motif. Cropping may trim peripheral props but must preserve the character gesture and recognizable crown/reward silhouette.

# Color and Materials

Use saturated Burger King reds, oranges, yellows, and dark chocolate-brown outlines against cream or a single strong campaign field; occasional mint or green may mark a positive/reward variant. The palette must relate directly to the cream, brown, orange, and red roles in `ui.md`. Surfaces read as printed flat color with crisp edges, not glass, metal, clay, glossy 3D plastic, or stock-vector gradients.

# Variants and States

Loyalty education favors oversized crown, trophy, or reward motifs with concise explanatory copy. Promotional and seasonal cards may add one festive character scene and a stronger red or yellow background. Reward or completion states may introduce green/mint locally while retaining brown outlines and the same flat construction. Profile/avatar artwork may reduce to a single centered character or face. Transactional product, basket, payment, address, and tracking states do not gain illustration merely to fill space.

# Avoid

- Do not draw required artwork with SwiftUI shapes, Canvas, programmatic vectors, SF Symbols, or emoji.
- Do not integrate the first generated result without visual review and explicit approval.
- Do not use generic stock-vector people, photorealistic 3D mascots, glossy gradients, thin geometric line art, or unrelated anime styling.
- Do not replace food photography with illustration or mix illustrated food into a photographic product grid.
- Do not place characters behind prices, totals, form fields, or long copy.
- Do not create busy multi-character scenes where one bold motif is sufficient.
- Do not use cool corporate blue/purple palettes that detach the asset from the warm system in `ui.md`.
