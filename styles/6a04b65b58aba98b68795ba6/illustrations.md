# Role

Illustration gives loans, cashback, cards, and deposits a premium acquisition layer while the banking workspace remains functional.

# Visual Language

- Glossy 3D studio objects in emerald, mint, chrome, and translucent glass.
- Coins, clocks, cards, stacked numbers, and geometric fragments.
- High-contrast green or white backgrounds with realistic reflections.
- Large numeric typography may be integrated into campaign assets.

# Composition

Center one hero object in a full-screen story or bounded banner. Keep headline and CTA in clear zones separate from the object silhouette.

# Usage

Use for loan, cashback, card, and deposit acquisition. Keep balances, transfer lists, payment categories, and forms illustration-free.

# Guardrails

- Do not place reflective objects behind financial data.
- Do not mix flat cartoon characters with the studio 3D set.
- Keep emerald as the dominant campaign hue.
- If a new product lacks a reference, match the glossy materials, centered studio composition, clean reflection, and restrained green palette so it harmonizes with `ui.md`.
