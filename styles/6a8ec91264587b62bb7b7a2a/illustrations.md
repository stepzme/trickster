# Overview

Ozon Bank uses polished 3D cards, gifts, bubbles, arrows, ribbons, and ticket-like receipts to make products, cashback, credit, and payment results feel tangible.

# Visual Style

Use smooth rounded 3D objects with saturated Ozon blue, violet gradients, bright gift-yellow, and soft translucent highlights. Keep shadows broad and optimistic.

# Composition

Place one product object on the right of a blue promotional panel, leaving a clear copy area. Success art can frame a centered receipt inside a soft blue gradient stage.

# Color and Materials

Anchor in royal and electric blue, then add violet, cyan, green, yellow, and small magenta accents. Keep operational surfaces white and avoid extending gradients behind dense transaction lists.

# Variants and States

Use illustration for card and credit promotion, cashback selection, onboarding, payment success, and empty product states. Financial controls and history should remain data-first.

# Avoid

Do not introduce an unrelated illustration style or substitute decorative symbols for source-supported imagery.
