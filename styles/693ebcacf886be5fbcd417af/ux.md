# Overview

Magnit unifies loyalty, store offers, grocery delivery, marketplace categories, cosmetics, cart, checkout, payment, and order tracking.

# Navigation

five persistent destinations connect Home, In Store, Delivery, Market, and Cosmetics. Search and loyalty remain available; cart remains available as an action.

# Core Flows

## Discover and add groceries

1. Enter through Home, Delivery, catalog, or search.
2. Browse promotions, categories, recipes, and previously purchased items.
3. Open a product for price, rating, reviews, attributes, and recommendations.
4. Add and adjust quantity in cart.

## Complete delivery

1. Confirm address, recipient, delivery time, and replacement behavior.
2. Apply promotions and choose payment.
3. Submit the order and receive delivery timing.
4. Track the courier or contact support.

# Interaction Patterns

- Sheets explain promo codes and focused fulfillment decisions.
- totals maintain a clear path through checkout.
