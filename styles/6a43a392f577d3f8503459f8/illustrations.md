# Overview

Yandex Pay uses glossy miniature 3D objects and softly lit service icons to make financial products approachable.

# Visual Style

Render coins, cards, gifts, routers, buildings, and service symbols as compact toy-like objects with rounded edges, pale materials, and restrained reflections.

# Composition

- Keep one object or a small group centered inside a soft tile.
- Use ample whitespace and avoid scenes with detailed backgrounds.
- Pair dimensional objects with clean labels rather than decorative copy.

# Color

Use pastel cyan, pink, lime, lilac, and warm yellow, often on misty iridescent gradients. Keep dark graphite reserved for critical actions and readable text.

# Usage

Use for product shortcuts, payment categories, rewards, promotions, and educational banners. Merchant offers may use photography instead.
