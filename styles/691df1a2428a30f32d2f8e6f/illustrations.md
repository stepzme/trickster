# Overview

The app uses editorial collage as a content identity: photographed people, sculpture, architecture, paintings, and cut-out objects are assembled into compact cultural scenes.

# Visual Style

Photographic cut-outs and reproductions are layered with restrained ornamental strokes. The result feels curated and magazine-like rather than cartoonish; each composition communicates a subject before the title is read.

# Composition

- Hero collages place one dominant figure near center with smaller objects supporting the topic.
- Course tiles crop artwork tightly and allow overlays for views, status, and bookmarks.
- Dark gradients at the lower edge protect white titles and metadata.

# Color and Materials

Artwork carries most of the color against a black interface. Pink, violet, mint, warm beige, and red accents appear inside imagery while controls remain primarily white, charcoal, and violet-blue.

# Variants and States

Use original, topic-specific editorial collages for featured courses and collections. Keep utility screens and lesson playback free of decorative art so the learning content remains primary.

# Production Requirements

Use only the formats and crops supported by the implemented placement. Where the documented imagery is compositionally important, temporary placeholders must preserve its placement, crop, scale, negative space, and approximate visual weight until final art exists. No unobserved light/dark variants or export ratios are prescribed.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the documented image language with unrelated stock art, arbitrary symbols, or a denser composition.

# Known Gaps

- Export aspect ratios, safe crop measurements, animation, and unobserved state variants were not documented.
