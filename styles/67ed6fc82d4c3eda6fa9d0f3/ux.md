# Overview

MAX is a messenger organized around contacts, calls, chats, media sharing, invitations, and service-account communication.

# Navigation

Four fixed destinations cover Contacts, Calls, Chats, and Settings. Conversation headers expose calling and overflow actions; creation uses compact black circular controls.

# Core Flows

## Start and continue communication

1. Find a contact, invite by link or QR, or start from a chat.
2. Exchange messages, reactions, attachments, voice, and location.
3. Escalate to an audio or video call.
4. Review shared media or service details from the header.

## Manage contacts and services

1. Search the address book or open a service chat.
2. Invite unavailable contacts.
3. Share a QR identity or link.
4. Adjust notifications, sound, and account preferences.

# Interaction Patterns

- The everyday shell is neutral white with bright blue active controls.
- Chats use pale blue message surfaces and compact delivery metadata.
- Calls switch to a dark focused stage with a rounded control tray.
- System sheets and permissions retain simple platform patterns but adopt MAX spacing and actions.
