# Overview

OTP Bank uses soft glossy 3D objects for transfers, products, insurance, education, and success: cards, banknotes, folders, bones, globes, houses, hearts, and floating check marks.

# Visual Style

Render rounded plastic or glass objects with pale lavender shadow, lime highlights, and small realistic material cues. The mood is friendly and accessible.

# Composition

Place one or two objects at the right edge of a white product row, or center a larger symbol above a transaction result. Preserve a clear label zone.

# Color and Materials

Use OTP lime, lavender, cyan, orange, and white, with charcoal details. Keep backgrounds pale and avoid dark dramatic lighting in transactional contexts.

# Variants and States

Use illustration for product families, insurance categories, onboarding education, transfer guidance, and success states. Controls and financial data should remain conventional and precise.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the observed imagery language with unrelated stock art or arbitrary symbols.
- Do not change the documented crop, density, or relationship to nearby text.
