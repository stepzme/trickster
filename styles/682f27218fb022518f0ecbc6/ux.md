# Overview

Flowwow supports location-aware discovery, category and store comparison, product evaluation, saved collections, cart configuration, checkout, order tracking, seller or courier communication, cancellation, and post-order rating. Preserve the continuity of recipient, address, timing, seller, and order context; do not copy gifting-specific tasks when the adapted product does not have them.

# Navigation

Primary destinations remain directly switchable during discovery. Category results lead into a store, and store navigation leads into products, messages, reviews, and store information without losing the current seller context. Product details open as a focused task and return to the originating store or result. Cart, checkout, payment, and tracking are successive states of one order rather than new primary destinations. Messages reached from an order retain the order and participant context.

# Core Flows

## Set location and discover an item

1. Open the home destination.
2. Confirm or change the delivery address.
3. Choose a category, occasion, recipient shortcut, promotion, or search.
4. Apply relevant filters.
5. Compare nearby stores using their products, rating, delivery terms, and bonus information.
6. Open a store or product while retaining the discovery state.

## Evaluate a store and product

1. Open a store from discovery results.
2. Review seller identity, rating, reviews, delivery terms, and product groups.
3. Open a product.
4. Review media, dimensions, recent availability, price, rating, description, delivery estimate, and price history.
5. Save the product or change quantity when needed.
6. Add the product to the active cart.

## Configure the cart

1. Open the active cart.
2. Review the seller, item, quantity, price, and earned bonuses.
3. Add or edit a seller comment.
4. Add a postcard or another optional service.
5. Apply available bonuses, discounts, or a promo code.
6. Review every optional charge and the updated subtotal.
7. Continue to order details.

## Complete checkout

1. Confirm whether the order uses delivery or pickup.
2. Select or edit the recipient and fulfillment address.
3. Add access details and courier instructions when required.
4. Choose an available delivery time.
5. Select a payment method.
6. Choose a tip or no tip.
7. Review the item cost, discounts, service fee, delivery, tip, and final total.
8. Continue to payment and receive the resulting order state.

## Track and manage an order

1. Open the active order.
2. Review the current status, planned time, recipient, address, comments, seller, and included items.
3. Edit details while the current order state permits changes.
4. Contact the store or courier from the order context.
5. Cancel the order when cancellation is available and confirm the consequence.
6. Follow the status until completion.
7. Rate the courier, store, or order when prompted after completion.

# Interaction Patterns

- Address changes refresh availability, delivery estimates, and nearby sellers while preserving the user's chosen discovery path where possible.
- Filters can be applied, cleared, and revisited without losing the current category.
- Opening and closing a product returns to the same store or result context.
- Add-to-cart confirmation exposes the active cart and its current total without forcing immediate checkout.
- Quantity, add-ons, discounts, delivery, payment, and tips update dependent totals immediately.
- Optional services always expose their selected state and added cost before progression.
- Order actions depend on the current status; unavailable edit, contact, cancel, or rating actions are not presented as successful.
- Chat opened from an order retains the relevant seller or courier and offers a route back to that order.
