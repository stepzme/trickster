# Role

Illustration differentiates product discovery from routine banking and makes abstract offers feel tangible.

# Visual Language

- Polished 3D objects with glossy, metallic, or translucent materials.
- Saturated green and electric-blue lighting on clean backgrounds.
- Cards, coins, service symbols, and product objects rendered as isolated hero forms.
- Soft shadows and restrained depth rather than busy scenes.

# Composition

Place one dominant object inside a rounded campaign card, preserving a clear text and CTA zone. Small 3D icons may label service categories when recognition improves.

# Usage

Use for new products, benefits, campaigns, and onboarding. Keep balances, transfers, payment forms, transaction history, and security controls free of decorative art.

# Guardrails

- Do not place illustration behind amounts or account data.
- Do not mix flat cartoons with the polished 3D material language.
- Keep green and blue aligned with `ui.md` rather than introducing a new palette.
- For a new subject, use one isolated glossy object, soft directional light, and ample copy-safe space so it harmonizes with `ui.md`.
