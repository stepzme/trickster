# Overview

Pure uses an irreverent collage-and-doodle language that makes dating feel candid, provocative, and deliberately unlike polished romantic lifestyle imagery.

# Visual Style

Illustrations combine heavy black ink, loose hand-drawn figures, comic faces, rough stickers, warped patterns, torn-paper edges, and high-contrast editorial collage.

# Composition

Build each screen around one oversized doodle or profile cutout, then layer speech-like scraps, badges, irregular bursts, and patterned fields. Controlled asymmetry is essential.

# Color and Materials

Black and white form the base. Hot fuchsia, lavender, dusty tan, burgundy, coral, and occasional electric blue provide loud but limited accents.
