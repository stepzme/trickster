# Overview

Ozon Fresh prioritizes rapid grocery discovery, basket building, delivery visibility, and lightweight post-delivery feedback.

# Navigation

A five primary destinations anchors Products, Catalog, Ready-made food, Cart, and Profile. A persistent delivery header keeps address and ETA visible while search, categories, and recommendations scroll underneath.

# Core Flows

## First launch and shopping

1. Confirm delivery context, browse image-led categories or search, open a product, adjust quantity inline, and continue to the cart.

## Cart and delivery

1. Review grouped items and recommendations, continue through checkout, then follow the courier status and rate the completed delivery.

# Interaction Patterns

Product quantity changes happen in place; the next commitment remains available; product-detail presentations focus the current item without losing the shopping context.
