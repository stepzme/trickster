# Overview

Simply joins a mobile wallet, card, service payments, transfers, history, bonuses, installments, promotions, and telecom-linked account management.

# Navigation

- Primary navigation switches among Home, Promotions, and Profile.
- Home exposes wallet balance, top-up, payments, history, transfer, card, installment, and bonuses.
- Deep finance tasks use back navigation and focused lists or forms.

# Core Flows

## Pay or transfer

1. Open Payments or Transfer from Home.
2. Search a service or choose phone/card transfer.
3. Enter details, verify identity when required, and confirm.
4. Review receipt or history and optionally save the destination.

## Use card and bonuses

1. Open Simply Card for application, details, PIN, blocking, statement, or wallet linking.
2. Open Bonuses or Promotions to compare benefit categories and partner offers.
3. Follow an offer into the relevant service when needed.

## Manage account

1. Open Profile for identity, applications, security, language, and terms.
2. Contact support or close/sign out through clearly separated destructive actions.

# Interaction Patterns

- Four stable shortcuts reduce navigation for frequent wallet tasks.
- Payments combine search, category shortcuts, saved items, and history.
- Finance forms stay linear and use native verification patterns.
- Promotions are visually richer but return into the same wallet context.
- Profile groups identity, security, other, and support sections.
