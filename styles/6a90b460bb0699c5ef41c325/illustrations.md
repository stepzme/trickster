# Overview

Ozon Job mixes documentary workplace photography with bright 3D editorial graphics. The graphics explain promotions, safety, training, and progress while photography establishes real work environments.

# Visual Style

Use chunky 3D objects and rounded characters with simplified faces, clean plastic materials, and crisp studio lighting. Compose them with parcels, warehouse equipment, tickets, calendars, or money cues. Photography should be bright, candid, and operational rather than aspirational lifestyle imagery.

# Composition

Place a single 3D scene inside a rounded banner or card, usually aligned to the right of text. For onboarding, photography may fill the upper half of the screen. Training thumbnails can use small isometric scenes on saturated gradient fields.

# Color and Materials

Lead with Ozon blue and cyan, supported by magenta, violet, coral, and yellow. Keep backgrounds light and let the graphic carry saturation. Preserve legible contrast for text and status badges.

# Variants and States

Use photography to show people and workplaces; use 3D scenes for abstract benefits, promotions, safety, course topics, and empty states. New artwork should match this rounded, high-saturation object family and never imitate user-facing system icons.

# Avoid

Do not introduce an unrelated illustration style or substitute decorative symbols for source-supported imagery.
