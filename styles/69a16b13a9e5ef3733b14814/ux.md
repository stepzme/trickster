# Overview

Tutu is a multimodal travel marketplace covering flights, hotels, trains, buses, tours, suburban rail, and car rental, with orders, favorites, wallet rewards, support content, and an AI trip assistant.

# Navigation

- A five-item bottom bar covers Search, Orders, Jarvel, Favorites or Info, and Profile.
- A horizontal service rail switches transport and lodging modes above the shared search form.
- Results progress through filters, item details, fare selection, passenger details, and checkout.

# Core Flows

## Find and book travel

1. Choose a service, route, date, and travelers on the home form.
2. Compare results and apply mode-specific filters or map view.
3. Review fare, baggage, cancellation, and traveler details before checkout.

## Book a stay

1. Search destination and dates, then filter or open the map.
2. Inspect photos, reviews, facilities, and room options.
3. Choose an offer, add guest details, and complete booking.

## Use Jarvel

1. Open Jarvel and describe the trip or question.
2. Review the assistant's suggested routes, places, and booking links.
3. Save the resulting trip context and continue to purchase.

# Interaction Patterns

- A single structured form adapts across travel products.
- Purple owns search and purchase; green calls out cashback.
- Sticky bottom actions preserve the current price and next step.
- Editorial and promotional cards support discovery beneath the primary booking task.
