# Overview

Booking supports a broad travel funnel while keeping each category search-first and comparison-led.

# Navigation

Search, Saved, Bookings, and My account are stable. Stays, Flights, Car rental, Taxi, and Attractions share the Search branch.

# Core Flows

## Search and compare

Set destination, dates, and party, then sort, filter, or switch to map. Compare price, rating, distance, availability, and benefits without losing search context.

## Book

1. Open the property or travel product.
2. Review photos, facilities, policies, reviews, and availability.
3. Select an option and enter traveler details.
4. Review cancellation, payment, taxes, and total.
5. Confirm and retain the reservation reference.

## Manage

Bookings groups itinerary, reservation detail, date changes, cancellation, messages, and map context.

# Interaction Patterns

- Keep search criteria editable.
- Show total and cancellation before commitment.
- Preserve Saved across list, map, and detail.
- Separate promotional benefits from contractual terms.
- Confirm destructive reservation changes.
