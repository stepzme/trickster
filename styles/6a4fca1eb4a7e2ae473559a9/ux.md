# Overview

Numo turns ADHD task support into a dark, energetic system of daily planning, voice capture, streaks, team accountability, community discussion, and story-based learning.

# Navigation

Five bottom destinations connect Do, Me, Teams, Tribe, and Hack. A floating microphone and blue add button stay above the bar on the daily task screen.

# Core Flows

## Plan and complete the day

1. Move through onboarding goals and routine preferences.
2. Land on the current day with date strip and routine filters.
3. Add a task by text or voice and place it in Routine, Health, or Relax.
4. Complete work and preserve streak or reward feedback.

## Learn and connect

1. Enter Hacks to choose a themed story collection.
2. Continue the next short lesson or video.
3. Respond to community prompts or move into Tribe and Teams for support.
4. Track progress from Me.

# Interaction Patterns

- Black canvas, condensed white type, hot orange-red, and electric blue create strong motivational contrast.
- Date and task controls stay sparse so a short list never feels like an empty utility.
- Story covers and community content provide the visual density rather than container chrome.
- Voice and add actions float near the thumb and remain more prominent than secondary navigation.
