# Overview

Drinkit connects shop selection, editorial discovery, drink building, cart, payment, pickup tracking, gifts, and post-order review.

# Navigation

The selected shop and profile remain . A taxonomy moves through the feed; cart and active order appear only when relevant.

# Core Flows

## Order a drink

1. Choose a coffee shop.
2. Browse editorial modules or categories.
3. Open a product and configure size or ingredients.
4. Add to cart and confirm shop, payment, and pickup details.
5. Track accepted, preparing, and ready states.

## Engage after purchase

1. Open the current order card.
2. Review items and pickup code.
3. Tip the barista, open a prediction, or share it.
4. Leave a product or order review.

# Interaction Patterns

- Keep shop availability visible before purchase.
- Separate editorial discovery from transactional sheets.
- Preserve builder choices when returning to a product.
- Use character moments after core status is clear.
- Make ingredients and allergens easy to reach.
