# Overview

hh job uses friendly flat character scenes and editorial career imagery for onboarding, product updates, empty states, and guidance.

# Visual Style

Characters use simplified anatomy, clean outlines, limited shading, and bright clothing or props. Scenes are optimistic, direct, and less formal than the job cards around them.

# Composition

Center one character and one recognizable career action inside a white card with generous space for a headline and CTA. Career articles may use a wider conceptual image.

# Color

Use hh blue with controlled red, green, orange, purple, and neutral skin or clothing tones against white or pale backgrounds.

# Usage

Use illustrations for onboarding, empty states, profile prompts, what-is-new messages, and career education. Keep vacancy results, employer facts, salary, and applications text-led.
