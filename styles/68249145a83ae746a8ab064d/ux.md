# Overview

Dodo Pizza guides users from address and menu discovery through customization, cart, delivery, payment, tracking, and rating.

# Navigation

Address anchors the top of the menu. Stories and category rails provide discovery, while cart, profile, and current-order entry points appear contextually.

# Core Flows

## Order food

1. Set or confirm delivery address.
2. Browse categories, stories, or product cards.
3. Open a product and configure size, options, or combo.
4. Add to cart and review add-ons, promo, and minimum order.
5. Choose delivery time and payment, then confirm.

## Track and close an order

1. Open current order status.
2. Follow accepted, kitchen, courier, and delivered stages.
3. Contact support or courier when available.
4. Rate the completed order.

# Interaction Patterns

- Ask for address before presenting availability.
- Preserve cart and modifiers while browsing.
- Show minimum order and total continuously.
- Use clear stages for live order status.
- Keep food media secondary to task controls during checkout.
