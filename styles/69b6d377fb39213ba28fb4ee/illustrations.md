# Overview

Telegram uses expressive mascot and emoji-like illustrations for empty states, onboarding, calls, gifts, and lightweight education.

# Visual Style

Characters are bright, rounded, and glossy with sticker-like outlines and playful facial expressions. Objects use simple 3D volume, clean highlights, and high legibility at small sizes.

# Composition

Center one character or compact scene above a short message and optional blue action. Keep the surrounding screen very quiet.

# Color

Use saturated yellow, blue, coral, and green with white highlights. The character palette may be playful, but the accompanying action remains Telegram blue.

# Usage

Use for empty chats, empty calls, onboarding, gifts, achievements, and supportive explanations. Do not place mascots inside dense chat lists or settings rows.
