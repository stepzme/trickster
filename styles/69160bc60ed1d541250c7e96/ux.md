# Overview

Windy.app personalizes weather around outdoor sports, then combines a forecast dashboard, saved spots, map layers, routes, community content, spot detail, and profile services.

# Navigation

- Home summarizes the current spot, multi-day outlook, nearest spot, map, favorites, community, and route forecast.
- A side menu opens Profile, Pro, offline mode, settings, guides, care, and tips.
- Weather Map and Spot Forecast are separate deep tools with their own layer and timeline controls.

# Core Flows

## Personalize and find a spot

1. Select sport, activity frequency, units, and location access during onboarding.
2. Review the nearest recommended spot and add it to Favorites.
3. Enter Home with personalized nearby and saved content.

## Inspect a forecast

1. Open a spot or Weather Map.
2. Choose time, model, layer, wind barbs, fronts, icons, or map type.
3. Compare current conditions, timeline, archive, services, and notification options.

## Plan and share activity

1. Create or upload a route from Home.
2. Review forecast along the path and nearby spots.
3. Save the route or use Community and spot services to prepare the activity.
