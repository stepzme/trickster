# Overview

Ostrovok uses playful 3D travel objects for campaigns and empty states, including luggage, cars, travel gifts, tickets, and paper-like destination characters.

# Visual Style

Mix clean 3D product rendering with crafted paper or folded-texture objects. Use rounded forms, bright brand color, and soft daylight shadows.

# Composition

Center a small travel scene on white for empty states, or place an oversized character or object inside a bounded campaign card with a protected text zone.

# Color

Anchor art in lime green and royal blue, then add white, silver, cyan, and small coral accents. Avoid muddy neutrals or heavy outlines.

# Usage

Use illustration for empty trips, transfer promotion, booking celebration, rewards, and seasonal campaigns. Continue to use real photography for destinations and properties.
