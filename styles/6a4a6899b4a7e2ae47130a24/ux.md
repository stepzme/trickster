# Overview

eGov Mobile combines identity, digital documents, government services, QR, messages, request history, and personal records.

# Navigation

Home, eGov QR, Services, Messages, and Profile remain stable. Search and assistant bridge service discovery; documents and banners deep-link contextually.

# Core Flows

## Receive a service

1. Find a service through search, category, situation, or recommendation.
2. Review requirements and FAQ.
3. Authenticate and confirm applicant data.
4. Complete fields, attach documents, and sign when required.
5. Submit and follow request status and resulting documents.

## Use a digital document

1. Open Digital documents.
2. Switch between personal and family.
3. Open a document and verify current state.
4. Share through the provided secure action.

# Interaction Patterns

- Keep applicant identity and request number visible.
- Explain authentication and signature before consent.
- Preserve form progress where permitted.
- Pair status with resulting documents.
- Keep legal and privacy detail reachable.
