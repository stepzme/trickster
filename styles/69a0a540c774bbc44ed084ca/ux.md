# Overview

My O! + Bank is a Kyrgyzstan super app spanning mobile service, O!Money, banking, rewards, marketplace, travel, gifts, credits, deposits, and home internet.

# Navigation

A four-item dock connects Home, Payments, History, and More. A central QR scanner overlaps the dock, while search, notifications, stories, and the active subscriber remain available .

# Core Flows

## Manage connectivity and money

1. Review mobile balance, data, minutes, tariff, and connected numbers.
2. Top up, reconnect a plan, manage services, or open detailed usage.
3. Enter O!Money or O!Bank for cards, accounts, transfers, credit, deposits, and bonuses.
4. Confirm the transaction and retain a receipt or reusable favorite.

## Shop in O!Market

1. Enter the marketplace from Home or the market tab.
2. Browse stores, announcements, categories, campaigns, or search.
3. Compare product cards, financing terms, and fulfillment offers.
4. Add to cart and complete the order inside the market context.

# Interaction Patterns

- Magenta identifies the O! ecosystem, QR scan, active navigation, and selected offers.
- Product and financial lists stay dense but use clear section labels and aligned values.
- The dock and QR scanner persist across long home and marketplace content.
