# Overview

Skyeng combines self-study, teacher products, AI lessons, speaking scenarios, vocabulary, homework, schedule, messages, courses, and settings.

# Navigation

- Five bottom tabs lead to Home, Homework, Schedule, Messages, and Practice.
- Vocabulary, course catalog, help, gifts, and profile open from contextual controls.
- Practice groups daily assignments, AI teacher, situations, video, and audiobooks.

# Core Flows

## Start learning

1. Choose self-study or teacher-led use during onboarding.
2. Review a recommended course or daily task.
3. Start the lesson and move through numbered steps.
4. Review completion or speaking results.

## Practice speaking

1. Choose an AI teacher or real-life situation.
2. Test the microphone and speech recognition.
3. Record, listen, read feedback, and retry.
4. Finish with skill results and next recommendations.

## Learn vocabulary

1. Search or open a word list.
2. Review definition, pronunciation, examples, and learning state.
3. Mark known, add, remove, report, or continue.

# Interaction Patterns

- Lesson progress stays visible across focused steps.
- Audio controls pair waveform, transcript, and retry.
- Discovery uses browsing sections; lessons collapse to one task at a time.
