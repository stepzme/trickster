# Overview

Dixy connects grocery discovery, loyalty, delivery, coupons, cart, checkout, tracking, and repeat purchase in one retail loop.

# Navigation

Home, Catalog, seasonal hub, Promotions, and Profile remain stable; search, scan, address, loyalty, and cart provide direct task entry.

# Core Flows

## Shop for delivery

1. Confirm the delivery address.
2. Search, scan, browse, or filter products.
3. Add items and adjust quantities in Cart.
4. Review substitutions, discounts, fees, and total.
5. Choose payment, confirm, and follow assembly and delivery.

## Use loyalty

1. Open the loyalty card.
2. Present or copy the barcode for discount and coin use.
3. Review tier, cashback, coins, coupons, and categories.
4. Return to shopping with benefits reflected in price.

# Interaction Patterns

- Keep address and fulfillment context persistent.
- Preserve the cart while browsing campaigns and categories.
- Show price changes after packing transparently.
- Keep cancellation and support reachable from order detail.
