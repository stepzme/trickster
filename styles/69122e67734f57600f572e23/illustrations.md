# Overview

Raiffeisen uses friendly editorial illustrations to explain onboarding, security, cashback, savings, subscriptions, and feature education. They soften otherwise utilitarian banking tasks without replacing core financial information.

# Visual Style

- Fine graphite outlines with intentionally loose, hand-drawn contours.
- Flat pastel fills in yellow, mint, peach, lavender, and pale blue.
- Simplified people, money objects, cards, locks, devices, and domestic scenes.
- Limited shading; depth comes from overlaps and occasional soft color blocks.

# Composition

- Onboarding illustrations sit as one centered hero above the primary message.
- Educational stories use cropped scenes inside small rounded horizontal cards.
- Larger profile and product banners let the artwork bleed behind a white content sheet.
- Keep one focal action or metaphor per image and leave clear space for adjacent copy.

# Color and Materials

Use the yellow brand accent as the anchor, then add one or two soft supporting hues from the UI palette. Keep black outlines and avoid saturated full-spectrum scenes.

# Variants and States

Use illustrations for explanation, reassurance, and lightweight promotion. Do not use them behind transaction rows, numeric forms, account balances, or other data that needs immediate scanning.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the documented image language with unrelated stock art, arbitrary symbols, or a denser composition.
