# Overview

EMIAS.INFO connects appointment booking, referrals, prescriptions, documents, medical records, health diary, relatives, and access control.

# Navigation

Home, Medical record, and More stay in primary navigation. Patient and policy context remain in contextual controls.

# Core Flows

## Book a doctor

1. Choose a goal or tap Book.
2. Select referral or specialty, clinic, doctor, date, and time.
3. Review and confirm the booking.
4. Add it to the calendar or rate the process.
5. Reopen to reschedule or cancel.

## Review medical history

1. Open Medical record.
2. Choose appointments, tests, studies, prescriptions, vaccination, or documents.
3. Open an item and review date, provider, and result.
4. Add user-supplied information only in supported sections.

# Interaction Patterns

- Keep patient and policy context persistent.
- Require explicit confirmation for cancel or deletion.
- Distinguish official records from user-entered data.
- Keep no-data states specific.
- Make access sharing and privacy reversible.
