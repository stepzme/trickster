# Overview

Wink is a media hub for films, series, television, music, books, sport, and kids content. It supports browsing and lets a title move directly into watching, saving, downloading, or subscribing.

# Navigation

- A persistent five-item bar separates Main, TV channels, Stories, Music, and More.
- Search, trends, and profile remain .
- Title pages keep the watch action available while details, trailers, episodes, and recommendations scroll beneath it.

# Core Flows

## Discover and watch

1. Browse editorial collections or search for a title.
2. Open the title page to check synopsis, rating, age, trailer, and related content.
3. Watch immediately, choose an episode, or resolve subscription access.

## Save for later

1. Add a title to favorites, reminders, or downloads.
2. Return through My Wink, Continue watching, Favorites, or Downloads.
3. Resume from the saved position with minimal setup.

## Switch media modes

1. Use primary navigation to enter live TV, short stories, or music.
2. Filter the chosen catalog and open a channel, track, concert, radio, or audiobook.
3. Keep playback controls and account access consistent across modes.

# Interaction Patterns

- A persistent watch action anchors title pages; secondary save and share actions sit together.
- Subscription, promo code, and payment steps appear only when access is required.
- Profile switching distinguishes primary, additional, and kids viewing contexts.
