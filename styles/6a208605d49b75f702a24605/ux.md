# Overview

OneTwoTrip is a multi-product travel service for flights, hotels, trains, buses, cars, eSIM, orders, cashback, chat, and destination inspiration.

# Navigation

Home, Cashback, Orders, Chats, and Profile are the primary destinations. A travel-product switcher gives Air, Hotels, Train tickets, Buses, and Cars equal entry points in the current context of Home.

# Core Flows

## Search and book a flight

1. Enter origin, destination, dates, passengers, and trip type.
2. Review calendar prices and select dates.
3. Compare itineraries, baggage, stops, timing, and flexible filters.
4. Choose a fare, provide traveler and payment details, then retain the order.

## Find a hotel

1. Enter destination, dates, and guests.
2. Compare image-led cards, rating, board, distance, cashback, and total price.
3. Filter, open on map, or choose a room.
4. Confirm traveler details and payment, then manage the stay from Orders.

# Interaction Patterns

- Dense results align times, baggage, price, rating, and cashback for comparison.
- Filters remain available throughout long flight and hotel lists.
