# Overview

Kompanion is a retail banking app for cards, wallets, payments, transfers, savings, loans, deposits, currency exchange, cashback, and service management.

# Navigation

Five bottom destinations cover Home, Payments, QR, History, and Menu. Home prioritizes cards and frequent transfers; search and notifications remain in the header.

# Core Flows

## Enter securely

1. Review and accept data-security terms.
2. Enter a phone number and verification code.
3. Complete identity checks when required.
4. Land on the banking dashboard.

## Manage a card

1. Select a card from the home carousel.
2. Review balance and common actions.
3. Open history, details, statements, or security settings.

## Transfer by phone

1. Choose phone transfer from Home.
2. Enter the recipient and select the destination bank.
3. Set amount and confirm.
4. Review success, repeat, save, or open the receipt.

# Interaction Patterns

- Card imagery and balances lead the dashboard.
- Frequent actions use compact white tiles with colored icons.
- Blue full-width buttons confirm financial operations.
- Success screens expose receipt, save, and repeat actions before returning home.
