# Overview

Chizhik has a stable authored character system centered on a compact black bird, saturated yellow fields, white speech-bubble shapes, and bold graphic props. It supports onboarding, home, stores, map pins, empty/status states, and promotions while product shopping remains led by real packshots. New authored assets must be created with the available image-generation model, visually approved before integration, and inserted as approved image assets; an illustration-required screen is incomplete until that asset is visible.

# Visual Style

Use a compact bird character with a strong black silhouette, sharp but friendly simplified features, and restrained white/red detail. The drawing is flat, graphic, and high contrast, with little or no shading. Speech bubbles, hearts, store/map markers, confetti-like accents, and simple geometric props share the same bold silhouette logic. The result should feel like authored retail poster art, not a soft generic cartoon or realistic mascot render.

# Composition

Place one bird pose or one bird-derived motif as the focal object against a broad yellow or white field. On onboarding and home surfaces, pair the mascot with a very large white speech bubble and a protected area for a short heavy headline. Empty/status states use a smaller centered bird or heart/bird motif with generous negative space. Map variants reduce the silhouette into a clear high-contrast pin. Promotional cards may combine one mascot pose with bounded product photography, but the bird, headline, CTA, and packshot must not overlap.

# Color and Materials

Saturated yellow, black, and white are the structural palette; red may appear as a small character, heart, warning, or promotional accent. Use crisp flat fills, strong edges, and minimal shading. Surfaces read like screen-printed or poster-like graphics, not glossy 3D toys, watercolor, clay, glass, or soft corporate gradients. The yellow and black must match the dominant roles established in `ui.md`.

# Variants and States

Launch and onboarding use the largest mascot and speech-bubble treatment. Home and promotional variants may add a bold prop, bounded product image, or simple geometric accent. Store and map variants compress the bird into a readable pin or compact badge. Empty, cancelled, favorite, and other status variants pair one clear expression or heart/bird motif with explicit text. Product cards, basket rows, checkout fields, prices, and order data remain photo- or data-led.

# Avoid

- Do not draw required mascot art with SwiftUI shapes, Canvas, programmatic vectors, SF Symbols, or emoji.
- Do not integrate generated artwork before visual review and explicit approval.
- Do not replace product packshots with mascot drawings or illustrated groceries.
- Do not change the bird into a rounded generic animal, photorealistic bird, glossy 3D figure, or detailed storybook character.
- Do not weaken the black silhouette with thin outlines, pastel fills, texture, or complex shading.
- Do not place the mascot behind prices, quantities, form labels, or long copy.
- Do not overcrowd a speech bubble or status scene with multiple characters and decorative text.
- Do not use blue/purple corporate gradients that detach the illustration from the yellow-white-black system.
