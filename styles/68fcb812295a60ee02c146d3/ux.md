# Overview

Otello is a hotel and apartment service organized around destination search, inspirational collections, Super Prices, map discovery, saved properties, bookings, and profile.

# Navigation

Search, Super Prices, Favorites, Bookings, and Profile form the bottom bar. Search remains pinned near the top of Home and over map or result contexts.

# Core Flows

## Discover and choose a stay

1. Search a city, hotel, or destination or enter through an editorial collection.
2. Compare Super Price shelves, destination discounts, or map clusters.
3. Open a property and review photography, rating, reviews, location, room, and price.
4. Choose dates and a rate, then confirm the reservation.

## Revisit bookings

1. Open Active, Past, or Cancelled bookings.
2. Sign in or locate a reservation when no history is attached.
3. Review the stay and complete any remaining action.
4. Start a new search from an empty or completed state.

# Interaction Patterns

- Bright green marks search, discounts, savings, and booking actions.
- Property photography leads; black rating and discount badges remain tightly attached.
- Horizontal hotel rails support comparison, while the map clusters places by neighborhood.
- Empty booking states use one friendly object and a direct recovery action.
