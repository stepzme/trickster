# Overview

Rostic's organizes restaurant selection, menu discovery, product customization, checkout, order tracking, promotions, and account history around a short mobile ordering loop.

# Navigation

- A four-item primary navigation anchors Home, Menu, Promotions, and Profile.
- Delivery or restaurant choice appears before ordering and remains accessible from the top of the experience.
- Product details and checkout open as focused layers with a persistent bottom action.

# Core Flows

## Choose and order

1. Select delivery or pickup and confirm a restaurant or address.
2. Browse categories and open a product.
3. Adjust ingredients, quantity, or combo options.
4. Review the cart, enter fulfillment details, and confirm payment.
5. Follow the order status and later open history or leave a review.

## Discover promotions

1. Browse stories or the promotions feed.
2. Open an offer and review its conditions.
3. Continue into the relevant catalog or product while preserving location context.

## Manage account

1. Sign in from Profile.
2. Review points, order history, payment methods, settings, or support.
3. Reorder or resolve an order from the same account context.

# Interaction Patterns

- Products support direct add, while deeper customization opens the product sheet.
- Quantity controls replace the add action after selection.
- Promotional content links back into the same catalog rather than creating a separate purchase model.
