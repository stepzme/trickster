# Overview

Lovi is an AI skincare companion that builds a personalized routine, tracks skin, scans faces and products, explains ingredient fit, recommends cosmetics, and provides focused insights.

# Navigation

Five fixed destinations cover Today, Products, New Scan, Insights, and Sunshine assistant. Premium and the smiling assistant remain accessible as floating controls above the tab bar.

# Core Flows

## Personalize and plan

1. Complete an extended profile covering age, sensitivity, target areas, goals, and current routine.
2. Enter the seven-day daily plan and skin diary.
3. Review educational tasks and track progress.

## Scan and evaluate

1. Choose face or product/food scanning.
2. Capture or select the subject.
3. Review fit, safety, effectiveness, ingredients, and assistant explanation.
4. Save, ask a follow-up, or open the product purchase destination.

# Interaction Patterns

- Soft gradient haze and white glassy cards make dense personalization feel calm.
- Green fit scores and checkmarks summarize suitability.
- The smiling assistant opens contextual explanations without replacing the current screen.
- Scan, favorite, assistant, and premium controls float above content with soft shadows.
