# Overview

Wolt uses a secondary authored illustration language for onboarding, address or location guidance, rewards, subscription promotion, and selected profile moments. Cheerful mascot characters and simplified objects make these moments warmer without replacing the product photography that dominates everyday commerce. When this language is required in an adapted product, generate finished raster artwork with an image-generation model and obtain explicit approval before integrating it; do not recreate it from SwiftUI shapes or symbol assemblies.

# Visual Style

The artwork is rounded, friendly, high-saturation, and character-led. Mascots and objects use simplified proportions, soft dimensional modeling, clean silhouettes, and gentle lighting rather than flat system-symbol geometry. Scenes feel polished and playful, with limited supporting props and no photorealistic human treatment. Recognizable motifs include the smiling mascot, reward-like objects, compact city or storefront scenes, and celebratory promotional vignettes.

# Composition

Illustration usually becomes the main visual mass of a focused onboarding or promotional region while leaving a clean area for a short title, supporting line, and action. A single mascot or compact scene is centered or slightly offset against a cyan, white, or warm colored field. Silhouettes remain readable at phone width, key characters are not clipped, and soft contact shadows ground objects without making the composition heavy.

Keep illustration out of dense catalog grids and transaction rows. It belongs in a dedicated upper field, contained card, or focused empty/promo module; food and product photography retains priority wherever real inventory is represented.

# Color and Materials

The palette stays bright and coordinated with the cyan UI accent, adding warm orange, yellow, green, purple, or skin-tone-like object colors as bounded highlights. Backgrounds are clean cyan, white, pale tints, or controlled warm gradients. Materials are smooth and softly dimensional, with gentle highlights and shadows, no gritty texture, and enough contrast to separate the focal character from the stage.

# Variants and States

Observed variants include a mascot-led splash or onboarding moment, an authored character/object onboarding scene, a standalone city or storefront-style address scene, rewards/profile cards, and a polished subscription promotional scene. The scale and number of props may change by role, but rounded forms, saturated color, simple staging, and a single clear focal point remain constant.

# Avoid

- Do not draw production illustrations with SwiftUI shapes, SF Symbols, emoji, or assembled interface primitives.
- Do not integrate generated artwork before the user explicitly approves the image itself.
- Do not replace food, merchant, map, or product photography with mascot illustration.
- Do not mix unrelated mascot styles, photorealistic people, sketchy line art, or generic corporate vector scenes.
- Do not use dense scenery, gritty texture, hard shadows, muted corporate palettes, or tiny focal characters.
- Do not crop faces, defining mascot features, or primary objects at the edge of a card.
