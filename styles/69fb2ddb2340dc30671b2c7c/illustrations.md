# Overview

Tiimo uses a gentle illustration system for tasks, reflection, learning, and supportive guidance.

# Visual Style

Small rounded avatars and object icons use soft pastels and simple facial expressions. Larger learning art uses loose black linework with lavender fields and occasional hand-drawn sparkle marks.

# Composition

Place tiny icons inside task rows, one avatar beside the floating dock, and larger line art inside a generous rounded card.

# Color and Materials

Use lavender, lilac, mint, pale aqua, soft yellow, and blush. Keep outlines dark and contrast calm.

# Variants and States

Use for routine identity, task visuals, mood, trophies, AI support, and educational cards. Do not overwhelm planning rows with large illustrations.

# Production Requirements

Use only the formats and crops supported by the implemented placement. Where the documented imagery is compositionally important, temporary placeholders must preserve its placement, crop, scale, negative space, and approximate visual weight until final art exists. No unobserved light/dark variants or export ratios are prescribed.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the documented image language with unrelated stock art, arbitrary symbols, or a denser composition.
