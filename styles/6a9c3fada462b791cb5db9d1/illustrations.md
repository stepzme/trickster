# Overview

Omio has an independently confirmed authored illustration system across splash, onboarding, home search, and promotional states. It is a soft travel-collage language used to establish destination mood before the app switches to dense functional booking surfaces.

Production contract: generate new artwork with an image-generation model, obtain explicit visual approval from the user, then integrate approved raster assets. Do not replace authored artwork with SwiftUI shapes, SF Symbols, emoji, icon-only placeholders, or any programmatic substitutes.

# Visual Style

Use softly rendered scenic travel artwork with low-contrast gradients, hazy blue-violet atmosphere, blush/coral light, and simplified transport objects. Trains, buses, aircraft, ferries, bridges, distant stations, hills, clouds, and birds are lightly dimensional with softened edges and minimal hard outlines. The art should feel calm, panoramic, and brand-authored rather than stock-like.

# Composition

The stable composition is a low horizon of travel objects beneath open sky. Splash and onboarding screens place the logo or centered text over the atmospheric field, with transport elements concentrated in the lower third. Home search uses the artwork as a full-width top header, cropped behind the search panel. Promotional art appears inside white or gradient cards and may focus on one device, badge, ticket, or savings motif.

# Color and Materials

The illustration palette extends the UI palette: navy and periwinkle for depth, blush and coral for warmth, pale cyan highlights, and white atmospheric details. Contrast stays restrained in scenery so coral actions and navy UI text remain dominant. Materials are matte and softly shaded, not glossy 3D.

# Variants and States

Observed variants include splash, privacy/onboarding backgrounds, discovery home headers, savings/promotional cards, and modal offer artwork. Functional comparison lists, passenger forms, billing, payment, filters, hotel detail text, and contractual totals should remain on plain UI surfaces rather than illustrated backgrounds.

# Avoid

Do not use flat SF Symbols, emoji, SwiftUI vector shapes, random travel clip art, stock photography as a substitute for the authored collage, hard black outlines, saturated cartoon palettes, or decorative artwork that hides route, fare, form, or policy information. New assets must follow the production contract: image-generation model -> explicit visual approval from the user -> approved raster asset integration.
