# Overview

Kuper uses expressive object art for empty states and department shortcuts while keeping products and merchants photography-led.

# Visual Style

The signature empty state is a bold outlined shopping bag with a bright green panel and a dry, skeptical face. Department art uses isolated, softly modeled objects with clean silhouettes and restrained shadows.

# Composition

Center a single large character-object above a short message and action for empty states. In grids, crop one object low inside a pale rounded tile with ample negative space.

# Color and Materials

Use black outlines, white space, and electric green as the core language. Department objects may retain natural colors, but their backgrounds remain pale and quiet.

# Variants and States

Reserve character art for empty and feedback states; use isolated objects for navigation and departments.

# Production Requirements

Exact export formats, aspect ratios, animation behavior, and appearance variants were not documented. Where imagery is compositionally important, temporary placeholders must preserve its documented scale, placement, crop, and visual weight until final art exists.

# Avoid

Do not introduce an unrelated illustration style or substitute decorative symbols for source-supported imagery.

# Known Gaps

Export specifications, animation rules, safe-crop measurements, and unobserved state variants were not available in the reviewed source.
