# Overview

Raiffeisen is a task-first banking app that combines accounts, fast transfers, payments, history, support, and product discovery. The interface keeps high-frequency actions .

# Navigation

- Five primary navigation destinations lead to Home, History, Payments, Chat, and More.
- Home starts with total balance, four primary shortcuts, educational stories, and account cards.
- Profile and notifications are available from controls in contextual controls.
- Product, payment, and settings groups open as focused lists or modals.

# Core Flows

## Register and order a card

1. Review the card offer during onboarding.
2. Choose an identity route: passport scan, manual form, office, or supported government profile.
3. Complete the form and choose delivery or office collection.
4. Review meeting details and receive the prepared-card state.

## Transfer money

1. Start from a Home shortcut or the Payments tab.
2. Choose phone, own accounts, another bank, card, or QR.
3. Select source and recipient, then enter the amount on the numeric step.
4. Confirm and return to the updated balance or transaction detail.

## Review finances and products

1. Open History to review transactions and income-versus-expense summaries.
2. Apply filters or open a transaction to change its category and expense inclusion.
3. Open All products to manage accounts or begin a new card, savings, investment, or loan flow.

# Interaction Patterns

- Short tutorial bubbles point to unfamiliar controls without blocking the whole screen.
- Empty states use a single friendly symbol and one explanatory sentence.
