# Overview

Pyaterochka combines loyalty, coupons, games, catalog browsing, grocery delivery or pickup, cart editing, checkout, and post-order additions.

# Navigation

A four-item primary navigation anchors Home, Catalog, Contact us, and Profile. Catalog keeps delivery mode, address, search, and scan entry visible before product categories.

# Core Flows

## Discover and shop

1. Enter through promotions, favorites, past purchases, category tiles, search, or barcode scan; filter results, open a product, and add or adjust items.

## Checkout and order

1. Review the cart and combo recommendations, handle unavailable items, sign in if needed, apply a promo code, choose payment, place the order, and optionally add items before cutoff.
