# Overview

Zopa uses small friendly botanical and financial illustrations to warm savings, growth, and promotional cards.

# Visual Style

Use flat vector scenes with rounded forms, subtle paper-like texture, simple plants, and small floating financial objects.

# Composition

Place one compact scene at the edge or lower corner of a card, preserving open space for amounts and action copy.

# Color

Use forest green and mint with restrained peach, coral, yellow, and cream accents.

# Usage

Use illustration for savings education, growth offers, benefits, and empty states; keep transfers and verification purely functional. New art should remain harmonious with `ui.md`.
