# Overview

Pyaterochka uses cheerful dimensional mascots and retail pictograms alongside product photography and promotional banners.

# Visual Style

Characters are rounded, glossy, brightly colored, and toy-like, with exaggerated eyes and friendly expressions. Category icons use the same soft 3D material and isolated-object treatment.

# Composition

Place one or two mascots inside white or green promotional panels, leaving room for a bold offer and a clear action. Smaller pictograms sit above short category labels.

# Color and Materials

Bright brand green and red anchor the system; yellow, orange, pink, mint, and sky blue add playful retail variety.

# Variants and States

Use mascots for loyalty, games, coupons, onboarding, and promotional encouragement. Keep catalog comparison and checkout photography-led so product and price remain authoritative.

# Production Requirements

Preserve the documented placement, crop, negative space, and visual weight in production exports. Before final art exists, placeholders must preserve those same compositional properties.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the observed imagery language with unrelated stock art or arbitrary symbols.
- Do not change the documented crop, density, or relationship to nearby text.

# Known Gaps

Animation, export dimensions, safe-crop specifications, appearance variants, and unshown state treatments were not documented.
