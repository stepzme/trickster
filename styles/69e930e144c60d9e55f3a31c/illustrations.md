# Overview

Simbank uses a recurring authored mascot to explain financial concepts, anchor offers, and give empty states a friendly focal point. The system is distinct from functional icons, payment marks, photography, charts, gradients, story thumbnails, and decorative emoji.

# Visual Style

Create flat vector-like cartoon scenes around a compact character with an oversized purple circular or hood-like head, a small simplified body, short limbs, and one directly relevant prop. Use soft closed geometry, mild asymmetry, a thin dark expressive contour, minimal interior detail, mostly flat fills, and only subtle shadowing. The character can recline, sit, hold an object, or interact with a safe, purchase, drink, or savings object, but must remain recognizably part of one mascot family.

Every new illustration must be generated with an image-generation model from this language. It must receive separate visual approval before interface integration, then be integrated as the approved raster asset.

# Composition

Treat the art as a medium spot illustration, usually centered above concise explanatory copy or placed beside a single offer. It should occupy roughly 15–30% of viewport height, preserve ample clean space around the silhouette, and remain legible at phone scale. Card variants may be smaller, but the full character or intended crop must still read clearly. Do not bleed the mascot edge-to-edge or place it behind balances, transaction rows, controls, or dense charts.

# Color and Materials

Purple is the mascot anchor, paired with black outlines and white face or body details. Props may introduce restrained yellow, green, gray, pink, or pale blue that relates to the surrounding pastel UI. Keep fills matte and mostly flat; depth comes from overlap, contour, and a restrained soft shadow rather than realistic light, texture, glass, or 3D material.

# Variants and States

- Onboarding and celebration may show an upright festive character with a drink or dressed-up detail.
- Empty states use a relaxed or reclining pose and minimal supporting props.
- Financial education pairs the mascot with one literal concept such as a safe, deposit, purchase, or savings object.
- Offer and referral heroes may use a seated character or a slightly wider scene while preserving the same proportions and contour language.
- No stable illustrated error variant was observed; do not invent one from this source.

# Avoid

- Do not draw or approximate the illustration with SwiftUI shapes, Canvas, programmatic paths or gradients, SF Symbols, emoji, text glyphs, or assembled UI icons.
- Do not integrate an unapproved generation, vector draft, screenshot crop, or placeholder; use the visually approved raster asset.
- Do not substitute stock cartoons, realistic photography, 3D emoji, glossy clay renders, or unrelated anime characters.
- Do not confuse Visa or bank marks, service icons, charts, story art, or decorative gradients with the mascot system.
- Do not overload a scene with several concepts, dense backgrounds, long copy, or edge-to-edge scenery.
