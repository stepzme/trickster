# Overview

Revolut is a modular financial hub for balances, cards, investing, payments, rewards, and plan management. A customizable home feed surfaces the current account first, while specialized tabs preserve their own task hierarchy and metrics.

# Navigation

- Four persistent tabs lead to Home, Invest, Payments, and RevPoints.
- Search remains , beside contextual analytics or card controls.
- Home is a stack of configurable widgets for accounts, actions, transactions, spending, and watchlists.
- Profile collects security, appearance, documents, notifications, plan, and invitations.

# Core Flows

## Manage an account

1. Choose an account from the balance carousel.
2. Use Add money, Move, Details, or More for the current account.
3. Pick a funding method or review account details and statements.
4. Return to Home, where the balance and latest transaction update in context.

## Invest

1. Open Invest to review the portfolio, news, watchlist, and market events.
2. Select an asset for overview, course, indicators, comparison, or price alerts.
3. Return to portfolio analytics for value, performance, profit and loss, and allocation.

## Pay and collaborate

1. Open Payments and choose a recent contact, scheduled transfer, request, or new payment.
2. Select Revolut, bank account, card, code, international, or payment-link routing.
3. Enter amount and recipient details, then review the resulting transfer thread or detail.
4. Optional groups support shared bills, messages, members, and custom backgrounds.

# Interaction Patterns

- Account context is selected before actions, reducing repeated source-account choices.
- Sheets handle bounded choices such as funding method, account selection, and more actions.
- Appearance and plan settings change presentation without changing the navigation model.
