# Overview

Trainline combines travel photography with a compact service-illustration system used for reassurance, education, and onboarding.

# Visual Style

Illustrations use thick dark outlines, flat mint, cyan, lime, and purple fills, and simple objects such as calendars, stars, locks, tickets, currencies, and help bubbles. Onboarding also places simplified product UI inside rounded photographic travel frames.

# Composition

Use one centered object or symbol per rounded square tile. Keep generous breathing room, a clear silhouette, and minimal internal detail. Larger onboarding compositions may layer a single UI card over a softly cropped journey photograph.

# Color

Anchor functional illustration in mint and teal, then use cyan, lime, or purple for one secondary idea. Keep outlines near-black and backgrounds pale enough for text and icons to remain separate.

# Usage

Apply this language to service benefits, trust, savings, ticket education, and onboarding. Use real photography for destination emotion; keep booking forms, timetables, conditions, and payment surfaces free of decorative artwork.
