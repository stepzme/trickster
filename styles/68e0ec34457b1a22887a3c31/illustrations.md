# Overview

List.am uses a limited but repeatable authored illustration language for verification, account gates, and empty or helper states. These visuals are secondary to listing photography, but they provide the main visual mass when marketplace content is absent. Promotional campaign tiles form a related but more dimensional family and should not be treated as interchangeable with the service illustrations.

# Visual Style

Service illustrations use thin dark outlines, flat saturated blue fills, simple geometric construction, and sparse accent colors. Shapes are clean and lightly playful, with occasional small sparkles or motion marks and limited character presence. Promotional tiles may use rounded toy-like objects with soft dimensional shading, but listing photos, maps, merchant logos, and functional category icons are not part of this authored illustration system.

Create required final artwork with an image-generation model. Present the generated image for explicit approval before integrating it into the app. Do not recreate the illustration with SwiftUI shapes, SF Symbols, emoji, or improvised code-drawn geometry.

# Composition

For empty, verification, and gated states, use one centered illustration above a short text block and primary action. The art should occupy a substantial but contained region, leaving broad white negative space and a clear vertical reading order. Small sparkle details may extend around the central object without becoming background texture. In promotional tiles, keep one contained object as the focal point inside a pastel rounded field with minimal adjacent copy; never let promotional art compete with real listing imagery in a content grid.

# Color and Materials

Anchor the service family in the same saturated blue used by `ui.md`, with white space, dark outlines, and restrained secondary accents such as soft yellow, red, mint, or lilac. Keep fills mostly flat and edges crisp. Promotional objects may add gentle gradients, soft highlights, and shallow shadows, but should still feel compact and clean rather than photorealistic or glossy. Preserve sufficient contrast against the white or pastel host surface.

# Variants and States

Verification and permission-like gates use a single clear symbolic scene. Empty states use a similarly scaled object or simple character scene with less visual urgency. Helper states may reduce the art to a compact spot illustration. Promotional variants can become more dimensional and colorful inside their own pastel tile, while retaining the interface blue as a connecting accent. Generate and approve each materially different state before integration rather than deriving it from code primitives.

# Avoid

- Do not substitute stock photography, clip art, emoji, or unrelated 3D packs.
- Do not draw final illustrations with SwiftUI shapes or assemble them from SF Symbols.
- Do not integrate generated artwork before explicit visual approval.
- Do not treat seller photos, maps, merchant logos, or category icons as illustration variants.
- Do not fill the background with decorative texture or dense scenery.
- Do not crop the central object tightly or shrink it into an insignificant icon.
- Do not mix the flat outlined service family and dimensional promotional objects inside one scene.
