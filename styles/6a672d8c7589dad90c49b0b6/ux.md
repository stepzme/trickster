# Overview

Suno combines AI song creation, social music discovery, playback, library management, publishing, and profile tools.

# Navigation

- Five primary destinations cover Home, Explore, Create, Library, and Profile.
- Create remains a primary destination in the global navigation.
- A mini-player remains from the current task while audio is active.

# Core Flows

## Generate a song

1. Open Create and enter lyrics or a prompt.
2. Define styles, optional audio reference, voice, and advanced controls.
3. Start generation, review results, then edit, publish, remix, or export.

## Explore music

1. Browse playlists, genres, moods, new songs, and recommendations.
2. Open a playlist or track.
3. Play, like, comment, share, remix, or follow the creator.

## Manage the library

1. Open liked tracks, playlists, videos, or audio messages.
2. Filter content or create and edit a playlist.
3. Open a song for publishing or detail actions.

# Interaction Patterns

- Bottom sheets group creation options without leaving the current context.
- The mini-player expands into playback without interrupting listening.
