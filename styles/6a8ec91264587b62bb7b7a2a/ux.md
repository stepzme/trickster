# Overview

Ozon Bank combines accounts, cards, cashback, transfers, payments, credit, savings, deposits, analytics, transaction history, chat, and product applications.

# Navigation

Home, Payments, Benefits, Chats, and More form the bottom bar. Home starts with a blue account carousel and quick money actions, then rewards, promotions, products, and recent operations.

# Core Flows

## Manage the main account

1. Review balance and card inside the account carousel.
2. Open the account for pay, top up, transfer, benefits, analytics, and payout services.
3. Move into details for limits, tariff, notifications, fraud protection, or salary.
4. Return to Home with the account state preserved.

## Transfer and review activity

1. Start from Transfer, Payments, a saved recipient, QR, or operation history.
2. Choose source, destination, amount, and optional message.
3. Confirm through the fast-payment receipt.
4. Repeat, template, autopay, document, or inspect the operation in History.

# Interaction Patterns

- Blue gradient account stages organize products; white modules carry operations and analytics.
- Bright blue is primary action, while pastel green and yellow communicate benefit or guidance.
- Product carousels preserve adjacent context and keep quick actions anchored below.
- Transaction rows align merchant, category, description, and signed amount for fast scanning.
