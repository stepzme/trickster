# Overview

List.am is a broad classifieds marketplace for goods, real estate, vehicles, services, jobs, business pages, messaging, paid promotion, and account management.

# Navigation

Five fixed destinations cover Home, Favorites, Post, Messages, and Profile. Home keeps menu, search, category chips, and settings in one header; category results add filters and optional map access.

# Core Flows

## Browse and contact

1. Choose a category, collection, tag, business page, or search query.
2. Narrow results with filters and compare photo-led cards.
3. Open an ad and review price, location, details, and seller information.
4. Call, message, share, or save the listing.

## Publish and promote

1. Verify the account and choose a category.
2. Add photos, description, attributes, contacts, and location.
3. Publish the ad and optionally choose a promotion package.
4. Manage active ads, wallet, messages, and profile settings.
