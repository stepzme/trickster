# Overview

Temu is a promotion-heavy marketplace for search, category browsing, product comparison, cart, checkout, order tracking, and account utilities.

# Navigation

- Five primary destinations cover Home, Categories, Profile, Cart, and a contextual fifth entry.
- Search, image search, and dense filter rails remain visible across discovery.
- Product, cart, checkout, and order tasks drill into focused screens.

# Core Flows

## Find a product

1. Search, use image search, or open Categories.
2. Apply filters, sorting, size, color, and category constraints.
3. Compare results by image, price, rating, sales, urgency, and delivery.

## Buy products

1. Review gallery, variants, size guide, reviews, seller, and details.
2. Add to Cart and adjust selection, quantity, and options.
3. Review discounts, coupons, delivery, guarantees, and total before paying.

## Manage orders

1. Open Profile and Orders.
2. Review receipt, delivery tracking, updates, and package search.
3. Contact delivery, leave a review, or manage notifications.

# Interaction Patterns

- Urgency labels, crossed prices, countdowns, and badges compress decision cues into products.
- Checkout preserves cart total and payment.
