# Overview

Telcell Wallet uses colorful 3D product scenes throughout its promotional system.

# Visual Style

Glossy cards, tickets, devices, luggage, transport, and geometric props float in soft coral, cyan, violet, and blue light. Objects feel polished and compact rather than cartoonish.

# Composition

Center one product cluster in a rounded vertical or landscape card. Use a simple gradient field and leave a clear corner for the label.

# Color and Materials

Coral and cyan anchor the family. Violet and blue may distinguish services; highlights stay soft and backgrounds remain uncluttered.

# Variants and States

Use for cards, tickets, subscriptions, partner offers, rewards, and service promotion. Keep payments, QR, banking lists, and profile data informational.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the observed imagery language with unrelated stock art or arbitrary symbols.
- Do not change the documented crop, density, or relationship to nearby text.
