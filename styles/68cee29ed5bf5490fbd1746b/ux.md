# Overview

Detsky Mir joins product discovery, family benefits, loyalty, ordering, pickup or delivery, and support in a dense marketplace flow.

# Navigation

Home, Catalog, Profile, and Cart remain fixed. Search and barcode scan bridge browsing, while product, checkout, and order detail drill down contextually.

# Core Flows

## Find and buy a product

1. Search, scan, browse categories, or open a promotion.
2. Filter and compare product cards.
3. Review product detail, sizes, rating, and availability.
4. Add to cart and choose delivery or pickup.
5. Select payment and recipient, then confirm.

## Use family benefits

1. Open the bonus card or family profile.
2. Add a child or review linked benefits.
3. Present or copy the bonus card.
4. Return to shopping with applicable pricing visible.

# Interaction Patterns

- Keep price, discount, size, and availability together.
- Preserve cart state across catalog navigation.
- Show pickup or delivery context throughout checkout.
- Confirm payment and cancellation explicitly.
- Keep mascot guidance separate from transactional copy.
