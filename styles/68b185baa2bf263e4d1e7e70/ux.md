# Overview

VK Clips is a short-video product for watching personalized feeds, reacting and sharing, discovering creators and music, recording or editing clips, and managing a creator profile.

# Navigation

- Five primary destinations cover Home, Trending, Create, Notifications, and Profile.
- Home switches Following and For You above the video content context.
- Creation moves through capture or import, editing tools, caption and privacy, then publish.

# Core Flows

## Watch and interact

1. Swipe through clips in Following or For You.
2. Like, comment, share, dislike, follow, or open the audio and creator.
3. Use overflow for reporting, link copying, or recommendation feedback.

## Create a clip

1. Record with camera effects or import media.
2. Add music, text, masks, speed, sound, and timeline edits.
3. Add caption and cover, configure comments, duets, and visibility, then publish.

## Manage a creator profile

1. Open Profile and review views, reactions, followers, and following.
2. Switch between published, drafts, and liked clips.
3. Edit interests, account, or clip settings.
