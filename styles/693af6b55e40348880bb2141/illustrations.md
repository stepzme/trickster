# Overview

Rostelecom uses expressive seasonal editorial illustrations for launch and bounded campaigns, combining saturated violet backdrops with hand-drawn objects, characters, gifts, and playful light.

# Visual Style

Use richly colored digital painting with visible brush texture, simplified silhouettes, warm orange details, and whimsical scale. The tone is festive and human, not glossy corporate 3D.

# Composition

Build one tall central scene with a clear focal object and generous top or bottom space for branding. Campaign art may fill a rounded story tile, but controls and tariff data remain outside the artwork.

# Color and Materials

Start from saturated violet and deep navy, then add orange, yellow, green, and cyan as small luminous accents. Preserve strong contrast and avoid pastel washing.

# Variants and States

Use this language for launch, seasonal campaigns, gifts, and celebratory success states. For new illustrations, inherit the purple-orange UI palette and rounded story geometry; keep ordinary service icons simple.

# Production Requirements

Use only the formats and crops supported by the implemented placement. Where the documented imagery is compositionally important, temporary placeholders must preserve its placement, crop, scale, negative space, and approximate visual weight until final art exists. No unobserved light/dark variants or export ratios are prescribed.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the documented image language with unrelated stock art, arbitrary symbols, or a denser composition.

# Known Gaps

- Export aspect ratios, safe crop measurements, animation, and unobserved state variants were not documented.
