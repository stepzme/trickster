# Overview

Wildberries is a dense marketplace covering personalized discovery, search, product comparison, cart, checkout, pickup or delivery, payment, tracking, cancellation, and rating.

# Navigation

- A five-item primary navigation covers Home/Search, Catalog, Wallet or services, Cart, and Profile.
- Home and search use product collections with persistent search and camera access.
- Product, cart, checkout, and order pages progressively replace discovery density with focused action.

# Core Flows

## Find and choose a product

1. Search by text, image, catalog, or promotional collection.
2. Apply filters and compare price, discount, rating, delivery date, and seller.
3. Open the product to inspect gallery, variants, price history, return terms, description, reviews, questions, and manufacturer.

## Buy an item

1. Add the selected offer to Cart or use Buy now.
2. Confirm quantity, address or pickup point, delivery time, payment, and subscription options.
3. Submit the order and follow processing or delivery status.

## Manage an order

1. Open the active order and view route, pickup code, or courier status.
2. Cancel or request a courier when available.
3. Rate the product and pickup point after completion.

# Interaction Patterns

- Recommendations continue through product, cart, processing, and tracking screens.
