# Overview

Megamarket is a broad marketplace spanning promotional discovery, catalog, search, favorites, product comparison, cart, checkout, delivery, and account management.

# Navigation

A primary navigation connects Home, search/catalog, favorites, profile, and cart. Address, bonuses, and search remain close of Home.

# Core Flows

## Find and choose a product

1. Enter through campaign stories, categories, search, or catalog.
2. Filter or compare result cards.
3. Review media, seller, rating, variants, price, delivery, and bonuses.
4. Save, compare, or add to cart.

## Complete an order

1. Review items, seller groups, bonuses, promo codes, and total.
2. Choose address and delivery interval.
3. Select payment and recipient details.
4. Submit and track the order from Profile.
