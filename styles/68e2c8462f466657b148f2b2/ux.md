# Overview

vc.ru is an editorial and social platform for reading feeds, following communities and authors, publishing posts, commenting, messaging, searching, and managing a professional profile.

# Navigation

- Five primary destinations cover Feed, Search, Chats, Notifications, and Profile.
- Feed tabs switch Popular, Fresh, and personalized content.
- Community and author pages open from post headers while the compose button starts publishing.

# Core Flows

## Read and shape the feed

1. Choose a feed tab and scan headlines, media, authors, and engagement.
2. Open a post or community for deeper reading.
3. React, comment, save, follow, hide, report, or tune recommendations.

## Find people and communities

1. Open Search and enter a topic or account.
2. Review communities, personal blogs, and posts.
3. Follow a result or open its content catalog.

## Create a community

1. Start New community and add image, name, and description.
2. Review rules and publish the community.
3. Add the first article and manage followers or notifications.

# Interaction Patterns

- Post overflow controls centralize feed tuning and reporting.
