# Overview

List.am uses compact 3D object scenes and simple line illustrations for marketplace promotions, category education, and account verification.

# Visual Style

Promotional objects are rounded and toy-like: a pomegranate, compact electric car, rocket, and verification badge. Functional onboarding art uses thin black outlines with flat blue fills.

# Composition

Place one centered object inside a pastel rounded card with a short label. Verification art uses one large line scene centered above the title and action.

# Color and Materials

Use bright blue as the anchor, supported by peach, yellow, mint, red, and soft lilac backgrounds. Keep outlines dark and shadows light.

# Variants and States

Use illustration for collections, marketplace education, verification, and promotion—not for listings. New art should retain the UI's blue interaction color, white space, and rounded cards.

# Production Requirements

Preserve the documented placement, crop, negative space, and visual weight in production exports. Before final art exists, placeholders must preserve those same compositional properties.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the observed imagery language with unrelated stock art or arbitrary symbols.
- Do not change the documented crop, density, or relationship to nearby text.
