# Overview

Weather presents current conditions, hourly and ten-day forecasts, detailed metrics, animated maps, saved cities, alerts, and widgets.

# Navigation

- A city detail scroll combines current conditions, hourly forecast, ten-day forecast, and metric items.
- Bottom actions open the weather map or saved-city list without adding a persistent primary navigation.
- The map exposes layer, location, city-list, and timeline controls directly over the visualization.

# Core Flows

## Check a city's forecast

1. Open a saved city or use current location.
2. Read temperature, condition, high and low, hourly trend, and ten-day range.
3. Scroll into precipitation, wind, UV, humidity, visibility, pressure, and sunrise detail.

## Explore weather on the map

1. Open the map from the city forecast.
2. Choose precipitation, temperature, wind, or another layer.
3. Scrub or play the forecast timeline and return to city detail.

## Manage locations and alerts

1. Open the city list and search for a city or airport.
2. Add, reorder, or remove saved locations.
3. Configure severe-weather notifications or add a widget.
