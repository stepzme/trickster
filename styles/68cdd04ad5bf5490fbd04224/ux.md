# Overview

VK Music combines personalized discovery, adaptive mixes, podcasts, search, listening history, playlists, downloads, and subscription management in an audio player.

# Navigation

- Five primary destinations cover Main, Mix, Podcasts, Search, and My Music.
- A persistent mini-player sits above navigation and opens the full player.
- Main uses shelves for mixes, recent listening, releases, and social recommendations.

# Core Flows

## Discover and play

1. Open Main or Mix and choose a recommendation shelf.
2. Start a mix, album, playlist, track, or podcast.
3. Control playback from the mini-player or open the full player for queue and lyrics.

## Find and save audio

1. Search for a track, artist, album, or podcast.
2. Open the result and play or add it to My Music.
3. Organize it into a playlist or download it when the plan allows.

## Manage a subscription

1. Open a gated feature such as downloads or ad-free listening.
2. Review benefits and plan pricing.
3. Subscribe, restore access, or return to free listening.

# Interaction Patterns

- The mini-player preserves context across every destination.
- Social playlists expose taste match before play.
