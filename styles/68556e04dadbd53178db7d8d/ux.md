# Overview

VkusVill combines loyalty, grocery delivery, store browsing, personalized discounts, catalog search, favorites, cart, checkout, order tracking, and support.

# Navigation

- Five primary destinations cover My item, Catalog, Stores, Profile, and Support.
- Catalog search, filters, favorites, fulfillment, and location remain available.
- Cart opens as a sheet and progresses into delivery, recipient, payment, and confirmation.

# Core Flows

## Build a grocery order

1. Confirm address, delivery window, or nearby store.
2. Search, browse categories, recommendations, or favorites.
3. Add products, adjust quantities in Cart, then complete checkout.

## Use loyalty benefits

1. Open My Card and review favorite product, nutrition, discounts, and QR access.
2. Choose or update a personalized benefit.
3. Scan the loyalty code in store or apply benefits online.

## Track and resolve an order

1. Open the active order after checkout.
2. Follow preparation and delivery stages.
3. Contact support, edit allowed details, or rate the result.
