# Overview

Lemana PRO is a home-improvement marketplace that combines loyalty, promotions, project inspiration, a deep product catalog, store availability, cart, delivery, and pickup.

# Navigation

A floating bottom dock keeps Home, Cart, Search, and Scanner close. Home adds loyalty, promo, purchase, and list shortcuts; catalog and product pages use focused headers with search and share.

# Core Flows

## Discover and select

1. Enter through phone and loyalty onboarding.
2. Browse promotions, ready-made room solutions, recommendations, or the catalog.
3. Filter products and inspect price, availability, specifications, reviews, and related goods.
4. Add the selected quantity to the cart.

## Checkout

1. Review quantities, weight, legal-entity mode, and recommendations.
2. Choose delivery or click-and-collect, address, and time.
3. Enter contact details and confirm the order.
4. Return to purchase history or order details for support.

# Interaction Patterns

- Yellow marks loyalty, cart, and commitment while charcoal carries filters and secondary controls.
- Search, cart, and scanner remain accessible through the floating dock.
- Product cards expose fulfillment availability before checkout.
- Long project and product pages use stacked sections with a persistent cart action.
