# Overview

Rocketbank uses expressive character and lifestyle imagery as part of the product personality. Visuals include the Rocky mascot, surreal avatars, cinematic venue photography, and playful generated scenes integrated directly into navigation and customization.

# Visual Style

- Bold, slightly uncanny characters with exaggerated expressions or styling.
- Soft-focus photography and cinematic color grading for avatars and venues.
- Occasional 3D or generated objects against dark, high-contrast environments.
- Graphic treatment stays playful and fashion-forward rather than corporate.

# Composition

- Character crops fill wide pills, cards, or edge-to-edge rounded portrait panels.
- Keep the subject large and singular, with minimal background detail near controls.
- Lifestyle cards use one strong image with overlaid white display text.
- Images may bleed under black floating controls, but essential faces and objects stay unobstructed.

# Color and Materials

Favor electric cyan, pink, orange, acid yellow, and deep black inside imagery. Place these against the UI's pale blue-to-pink wash so the media feels vivid without making the surrounding chrome noisy.

# Variants and States

Use character and lifestyle visuals for onboarding, assistant identity, venue discovery, referrals, and personalization. Keep balances, transfer forms, settings, and confirmations mostly typographic.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the observed imagery language with unrelated stock art or arbitrary symbols.
- Do not change the documented crop, density, or relationship to nearby text.
