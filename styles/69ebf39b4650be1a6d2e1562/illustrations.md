# Overview

2GIS uses vivid 3D brand scenes for onboarding and small playful objects in recommendations. Illustration explains capabilities without entering the operational map itself.

# Visual Style

Use glossy simplified 3D objects, bold geometric forms, and thick spatial ribbons. Materials are clean and synthetic, with strong colored light on a dark blue-black field.

# Composition

Center one object or short scene in open space. Let a single curved path connect objects when motion or routing is the idea. Small recommendation artwork may sit inside a rounded card with the map outside it.

# Color and Materials

Lead with saturated green and electric blue, supported by red, yellow, and occasional rainbow transitions. Keep backgrounds dark for onboarding and light for compact content cards.

# Variants and States

Use 3D scenes for onboarding, feature education, and playful scanning states. Keep navigation, map markers, place cards, and transport controls iconographic rather than illustrative.

# Production Requirements

Use only the formats and crops supported by the implemented placement. Where the documented imagery is compositionally important, temporary placeholders must preserve its placement, crop, scale, negative space, and approximate visual weight until final art exists. No unobserved light/dark variants or export ratios are prescribed.

# Avoid

- Do not replace the documented image language with unrelated stock art, arbitrary symbols, or a denser composition.
