# Overview

Home Credit Bank supports accounts and cards, transfers, payments, conversion, product opening, card controls, analytics, transaction history, offers, autopayments, and support.

# Navigation

Five primary destinations anchor Home, All accounts, Transfers, Payments, and For me. Home exposes product tabs, quick actions, finance summary, and instant transfer entry.

# Core Flows

## Transfer or pay

1. Choose transfer or payment type, select source, identify recipient or service, enter amount and details, review fee and rate, confirm, then view or share the receipt.

## Manage products and finances

1. Open a card or account, inspect balance and history, manage limits or settings, analyze income and spending, or compare and open a new product.

# Interaction Patterns

Amounts and sources stay visible through commitment, segmented controls switch method or period, sheets isolate focused decisions, and transaction results preserve receipt and favorite actions.
