# Overview

Tiimo uses a stable authored image language across onboarding, tasks, focus, statistics, and supportive guidance. It combines loose black line illustration, soft lavender fields, pastel object icons, circular progress motifs, and a recurring rounded purple mascot. Imagery makes planning approachable while leaving task structure legible.

# Visual Style

Generate illustrations with an image-generation model. Use relaxed black linework with slightly irregular human character, flat or softly shaded lavender shapes, sparse pastel accents, and rounded friendly silhouettes. Small task images may be simplified object or routine icons; larger scenes may show hands, devices, planning objects, brains, mood, or streak metaphors. Keep the purple mascot's minimal rounded face recognizably consistent.

Every generated image must be visually reviewed and explicitly approved before integration. Integrate the approved output as a raster asset. Do not redraw or substitute artwork with SwiftUI shapes, `Canvas`, programmatic vectors, SF Symbols, emoji, or code-generated approximations.

# Composition

Large illustrations sit inside generous rounded cards or onboarding fields and occupy roughly a quarter to half of their container while leaving clean space for a short heading or control. Keep one focal object, broad breathing room, and intentional cropping. Small task images remain compact, padded, and aligned with the label; the mascot is a small companion control unless the sampled composition calls for a hero.

# Color and Materials

Anchor images in warm white, near black, lavender, and brand violet, with controlled pale yellow, blush, aqua, mint, or green accents. Outlines are dark but not heavy. Shading is soft and limited; avoid glossy realism except for the observed photographed-device variant. Images feel printed, drawn, or softly layered rather than metallic or photorealistic.

# Variants and States

Onboarding can use the largest scene or a photographed-device composition with violet overlay. Planning and tasks use compact authored objects. Focus uses circular rings and quiet central imagery. Statistics, streak, mood, and progress use larger line-art cards or mascot variants while preserving line quality and palette. Selected, completed, or celebratory variants may add one semantic green or brighter pastel accent without changing medium.

# Avoid

- Do not generate unrelated stock-photo scenes, glossy 3D mascots, corporate vector people, or dense multicolor collages.
- Do not substitute approved imagery with SwiftUI shapes, `Canvas`, programmatic paths, SF Symbols, emoji, or icon-font glyphs.
- Do not integrate model output before visual approval.
- Do not crop away the focal object or remove negative space reserved for nearby text.
- Do not mix inconsistent line weights, mascot anatomy, lighting models, or palette families.
- Do not enlarge task icons until they compete with the task name, duration, or completion state.
