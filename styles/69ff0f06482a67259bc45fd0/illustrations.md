# Overview

Ivi uses a narrow authored glossy 3D/CG system for onboarding, subscription success, and account subscription promotion. It is separate from cinematic posters, thumbnails, photos, video key art, the Ivi mark, functional icons, charts, and campaign media.

# Visual Style

Create soft rounded block-like objects, simplified hands, cards, gifts, or subscription bundles with inflated edges and sparse white sketch or orbit accents. Use smooth plastic- or glass-like surfaces, soft gradients, glossy highlights, magenta bloom, and a dark vignette field. Forms are volume-driven rather than outlined; line appears only as a light gestural accent.

Every new illustration must be generated with an image-generation model in this language. It requires separate visual approval before interface integration and must then be integrated as the approved raster asset.

# Composition

Center one compact object or related object cluster above the message with generous near-black or deep-plum negative space. Keep the object mostly contained and the silhouette easy to read, occasionally allowing a large central crop. Subscription-card variants may use a smaller gift render, but it should remain a clear focal object rather than a generic icon. Never mix illustration into poster grids or player imagery.

# Color and Materials

Hot pink and magenta dominate, supported by deep purple-black, white highlights, occasional warm skin tones, and limited gift-box accent colors. Use glossy rounded materials, soft studio shadows, pink glow, and controlled bloom. Avoid noisy texture, realistic environments, hard metallic contrast, or unrelated rainbow lighting.

# Variants and States

- Onboarding may show a simplified hand interacting with a preference card or another centered branded object.
- Subscription success uses a larger gift or bundle composition above concise confirmation.
- Account subscription promotion uses a smaller contained gift render within a dark card.
- No stable authored empty, loading, permission, or error family was observed.

# Avoid

- Do not draw or approximate the art with SwiftUI shapes, Canvas, programmatic paths or gradients, SF Symbols, emoji, text glyphs, or assembled media icons.
- Do not integrate an unapproved generation, placeholder, screenshot crop, or temporary vector; use the visually approved raster asset.
- Do not substitute film posters, thumbnails, key art, logos, campaign media, or generic gift icons.
- Do not use stock 3D emoji, realistic hands, clay characters, dense scenes, or bright white backgrounds.
- Do not spread promotional illustration across the catalog, detail pages, or player.
