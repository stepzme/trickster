# Overview

Yandex Practicum is a course companion that keeps active learning, catalog discovery, support, and account management in a quiet four-tab structure.

# Navigation

- The primary navigation separates Learning, Catalog, Support, and Account.
- The Learning tab centers the active course and its next lesson.
- Course pages open a focused curriculum overlay with topic and lesson levels.

# Core Flows

## Continue a course

1. Open the active course from Learning.
2. Review progress, program, notes, or course information.
3. Enter a topic, choose a lesson, and continue through its content.

## Choose a course

1. Browse Catalog by discipline and filters.
2. Compare course cards with duration, employment support, and price.
3. Open the chosen course or return to the current learning plan.

# Interaction Patterns

- The home screen gives one course and one Continue action clear priority.
- Curriculum uses a drill-down list with checks for completed lessons.
- Search and filter controls float lightly over long catalog lists.
- Support is a simple conversation inbox; account settings use familiar rows.
