# Overview

ForteApp joins banking products, cards, transfers, payments, forex, services, documents, history, and support.

# Navigation

Chats, History, Home, Transfers, and Payments remain stable; product tabs organize cards, loans, deposits, and accounts.

# Core Flows

## Transfer money

1. Choose transfer type.
2. Select source and destination.
3. Enter amount and review recipient, fees, and limits.
4. Confirm with authentication.
5. View receipt, favorite, or repeat.

# Interaction Patterns

- Keep source and destination explicit.
- Review fees and limits before confirmation.
- Pair status color with text.
- Preserve history and receipt access.
