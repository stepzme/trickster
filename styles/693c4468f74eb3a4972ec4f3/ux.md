# Overview

AliExpress is a promotion-heavy marketplace built around search, themed feeds, product detail, cart, checkout, favorites, and loyalty. It prioritizes price, discount, delivery, rating, and purchase volume in every product context.

# Navigation

Home, Combo, Below Market, Cart, and Profile form the bottom bar. Search with image lookup remains prominent. Campaign destinations can temporarily simplify the bar while keeping search and cart available.

# Core Flows

## Discovery and search

1. Set delivery country and privacy preference.
2. Browse campaign rails, price programs, and recommendations.
3. Search by text or image, then refine with filter, delivery, rating, and sort controls.

## Product and favorites

Select variants, review rating, orders, description, delivery, seller, and recommendations. Add to cart, buy now, or save to a named list without losing the selected variant.

## Cart and checkout

1. Select items by seller and adjust quantity.
2. Review delivery and total before checkout.
3. Enter recipient, address, payment, and delivery choice.
4. Pay through a persistent total action, then track the order.

## Campaign and loyalty

Combo and Below Market explain their rules before purchase. Profile groups orders, favorites, installments, stores, reviews, points, coupons, settings, and daily rewards.

# Interaction Patterns

- Keep price, discount, rating, orders, and delivery together.
- Preserve seller grouping and item selection in cart.
- Explain campaign constraints before checkout.
- Use distinct primary actions for add-to-cart and immediate purchase.
- Keep totals fixed near the final action.
