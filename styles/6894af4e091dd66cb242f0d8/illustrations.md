# Overview

4 lapy uses a repeatable image language built from real pet cutouts, bold black animal silhouettes, and flat saturated geometric backplates. It carries onboarding, promotions, categories, empty results, favorites, payment, and order-confirmation moments while clean product photography remains a separate commerce layer.

# Visual Style

The central subject is usually a photographic dog or cat isolated from its original background, with a clean but slightly playful contour. Subjects sit against simple blobs, circles, hearts, stripes, or split-color fields in orange, pink, violet, cyan, mint, or yellow. Black silhouette animals and compact black speech bubbles add a graphic counterweight. Small sticker-like marks may overlap the subject or backplate, but the rendering remains flat and crisp rather than painterly.

Generate required illustrations with the available image-generation model. Do not redraw pets, mascots, blobs, stickers, or promotional scenes from SwiftUI shapes or substitute them with SF Symbols. Present generated results for explicit approval before integrating them into the app.

# Composition

On full-screen brand moments, one pet or silhouette can dominate the middle of the viewport against a large split or solid color field, leaving controlled negative space for a short title and action. In promotional cards, the subject typically occupies about one third to one half of the card, often cropped at an edge and overlapping its backplate. Empty and confirmation states center a pet-led composition above or beside the supporting text, with enough scale to make the image the primary visual cue rather than an icon.

Category artwork is more compact: a single black animal silhouette sits inside a saturated disk or similarly simple field. Product packs use a separate contained-photo treatment and must not be collaged into the authored pet artwork.

# Color and Materials

Use the pale canvas and white card surfaces from `ui.md` as quiet framing. Illustration fields use saturated orange, soft pink, violet, cyan, mint, and yellow with near-black silhouettes, speech bubbles, and type accents. Keep color areas mostly flat with crisp boundaries. Photography should retain natural fur texture and believable lighting; backplates stay deliberately graphic and low-detail. Avoid glossy 3D materials and unrelated pastel gradients.

# Variants and States

Onboarding and promotional variants may use the largest, most layered cutouts and multiple geometric elements. Empty search and favorites states use a simpler centered pet plus one strong shape. Confirmation, paid-order, and bonus states may add celebratory disks or sticker marks without changing the cutout medium. Category variants reduce the language to one black silhouette and one saturated field. Real products, saved-pet avatars, clinics, and service providers remain photographic rather than illustrated.

# Avoid

- Do not replace generated authored imagery with SwiftUI shapes, emoji, SF Symbols, or stock icon packs.
- Do not substitute generic vector animals, children's-book drawings, glossy 3D mascots, or unrelated lifestyle stock photography.
- Do not put product-pack photography inside the promotional cutout collage language.
- Do not shrink empty-state or promotional art to a decorative icon when it is the primary visual mass.
- Do not use busy scenic backgrounds, detailed environments, or multiple competing focal subjects.
- Do not crop away faces, ears, or the expressive pose of the animal subject.
