# Overview

Sutochno.ru supports accommodation search, comparison, booking, host chat, reservation management, favorites, and cashback.

# Navigation

- Five bottom destinations cover Search, Favorites, Reservations, Messages, and Profile.
- Search begins with destination, dates, and guests, then continues to list or map results.
- Property and checkout screens use back navigation and a sticky booking action.

# Core Flows

## Find accommodation

1. Enter destination, dates, and guests.
2. Apply filters or switch to map search.
3. Compare image-led result cards by price, rating, location, and attributes.

## Book a stay

1. Review gallery, description, amenities, rules, reviews, and availability.
2. Choose terms, enter guest details, and apply bonuses or a promo code.
3. Review total, prepayment, payment on arrival, and cancellation before confirming.

## Manage a reservation

1. Open Reservations and select an active booking.
2. Review details, add to calendar, contact the property, or ask support.
3. Cancel through the explicit reservation action when available.

# Interaction Patterns

- Black sticky actions contrast with the pink brand accent.
- Photography carries discovery; cards keep rating, price, and location close to the image.
- Horizontal carousels support recent searches, destinations, and reviews.
- Checkout exposes cost and conditions before commitment.
