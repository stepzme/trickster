# Overview

Mail.ru uses friendly 3D objects and a soft white-blue mail mascot to make empty productivity states feel approachable.

# Visual Style

Objects are simplified, softly shaded, and slightly surreal: paper maps, small planets, pins, and folded characters combine with sparse black line annotations.

# Composition

Center one object above concise guidance with generous white space. Keep the art compact enough that the primary creation action remains visible.

# Color

Use white, pale blue, soft gray, and restrained product accents. Black hand-drawn marks add direction without overpowering the object.

# Usage

Use illustrations for empty tasks, missing labels, onboarding, and lightweight education. Keep mail lists, files, and note cards content-led; new art must harmonize with the UI's white canvas and pastel product colors.
