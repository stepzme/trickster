# Overview

Otello uses a recurring authored spot-illustration system for launch, loading, empty, utility, and loyalty-like moments. It consists of isolated glossy 3D objects such as the green key, red heart, green suitcase, and soft abstract forms. The art supports state recognition but remains secondary to real accommodation photography. When a screen calls for this art, generate it with the available image-generation model, obtain visual approval, and integrate the approved raster asset. Do not recreate it from SwiftUI shapes, SF Symbols, emoji, or programmatic gradients. The state is not visually complete until the generated asset is visible in the running app.

# Visual Style

Use compact toy-like 3D objects with smooth plastic or rubber materials, rounded proportions, gentle studio lighting, soft contact shadows, and one clearly readable silhouette. Objects can carry a minimal expressive gesture but should not become detailed narrative characters. Surfaces are polished without chrome, excessive reflections, or photoreal texture. The mood is friendly, direct, and functional.

# Composition

Place one dominant object in the center of a white, off-white, deep-teal, or neon-green field with generous negative space. Loading and empty-state objects remain modest in scale and sit above or near concise native text. A promotional or loyalty composition may pair two or three related forms, but it must not cover accommodation imagery or compete with the primary action. Keep text and controls outside the generated asset.

# Color and Materials

Use neon green as the main material, balanced by white, deep teal, and charcoal. Red can appear on heart or alert-adjacent objects; small coral, blue, or pale neutral details may support the focal form. Lighting should create soft gradients through volume, not through a flat programmatic background. Preserve high silhouette contrast and restrained shadows. Avoid rainbow palettes, metallic luxury materials, busy texture, glassmorphism, or neon glow.

# Variants and States

- **Launch or brand loading:** isolated green key or related mark centered on a full deep-teal, neon-green, or clean light field.
- **Search or processing:** small centered key-like object with minimal surrounding UI and ample whitespace.
- **Empty or saved state:** one friendly suitcase, heart, or utility object above concise native state text and action.
- **Loyalty or benefit:** a small cluster of abstract green-led forms, kept separate from property inventory and photography.

Do not invent narrative scenes, human characters, or new error/permission variants unless the generated proposal is explicitly approved.

# Avoid

- SwiftUI shapes, Canvas drawings, programmatic gradients, SF Symbols, emoji, or stock 3D icons standing in for generated art.
- Flat vector line art, hand-drawn scenes, photoreal objects, glossy chrome, or detailed human mascots.
- Dense multi-object scenes, scenic backgrounds, or illustrations layered over hotel photography.
- Text, logos, controls, prices, or status labels baked into the generated asset.
- Crops that remove the object silhouette or art so small that it reads as an incidental icon.
- Integrating the first generated result without visual review and explicit approval.
