# Overview

Airba fresh uses a cheerful avocado mascot and grocery-themed 3D icons to soften dense commerce screens and explain empty or promotional states.

# Visual Style

Render the avocado as a rounded cartoon character with large eyes, small limbs, soft shading, and expressive props. Supporting icons use glossy, colorful grocery and reward objects.

# Composition

Center the mascot in generous white or pale-green space with one short explanation below. Promotional banners can combine the character, oversized reward objects, and product color fields.

# Color

Anchor the system in fresh green, then use yellow, orange, blue, and pink for reward and category accents. Keep the mascot's brown pit and green body consistent.

# Usage

Use the mascot for onboarding, bonuses, empty states, profile prompts, and promotional education. Use product photography for shopping decisions and simple line icons for navigation.
