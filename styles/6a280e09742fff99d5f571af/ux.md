# Overview

ASOS moves from inspiration to purchase through Home, category discovery, search, saved items, bag, and account. Product imagery stays primary while delivery, returns, size, and price remain close to the decision.

# Navigation

Home, Search, Bag, Saved, and Account persist in the floating bottom bar. Search and notifications remain reachable from discovery; back navigation preserves catalog position.

# Core Flows

## Browse and search

Campaign modules and personalized rails lead into category grids. Sort and Filter remain visible at the top of results, with selected facets reversible from dedicated lists.

## Product decision

1. Open a product from a grid or recommendation.
2. Inspect media, colors, price, ratings, fit, delivery, and returns.
3. Select size, save, share, or add to bag.

## Saved items and bag

Saved products can be grouped into boards and moved to bag. Bag exposes variant, size, quantity, total, delivery threshold, checkout, and Apple Pay without hiding item edits.

## Checkout and account

Checkout separates address, billing, payment, and order review. Account keeps orders, returns, support, personal details, address book, preferences, and gift vouchers in predictable lists.

# Interaction Patterns

- Preserve scroll position between product and catalog.
- Keep size selection in the purchase path.
- Show reduced and former prices together.
- Keep Save and Add to Bag reachable on long pages.
- Explain delivery and return constraints before payment.
