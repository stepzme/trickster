# Overview

Radio Arzamas is an editorial audio library organized around courses, podcasts, lecturers, saved material, downloads, and listening history. Content discovery is visual, while playback and subscription state remain available without leaving the current context.

# Navigation

- A five-item bottom bar leads to Home, Catalog, Saved, Downloads, and History.
- Profile opens from a circular control in the top-right corner.
- Catalog switches between Albums, Tracks, and Lecturers, with topic chips and sorting below.
- A persistent mini-player appears above the bottom bar during playback.

# Core Flows

## Discover and listen

1. Browse the editorial hero and horizontally scrolling content shelves.
2. Open a course or podcast to review its author, description, and episode list.
3. Start the full work or an individual episode.
4. Continue through the mini-player or open the full player for queue, timer, and playback settings.

## Search and filter

1. Open Catalog and enter a query.
2. Narrow results by content type, topic chips, lecturer, or ordering.
3. Open a result, save it, download it, or begin listening.

## Save and subscribe

1. Bookmark a course or episode from a shelf or detail page.
2. Manage saved and downloaded content from their dedicated tabs.
3. When premium material is opened, compare monthly and annual plans in the subscription sheet.
4. Complete or restore a subscription from Profile.

# Interaction Patterns

- Horizontal shelves keep the dark editorial feed dense without turning it into a list.
- Bookmark, download, share, and overflow actions repeat on cards and detail pages.
- Outlined topic chips act as filters; the active navigation item and primary actions use yellow.
- Playback is continuous across screens through a bottom-docked mini-player.
- Subscription offers use a bottom sheet or embedded profile card rather than a separate visual system.
