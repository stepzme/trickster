# Overview

Choco routes users among groceries, restaurants, takeaway, entertainment, coupons, QR payment, and lifestyle services while retaining shared account and order primitives.

# Navigation

Home selects the vertical. Each vertical uses a focused catalog and its own relevant primary navigation; orders, messages, and profile reconnect the system.

# Core Flows

## Grocery or restaurant order

1. Select vertical and address.
2. Search or browse categories and offers.
3. Open product, dish, restaurant, or collection.
4. Add and adjust basket.
5. Review fees, promo, bonuses, payment, and total.
6. Confirm, track stages, contact support, and rate.

## Entertainment and coupons

1. Browse category, map, listing detail, reviews, availability, and certificate terms before purchase.

# Interaction Patterns

- Never mix baskets across verticals.
- Keep the active address visible.
- Show service and delivery fees separately.
- Preserve tracking after payment handoff.
