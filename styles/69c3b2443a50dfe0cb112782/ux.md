# Overview

Kaspi combines banking, payments, transfers, marketplace, travel, government services, and messaging in a single high-density service hub.

# Navigation

four persistent destinations cover Home, Kaspi QR, Messages, and Services. Home exposes the most-used services immediately; deeper banking tasks use conventional back navigation and segmented subviews.

# Core Flows

## Authorize

1. Enter a phone number.
2. Confirm the SMS code.
3. Restore access on a new device when required.
4. Enter the same service hub as an authenticated user.

## Manage money

1. Open My Bank from the home shortcut collections.
2. Choose an account, card, deposit, or loan.
3. Review balances and progress, then open the needed action.

## Transfer to a client

1. Open Transfers and choose recipient type.
2. Identify the Kaspi client by phone, card, or QR.
3. Enter amount and optional message.
4. Confirm and receive a clear success receipt.

# Interaction Patterns

- Success states provide a receipt path and a direct return route.
