# Overview

Ozon Travel uses bright campaign graphics that combine destination photography with oversized dimensional ribbons, rings, tags, and playful travel symbols.

# Visual Style

Graphics are glossy, saturated, and energetic, with Ozon blue, magenta, violet, cyan, and occasional neon green layered as bold 3D forms.

# Composition

Use a single dominant dimensional motif around a concise offer, supported by a destination photo or flat gradient; keep critical booking controls outside the artwork.

# Color

Electric blue anchors the composition, with magenta and violet gradients, white type, and small high-contrast promotional accents.

# Usage

Apply this language to seasonal offers, loyalty prompts, and destination campaigns. Functional search, passenger, payment, and order surfaces remain clean and restrained.
