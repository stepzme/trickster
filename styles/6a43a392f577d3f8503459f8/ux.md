# Overview

Yandex Pay is a bright financial hub for cards, Split, rewards, payments, shopping offers, and transaction history, unified by soft iridescent surfaces and a persistent Pay action.

# Navigation

- A four-item primary navigation separates Home, Stores, Payments, and History.
- The Pay action opens the camera and payment scanner.
- Profile and Plus balance remain accessible from the top of primary screens.

# Core Flows

## Pay by QR

1. Open Pay from the persistent action.
2. Scan a code and review merchant, amount, and funding source.
3. Confirm and return to the merchant or payment hub.

## Explore and manage money

1. Review cards, Split, rewards, credit, and monthly prompts on Home.
2. Use Payments for transfers, bills, mobile, internet, tickets, and services.
3. Inspect History with account and date filters.

# Interaction Patterns

- Confirmation screens reduce the interface to merchant, amount, source, and one decisive action.
